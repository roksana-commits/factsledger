# Tura Law College

## Overview

Tura Law College is a legal education institution located in Tura, West Garo Hills, Meghalaya [6]. Established in 1975, the college provides professional legal training and is a recognized entity within the higher education landscape of the state [4] [5]. It operates as a private, self-financed institution under the regulatory framework of the Meghalaya government's education department [5]. The college serves as a significant academic hub for the Garo Hills region, maintaining active engagement with the local community and university systems [1] [8].

## Recognition and accreditation

The college is formally recognized by the Bar Council of India (BCI) for its legal education programs [4]. It has maintained its status as a Center of Legal Education (CLE) since its founding in 1975 [4]. While official regulatory lists have historically noted approval periods through 2021-22, the institution continues to function with the administrative and academic support of its affiliating university, indicating ongoing operational status [1] [2].

## Leadership and governance

Tura Law College is governed by a body that includes representatives nominated by North-Eastern Hill University (NEHU) [3]. This governance structure ensures alignment with the academic standards and administrative requirements of the central university system [3]. The institution is categorized as a private, self-financed college by the Directorate of Higher and Technical Education, Government of Meghalaya [5].

## Academic programmes

The primary academic offering at Tura Law College is the three-year Bachelor of Laws (LL.B.) degree [2] [4]. The program is affiliated with North-Eastern Hill University (NEHU), which manages the examination processes and confers the degrees [1] [2]. Recent academic records indicate that the college continues to conduct examinations and process results for its student body, with result notifications published as recently as May 2026 [2].

## Community activity

The college campus is frequently utilized for regional academic and civic activities. In August 2026, the campus served as a venue for a sit-in demonstration by ad-hoc teachers in Meghalaya, highlighting the institution's role as a central point for local professional and community engagement [8]. Additionally, the college environment has been subject to local security monitoring, as evidenced by police investigations into incidents occurring near the campus in early 2026 [7].
