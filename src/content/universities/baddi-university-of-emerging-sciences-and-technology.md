# Baddi University of Emerging Sciences and Technology

## Overview
Baddi University of Emerging Sciences and Technology (BUEST) is a private higher education institution located in Baddi, Makhnumajra, within the Solan District of Himachal Pradesh, India [[1]](https://prsindia.org/files/bills_acts/acts_states/himachal-pradesh/2009/2009HP21.pdf) [[4]](https://www.baddiuniv.ac.in/). The university's origins trace back to the establishment of the Centre for Advanced Studies in Engineering (CASE) Society in 2000, which subsequently founded the Institute of Engineering and Emerging Technology (IEET) in 2002 [[4]](https://www.baddiuniv.ac.in/). The institution was formally granted university status in 2009 under state legislation [[1]](https://prsindia.org/files/bills_acts/acts_states/himachal-pradesh/2009/2009HP21.pdf).

## Recognition and accreditation
The university was established under the **Baddi University of Emerging Sciences and Technology (Establishment and Regulation) Act, 2009 (Act No. 21 of 2009)**, enacted by the Government of Himachal Pradesh [[1]](https://prsindia.org/files/bills_acts/acts_states/himachal-pradesh/2009/2009HP21.pdf). It is recognized by the **University Grants Commission (UGC)** under Section 2(f) and Section 22 of the UGC Act, 1956, authorizing it to award degrees [[2]](https://www.baddiuniv.ac.in/about/ugc-approval/).

BUEST has received an **A+ Grade** accreditation from the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) [[5]](https://www.careers360.com/university/baddi-university-of-emerging-sciences-and-technology-baddi). Additionally, its various professional programs are approved by the following statutory bodies:
*   **All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE)** for engineering and management programs [[4]](https://www.baddiuniv.ac.in/).
*   **Pharmacy Council of India (PCI)** for pharmacy education [[4]](https://www.baddiuniv.ac.in/).
*   **Indian Nursing Council (INC)** for nursing programs [[4]](https://www.baddiuniv.ac.in/).
*   **Himachal Pradesh Private Educational Institutions Regulatory Commission (HPPERC)** [[3]](https://hpperc.hp.gov.in/index/privateUniversities).

## Leadership and governance
The university is sponsored by the **Centre for Advanced Studies in Engineering (CASE) Society**, which was established on May 2, 2000 [[4]](https://www.baddiuniv.ac.in/). The governance structure is led by:
*   **Chancellor:** Shri Pradeep Srivastava [[4]](https://www.baddiuniv.ac.in/).
*   **Vice-Chancellor:** Prof. Dr. T. R. Nandal [[4]](https://www.baddiuniv.ac.in/).
*   **Chairman (CASE):** Shri Ram Prasad Agarwal [[4]](https://www.baddiuniv.ac.in/).
*   **Registrar:** Dr. Khushmeet Kumar [[4]](https://www.baddiuniv.ac.in/).

## Academic programmes
BUEST organizes its academic offerings through several specialized schools and colleges, catering to diverse technical and professional fields [[4]](https://www.baddiuniv.ac.in/):
*   **School of Engineering & Emerging Technologies (SEET):** The successor to the original IEET.
*   **School of Pharmacy and Emerging Sciences (SPES).**
*   **School of Sciences (SOS).**
*   **School of Agricultural Sciences (SOAS).**
*   **School of Management Studies (SMS).**
*   **BUEST Nursing College.**

## Community activity
In 2026, the university marked a significant milestone by hosting its **5th Convocation Ceremony**, celebrating the academic achievements of its graduating students [[4]](https://www.baddiuniv.ac.in/baddi-university-proudly-hosted-its-5th-convocation-ceremony/).

## Research or professional work
The institution's national standing is reflected in its rankings. In 2023, BUEST was ranked **146th in India** by the Indian Institutional Ranking Framework (IIRF) [[6]](https://iirfranking.com/university/baddi-university-of-emerging-sciences-and-technology/635ba8e39f687dca4771d733).

This profile does not claim that BUEST is ranked within the top 200 universities in the official National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF) for 2023 or 2024, although the institution has participated in the data submission process [[4]](https://www.baddiuniv.ac.in/).
