## Overview

Rabindranath Tagore University, formally the Rabindranath Thakur Vishwavidyalaya, is a state university in Hojai,
Assam, India. It was created by upgrading Hojai College — a general degree college founded on 11 July 1964 — under
the Rabindranath Thakur Vishwavidyalaya Act, 2017 (Assam Act No. XXXIV of 2017), which received the Assam
Governor's assent on 16 October 2017
([university's own account](https://rtuassam.ac.in/about.php)). The University Grants Commission's own list of
state universities confirms this status, recording a notification date of 25 October 2017 and inclusion in the UGC
list on 19 August 2019
([UGC](https://www.ugc.gov.in/pdfnews/7122907_LSPQ-NO-914-Annexure-I-CPP-I-PU.pdf)) — a state, not private,
university, despite its name resembling that of several private "Rabindranath Tagore University" institutions
elsewhere in India. Prof Manabendra Dutta Choudhury serves as Vice-Chancellor.

## Campus development

[The Sentinel (Assam)](https://www.sentinelassam.com/north-east-india-news/assam-news/formal-handover-of-first-phase-campus-of-rabindranath-tagore-university-in-hojai)
reported that the university's first-phase campus, built at Sagarbasti near Hojai town, was formally handed over
on 10 July 2025. The project carried a sanctioned cost of Rs 58.91 crore against a tendered amount of Rs 56.40
crore, and comprised a double-storeyed academic building (8,366 sq m), an administrative building (2,848 sq m),
electrical infrastructure, water supply and fire-safety systems, and site works including roads, landscaping and a
boundary wall. The contractor was named as Messrs MP Khaitan, and the campus was received by Dr Shekhar Kanti
Sarkar, then Acting Registrar, in the presence of Vice-Chancellor Prof Manabendra Dutta Choudhury.

## Academic partnerships

On 26 December 2025, the Vice-Chancellors of Rabindranath Tagore University and Dibrugarh University signed a
memorandum of understanding intended to "promote joint research initiatives, faculty exchange programmes, and
innovative academic activities for the benefit of students and teachers,"
[The Sentinel (Assam)](https://www.sentinelassam.com/north-east-india-news/assam-news/rabindranath-tagore-university-dibrugarh-university-sign-mou-to-strengthen-collaboration)
reported. The signing was attended by Dibrugarh University Registrar Dr Paramananda Sonowal and Rabindranath
Tagore University's OSD Dr Pranjal Morang, among others.

## Cultural programming

The university has hosted an annual North East Folk Festival under its Viksit Bharat@2047 Cell. The first edition,
held 28-29 January 2025, drew twelve teams from communities across the northeast, including a delegation from
Rajiv Gandhi University in Arunachal Pradesh, and was addressed by Vice-Chancellor Prof Manabendra Dutta Choudhury,
who said the university intended to "expand the festival to a larger scale in the coming years, inviting
participants from across India"
([The Sentinel (Assam)](https://www.sentinelassam.com/north-east-india-news/assam-news/rabindranath-tagore-university-hosts-north-east-folk-festival-celebrating-northeasts-cultural-heritage-in-hojai)).
A second edition followed in 2026, inaugurated by tabla player Paban Bordoloi and including an ethnic food
festival with participation from five affiliated colleges — Lanka Mahavidyalaya, Udali College, Lumding College,
Alhaz Sunai Bibi Choudhury College and Hojai Girls College
([The Sentinel (Assam)](https://www.sentinelassam.com/north-east-india-news/assam-news/northeast-folk-festival-held-at-rabindranath-tagore-university-hojai)).

## Administrative matters

[Careers360](https://news.careers360.com/rabindranath-tagore-university-suspends-deputy-registrar-over-fake-degree-allegation-report-forging-academic-document-secure-post)
reported, citing The Assam Tribune, that the university suspended Deputy Registrar Shekhar Kanti Sarkar with
immediate effect after he failed to satisfactorily respond to a notice concerning an allegation that he had
submitted fraudulent academic credentials. Vice-Chancellor Manabendra Dutta Choudhury was quoted saying "the
suspension was ordered after careful scrutiny of available records and in compliance with institutional norms, and
further action will be taken based on due process." Sarkar is the same official who, as Acting Registrar, had
received the university's first-phase campus on the university's behalf in July 2025.
