# Shri Pillappa College of Engineering

## Overview
Shri Pillappa College of Engineering (SPCE) was a private technical institution located in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India [[1]](https://vtu.ac.in/bengaluru-affiliated-colleges/). Established in 2012 under the Sudha Pillappa Education Trust, the college was situated at #79, Kondasettihalli Road, Hesaraghatta Hobli in the Bangalore North region [[3]](https://educationworld.in/shri-pillappa-college-of-engineering-bangalore-admissions-open-for-engineeing/). While the institution was active for over a decade, providing undergraduate engineering education, it has been reported as closed or inactive as of 2026 [[6]](https://collegeandfees.com/engineering-colleges/karnataka). The college's official web domain is no longer resolving, further indicating a cessation of operations.

## Recognition and accreditation
The institution was formally recognized by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), holding the Institution ID 1-457474151 [[2]](https://facilities.aicte-india.org/dashboard/pages/angulardashboard.php#!/approved). It was affiliated with the Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belagavi, which assigned it the college code "1PL" [[1]](https://vtu.ac.in/bengaluru-affiliated-colleges/) [[4]](https://easenotes.com/blogs/vtu-college-codes.html). While some third-party historical profiles have suggested the institution held accreditation from the National Board of Accreditation (NBA), these claims remain unverified by official regulatory records and are not confirmed by current statutory listings [[5]](https://www.careers360.com/colleges/shri-pillappa-college-of-engineering-bangalore).

## Leadership and governance
SPCE was governed by the Sudha Pillappa Education Trust, which was responsible for its establishment and initial administrative oversight [[3]](https://educationworld.in/shri-pillappa-college-of-engineering-bangalore-admissions-open-for-engineeing/). The trust managed the college's admissions and infrastructure development during its period of operation. As of late 2026, the status of the trust's ongoing involvement with the institution is unclear following reports of the college's closure [[6]](https://collegeandfees.com/engineering-colleges/karnataka).
