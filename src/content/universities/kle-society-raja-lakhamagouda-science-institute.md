## Overview

The **K.L.E. Society's Raja Lakhamagouda Science Institute (Autonomous)**, also known as **RLSI**, is a prominent higher education institution dedicated to scientific studies in Belagavi, Karnataka, India [1]. The institute was founded in 1935 when the Karnataka Lingayat Education (K.L.E.) Society introduced science courses at the existing Lingaraj College [3]. By 1941, the science department had grown sufficiently to be established as an independent college, named in honor of **Sardar Raja Lakhamagouda Basavaprabhu Sardesai of Vantamuri**, a local philanthropist and ruler whose contributions were instrumental to its development [3]. A significant milestone in the institution's history was its formal inauguration in 1944 by the Nobel laureate **Sir C.V. Raman** [3].

## Recognition and accreditation

RLSI is recognized for its academic quality and institutional governance through various national regulatory frameworks. The **University Grants Commission (UGC)** granted the institute autonomous status during the 2010-2011 academic year, a status that has been maintained and extended through official communications such as UGC Letter No. F-2-10/2025 [5].

The institute is currently affiliated with **Rani Channamma University, Belagavi**, having transitioned from its previous affiliation with Karnatak University, Dharwad [2]. In terms of quality assurance, the **National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC)** awarded RLSI an **'A+' Grade** with a **Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 3.42** in its fourth cycle of accreditation in 2024, which remains valid until 2028 [4].

## Leadership and governance

The institute operates under the management of the **K.L.E. Society**, one of India's largest educational organizations [1]. Governance is overseen by a dedicated Governing Body, which is currently chaired by **Dr. Prabhakar P. Kore**, a noted educationalist and political figure [1]. The administration follows the autonomous guidelines set by the UGC to ensure academic and operational flexibility [5].

## Academic programmes

RLSI offers a diverse range of undergraduate and postgraduate programs focused on science and technology. The core academic offerings include:

| Level | Programs Offered |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Undergraduate** | Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.), Bachelor of Computer Applications (BCA) [6] |
| **Postgraduate** | Master of Science (M.Sc.) in Physics and Mathematics, Master of Computer Applications (MCA) [6] |

The institute continues to expand its curriculum, recently announcing the introduction of the **MCA program** for the 2025-2026 academic year to meet the growing demand for technical education [6].

## Community activity

The establishment and growth of the institute were heavily supported by community philanthropy, most notably by the **Sardesai of Vantamuri** family [3]. The institution serves as a regional hub for scientific education in North Karnataka, fulfilling the K.L.E. Society's mission of providing accessible higher education to the local population [1] [3].

## Research or professional work

The institute has a legacy of producing scholars who have contributed significantly to the global scientific community. A notable example is the physicist **Kameshwar C. Wali**, who was part of the institute's inaugural year in 1944 and went on to have a distinguished career in theoretical physics [7]. The autonomous status of the college allows for a curriculum that integrates modern scientific research and professional training [5].
