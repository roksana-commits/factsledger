## Overview

Terra State Community College is a public community college in Fremont, Ohio, founded in 1968. It has operated under three names over its history and was placed under state fiscal watch in 2026.

## Founding and name changes

The college opened on 1 September 1968 as Vanguard Technical Institute, initially enrolling high school juniors in technical programmes including electronics and data processing. The Board of Trustees renamed it Terra Technical College in 1973, and it became Terra State Community College in 1994 when it added Associate of Arts and Associate of Science degrees to its existing applied-science programmes ([Terra State Community College](https://www.terra.edu/about_us/our_history.php)).

## 2026 fiscal watch and leadership changes

On 23 March 2026, the Ohio Department of Higher Education placed Terra State under fiscal watch, following a state auditor's recommendation that cited missing annual audits for fiscal years 2024 and 2025, late payments to vendors, and flaws in the college's payment processing ([Tiffin Ohio](https://tiffinohio.net/posts/terra-state-names-chad-breeden-interim-president/)). President Dr. Ronald Schumacher, who had led the college since 2018, resigned on 2 April 2026, and Dr. Cory Stine was appointed interim president the same day; the Board of Trustees announced a fiscal recovery plan involving closer coordination with the Ohio Auditor of State and more frequent financial review cycles ([13abc](https://www.13abc.com/2026/04/04/terra-state-community-college-announces-leadership-change-fiscal-recovery-plan/)).

Dr. Stine left for another college position after less than two months, and Dr. Chad Breeden, a retired Marine Corps colonel and vice president of operations at the United States Naval Community College, became the college's second interim president effective 20 July 2026, tasked with continuing the state-required financial recovery plan ([Tiffin Ohio](https://tiffinohio.net/posts/terra-state-names-chad-breeden-interim-president/)).
