# Overview

Tura Government College is a prominent higher education institution situated in Tura, within the West Garo Hills District of Meghalaya, India [1]. Established in 1958, the college serves as a primary academic hub for the Garo Hills region, providing co-educational undergraduate instruction across various disciplines [1] [4]. The institution is managed directly by the Department of Education under the Government of Meghalaya and is situated on its own urban campus [1] [5].

As of the 2023-2024 academic year, the college maintains a significant student body with 3,293 enrolled students and a faculty strength of 77 full-time members against 86 sanctioned posts [2]. The institution celebrated its 65th Foundation Day on September 9, 2023, marking its long-standing contribution to the state's educational landscape [5].

# Recognition and accreditation

Tura Government College holds statutory recognition from the University Grants Commission (UGC) under Sections 2(f) and 12(B) of the UGC Act, 1956 [3]. The college achieved a significant milestone in quality assurance by receiving accreditation from the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC) with a Grade B+ (CGPA of 2.53) during its first accreditation cycle [1]. This accreditation is valid for a five-year period from June 28, 2022, to June 27, 2027 [1].

# Leadership and governance

The college is a government-run institution governed by the Government of Meghalaya [1] [5]. The foundation of the college was a result of efforts by regional leaders, most notably Capt. Williamson A. Sangma, who served as the first Chief Minister of Meghalaya, alongside Mody K. Marak and Harrison W. Momin [5]. The current administrative leadership is headed by the Principal, Shri Isaac W. Momin [1].

# Academic programmes

The institution is affiliated with the North Eastern Hill University (NEHU) in Shillong and offers undergraduate degrees in Arts, Science, and Commerce [1] [2]. In response to national educational reforms, the college implemented the Four Year Undergraduate Programme (FYUP) starting from the 2023-2024 academic session, aligning with the National Education Policy (NEP) 2020 [2].

The academic offerings are organized across 17 departments, including:
*   **Humanities and Social Sciences:** English, Garo, Assamese, Bengali, Hindi, History, Political Science, Economics, Philosophy, and Education [2].
*   **Sciences:** Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, Botany, and Zoology [2].
*   **Commerce:** Comprehensive undergraduate commerce curriculum [2].

During the 2023-2024 session, the college offered a total of 200 courses, with 1,048 students reaching their final year of study [2].

# Community activity

Tura Government College actively engages in student development and national youth initiatives. In July 2023, the institution organized "Deeksharambh," its inaugural Student Induction Programme designed to assist new students in transitioning to the NEP 2020 framework [2].

Recent activities include:
*   **Employability Workshops:** On August 19, 2026, the college hosted a "College to Career" workshop under the MeghDEE initiative to enhance student career prospects [6].
*   **National Sports Engagement:** The institution participated in the nationwide Khelo India Dialogue on August 29, 2026, promoting sports and youth development [7].
