# Overview

Sankardev College is a prominent government-aided undergraduate institution located in Bishnupur, Shillong, Meghalaya [1]. Established on September 2, 1962, the college serves as a key educational hub in the East Khasi Hills district [1] [2]. It is permanently affiliated with North Eastern Hill University (NEHU) and is recognized by the University Grants Commission (UGC) under Sections 2(f) and 12(B) of the UGC Act, 1956 [2]. The college provides higher education to approximately 1,600 students across Arts and Science streams [1].

# Recognition and accreditation

The college maintains official recognition as a "Non-Government, Aided" institution [2]. It has undergone multiple cycles of assessment by the National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC). In its second cycle, it was awarded a 'B' Grade with a CGPA of 2.55 [3]. More recent institutional markers and secondary educational portals indicate an upgraded status of **'B+' Grade** as of the 2024-2026 period [4] [5]. Additionally, the college is recognized by the Government of Meghalaya as a tobacco-free campus and serves as a designated venue for Meghalaya Public Service Commission (MPSC) examinations [6] [7].

# Leadership and governance

Sankardev College is managed by a Governing Body and operates under the Deficit System of Grants-in-Aid, which it adopted in 1966 [1]. Historically, the college's leadership included notable figures such as M.M. Choudhury, the former Speaker of the Assam Legislative Assembly [1]. As of 2026, the institution is led by Principal **Dr. Eureka F. P. Lyngdoh** [8]. The college employs a staff of approximately 63 teaching members and 26 non-teaching members to support its academic operations [1].

# Academic programmes

The college offers Bachelor of Arts (B.A.) and Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) degrees with Honours options across various disciplines [1] [9].

| Stream | Honours Subjects Offered |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Arts** | English, Economics, Political Science, Education, History, Philosophy, Sociology, Khasi |
| **Science** | Physics, Mathematics, Chemistry, Botany, Zoology, Statistics |

The B.Sc. General course was introduced in 1991, followed by the introduction of Honours in science disciplines in 1995 [1]. The Department of Statistics was formally established in 2006 [1].

# Community activity

The college is active in community service and national development programs. Its National Service Scheme (NSS) Unit has achieved national recognition, receiving the **Indira Gandhi NSS Award** in 2011 [1]. The institution also collaborates with state authorities for public awareness initiatives, such as hosting events for Financial Literacy Week in coordination with the Reserve Bank of India and the Government of Meghalaya [6].
