## Overview

Adikavi Nannaya University is a state university headquartered in Rajahmundry (Rajamahendravaram), in the East
Godavari district of Andhra Pradesh, India. The University Grants Commission's own consolidated list of
universities records it as a state university established in 2006
([University Grants Commission](https://www.ugc.gov.in/oldpdf/Consolidated%20list%20of%20All%20Universities.pdf)).
Its academic reach extends beyond its headquarters to campuses at Kakinada and Tadepalligudem, with affiliated
colleges spread across the combined Godavari districts ([The Hans India](https://www.thehansindia.com/andhra-pradesh/applications-invited-for-aknu-convocation-1047102)).

## Leadership

In February 2025, the Governor of Andhra Pradesh, Justice S. Abdul Nazeer, issued a notification naming
[Prof. Sathupati Prasanna Sree](/people/sathupati-prasanna-sree/) — until then a professor of English at Andhra University — as the university's
vice-chancellor ([Deccan Chronicle](https://www.deccanchronicle.com/southern-states/andhra-pradesh/new-vcs-named-for-9-ap-universities-1862038)).
The same notification named vice-chancellors for eight other Andhra Pradesh state universities. In November 2025,
Prasanna Sree received the Ramoji Excellence Award 2025 in the art-and-culture category, presented by the
Vice-President of India, CP Radhakrishnan, in recognition of her work developing scripts for endangered tribal
languages ([The Hans India](https://www.thehansindia.com/andhra-pradesh/aknu-v-c-prasanna-receives-ramoji-excellence-award-1023924)).

## Recognition and infrastructure

In January 2026, the university received a five-star quality rating certificate from HIM International
Certification, presented by Andhra Pradesh IT and Human Resources Development Minister Nara Lokesh at a
vice-chancellors' conference in Vijayawada. The Hans India described it as the first time any university in the
state had received the honour ([The Hans India](https://www.thehansindia.com/andhra-pradesh/aknu-bags-prestigious-5-star-quality-rating-1037139)).

The previous month, Minister Lokesh had inaugurated three new academic blocks — named Manjeera, Gautami and
Indravati — built at a combined cost of about ₹34 crore at the university's Arts College campus in
Rajamahendravaram. During the same visit, he also toured the neighbouring
[Government College, Rajahmundry](/universities/government-college-rajahmundry/), described in the report as a
173-year-old institution ([The Hans India](https://www.thehansindia.com/andhra-pradesh/lokesh-inaugurates-new-buildings-at-arts-college-aknu-1032407)).

## Academic administration

In July 2026, the university launched an online moderation system for assessing B.Ed (teacher-education) projects,
records and academic activities, incorporating GPS and facial-recognition technology. The Hans India reported it as
the first system of its kind introduced by a university in the Telugu-speaking states
([The Hans India](https://www.thehansindia.com/andhra-pradesh/online-moderation-system-launched-at-aknu-1096268)).

In February 2026, the university invited applications for its 16th, 17th and 18th convocations, to be held
together and covering postgraduate, undergraduate and PhD degrees from the 2022–23 through 2024–25 academic years,
open to students from its Rajamahendravaram, Kakinada and Tadepalligudem campuses and its affiliated colleges in
the combined Godavari districts ([The Hans India](https://www.thehansindia.com/andhra-pradesh/applications-invited-for-aknu-convocation-1047102)).

## What this profile does not claim

This profile does not assert the specific founding statute number or the naming rationale (after the Telugu poet
Nannayya) reported only on the university's English Wikipedia article, since no independent, fetchable source
confirming either detail was found; it states only the year of establishment, independently confirmed by the UGC's
own list. It also does not restate a Hans India report's description of the university as the state's largest, with
seven university college campuses and about 320 affiliated colleges, since that specific figure rests on a single
source and was not independently corroborated. No NAAC accreditation grade is stated, because no independent source
reporting one was found.
