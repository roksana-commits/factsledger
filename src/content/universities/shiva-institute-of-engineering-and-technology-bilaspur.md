## Overview

The Shiva Institute of Engineering and Technology (SIET), located in Bilaspur, Himachal Pradesh, was a private technical college established in 2009 [4](https://www.shiksha.com/college/shiva-group-of-institutions-bilaspur-59193) [6](https://bharateducation.org/details.php?view_id=96). Managed by the Shiva Educational Society, the institution was part of the Shiva Group of Institutions [3](https://shivaeducation.com/) [7](https://www.facebook.com/SGIbilaspurHP/). It was founded to address the demand for technical education in the region, offering both undergraduate and diploma programs [4](https://www.shiksha.com/college/shiva-group-of-institutions-bilaspur-59193). However, as of August 2026, the institution is classified as defunct or in a state of progressive closure, with its official website becoming inactive [2](https://www.himtu.ac.in/en/about-us/constituent-units) [8](http://siethp.in/).

## Recognition and accreditation

During its operational tenure, SIET was recognized by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), which provided the necessary regulatory approvals for its technical programs [1](https://www.aicte-india.org/). The college was initially affiliated with Himachal Pradesh University (HPU) before transferring its affiliation to Himachal Pradesh Technical University (HPTU) [4](https://www.shiksha.com/college/shiva-group-of-institutions-bilaspur-59193) [6](https://bharateducation.org/details.php?view_id=96). As of August 27, 2026, the institution is no longer included in the HPTU list of affiliated colleges, confirming its loss of active status [2](https://www.himtu.ac.in/en/about-us/constituent-units).

## Leadership and governance

The institution was governed by the Shiva Educational Society, which also manages other educational units in the region, such as the Shiva Institute of Pharmacy and the Shiva College of Education [3](https://shivaeducation.com/) [7](https://www.facebook.com/SGIbilaspurHP/). The society has recently shifted its focus toward medical and pharmacy-related disciplines, which coincided with the phasing out of the engineering department [4](https://www.shiksha.com/college/shiva-group-of-institutions-bilaspur-59193).
