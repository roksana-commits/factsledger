# Overview

The Sri Dharmasthala Manjunatheshwara (SDM) College of Dental Sciences & Hospital is a private dental institution located in Sattur, Dharwad, Karnataka [4]. Established on September 16, 1986, the college was founded by the Shri Dharmasthala Manjunatheshwara Educational (SDME) Society under the leadership of Dr. D. Veerendra Heggade [4]. The institution occupies a 23-acre campus and has grown from an affiliated college to a significant constituent unit of a private university [4] [3].

# Recognition and accreditation

SDM College of Dental Sciences & Hospital is recognized by the **Dental Council of India (DCI)** for its undergraduate and postgraduate dental programs [1]. The institution has a long history of high-tier accreditation, becoming the first dental college in India to be awarded five-star status by the **National Assessment and Accreditation Council (NAAC)** in 2002 [5]. It has subsequently maintained an 'A' grade across three consecutive NAAC accreditation cycles [5]. In the **2025 National Institutional Ranking Framework (NIRF)**, the college was ranked **25th** in the Dental category [2].

# Leadership and governance

The college is governed as a constituent unit of the **Shri Dharmasthala Manjunatheshwara University (SDMU)**, following the enactment of the Shri Dharmasthala Manjunatheshwara University Act, 2018 by the Government of Karnataka [3]. The university operates as a private unitary institution, with the college transitioning from its previous affiliation with the Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences (RGUHS) to the SDMU framework on December 19, 2018 [3] [4].

# Academic programmes

The institution offers a comprehensive range of dental education programs:

| Bachelor of Dental Surgery (BDS) | Undergraduate | 100 [1] |

The BDS degree is also recognized by the **Government of Malaysia**, facilitating international professional mobility for its graduates [4].

# Community activity

The college is actively involved in specialized healthcare services, notably through its long-standing partnership with **Smile Train (USA)** [4]. This collaboration focuses on providing corrective surgeries for patients with cleft lip and palate deformities, integrating clinical service with professional training [4].

# Research or professional work

A major distinction of the college is its pioneering role in forensic dentistry. In January 2006, the institution established India's first exclusive **Department of Forensic Odontology** [6]. This department provides specialized fellowship programs and has been instrumental in assisting national criminal investigations through dental identification and analysis [6] [4]. Furthermore, the college maintains a Memorandum of Understanding with **Tufts University School of Dental Medicine** in the United States for academic and research collaboration [4].
