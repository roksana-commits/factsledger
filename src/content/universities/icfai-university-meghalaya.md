## Overview

ICFAI University, Meghalaya is a private university in the Indian state of Meghalaya. It appears on the
[Government of Meghalaya's list of private universities](https://megeducation.gov.in/dhte/pages/priuniversity.html),
which records it as established under the ICFAIU Act 2005. Its headquarters are in Tura, in West Garo Hills, and it
operates a second campus in Shillong.

The university is part of the ICFAI group, which operates separately constituted universities in several Indian
states. Reporting about "ICFAI University" in the region frequently concerns one of those other institutions;
claims on this page are limited to sources that identify the Meghalaya university specifically.

## Statutory status and recognition

The university was established under the Institute of Chartered Financial Analysts of India University Act, 2005,
listed by the state's Directorate of Higher and Technical Education as the ICFAIU Act 2005 and cited by the
university itself as Meghalaya Act No. 12 of 2005
([recognitions page](https://www.iumeghalaya.edu.in/aboutus/recognitions-accreditations) — first-party).

The university states that it was notified under Section 2(f) of the UGC Act 1956 by notification
No. F.9-16/2007 (CPP-I) dated 4 November 2009, and that it is competent to award degrees specified by the
University Grants Commission under Section 22 of that Act (first-party). It also lists membership of the
Association of Indian Universities and the Association of Commonwealth Universities. These are recorded here as the
university's own statements; see [Open items](#open-items) for what could not be checked independently.

## Campuses

The university describes its Tura headquarters as occupying 13.5 acres, and its Shillong campus as housed in a
rented building at Dhankheti ([about page](https://www.iumeghalaya.edu.in/aboutus/about-university) — first-party).

## Academic structure

The university lists three faculties — Management Studies, Science and Technology, and Humanities and Social
Sciences — offering undergraduate programmes including BBA, BCA, B.Com, B.Sc and BA (Hons) degrees, and
postgraduate programmes including MBA, MCA, MSW, M.Com and MA specialisations (first-party).

### Academic activity

In July 2026, [The Shillong Times](https://theshillongtimes.com/2026/07/18/icfai-tura-hosts-natl-seminar-on-digital-marketing/)
reported that the Department of Management at the Tura campus concluded a three-day national seminar,
"Digital Disruption in Marketing: Challenges, Opportunities and Sustainability", organised with IUP Publications.
The report records participants from 13 states and four technical sessions with moderators from institutions
including IIM Rohtak and North-Eastern Hill University.

## Leadership

**Dr Alicia Gatphoh** is identified as Vice-Chancellor in
[The Shillong Times' report of the July 2026 national seminar](https://theshillongtimes.com/2026/07/18/tura-ni-icfai-university-meghalaya-o-salgittamna-ong%C2%B7atanggipa-national-seminar-o-icfai-university-ni-vice-chancellor-dr-alicia-gatphoh-aro-gipin-skigiparang-bak-ra%C2%B7enga/),
and held the office at the university's fourth convocation in April 2023
([Highland Post](https://highlandpost.com/icfai-holds-convocation-ceremony-for-544-students/)).

At that 2023 convocation, Lt General (Dr) Arvinder Singh Lambha was reported as Chancellor.

## Convocation

[Highland Post](https://highlandpost.com/icfai-holds-convocation-ceremony-for-544-students/) reported that the
university's fourth convocation, held on 29 April 2023, conferred degrees on 544 students. Chief Minister
Conrad K. Sangma attended as chief guest. The ceremony included six general proficiency awards, 15 gold medals and
18 silver medals.

## Student activity

In August 2026, [Hub Network](https://hubnetwork.in/tura-ni-icfai-university-o-khelo-india-dialogue-ko-ong%C2%B7ata/)
reported that the university's NSS unit hosted a Khelo India Dialogue event at Tura to mark National Sports Day,
supported by the Ministry of Youth Affairs and Sports, MY Bharat and the Sports Authority of India. The report names
NSS Programme Officer Bryan Boon R. Marak, West Garo Hills District Youth Officer Himanshu Kashyap, and Meghalaya
Ranji Trophy cricketer Kilco R. Marak among those present.

## What this profile does not claim

No claim is made about the university's size, ranking, placement outcomes or standing. Directory and admissions
aggregator sites publish figures on all of these; none is cited here, because those sites reproduce unverified data.
No accreditation grade is stated, because no accreditation was found in any source.
