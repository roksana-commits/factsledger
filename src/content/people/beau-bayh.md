## Overview

Birch Evans "Beau" Bayh IV is a seventh-generation Hoosier, a U.S. Marine Corps veteran and
attorney. He is the son of Evan Bayh, a former Indiana governor and U.S. senator, and the
grandson of Birch Bayh, also a former U.S. senator — a family political lineage spanning
three prior generations of Indiana officeholders. He holds degrees from Harvard University,
served as a Marine Corps infantry officer, and worked as a judicial law clerk for a federal
appeals court judge until August 2025
([WTHR](https://www.wthr.com/article/news/politics/beau-bayh-son-former-indiana-governor-and-us-senator-evan-bayh-launches-campaign-indiana-secretary-of-state-diego-morales/531-c6f6f4a2-2cda-4fc2-a498-9be3b1f428e0)).

## 2026 Secretary of State campaign

On 6 October 2025, Bayh filed to run for Indiana Secretary of State as a Democrat,
challenging Republican incumbent Diego Morales. He framed his campaign around cleaning up
the office, citing Morales's hiring of a brother-in-law and questions about travel spending
and vehicle purchases. His platform included eliminating wasteful spending, expanding voter
access in a state that ranks 50th nationally in voter participation, ending straight-ticket
voting, and pledging to serve as a nonpartisan administrator
([WTHR](https://www.wthr.com/article/news/politics/beau-bayh-son-former-indiana-governor-and-us-senator-evan-bayh-launches-campaign-indiana-secretary-of-state-diego-morales/531-c6f6f4a2-2cda-4fc2-a498-9be3b1f428e0);
[Indiana Capital Chronicle](https://indianacapitalchronicle.com/2025/10/06/democrat-beau-bayh-to-run-for-indiana-secretary-of-state/)).

On 6 June 2026, Bayh won the Democratic nomination at the state convention, defeating
Blythe Potter with 61 percent of delegate votes (1,385 to 883). He entered the convention
with a substantial fundraising advantage, $1.9 million on hand compared with Potter's
$66,600, and campaigned on an independent audit of the secretary of state's office, expanded
voter participation, and stronger election security including voter ID support
([Indiana Capital Chronicle](https://indianacapitalchronicle.com/2026/06/06/beau-bayh-defeats-blythe-potter-for-democratic-secretary-of-state-nomination/)).
