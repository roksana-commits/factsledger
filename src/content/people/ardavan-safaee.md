## Overview

Ardavan Safaee has been president of Pathé Films, one of France's largest film production and distribution
companies, since June 2019, succeeding Jérôme Seydoux in the role. He joined the Pathé group in 2015 as
director of production and became director general of Pathé Films in February 2018 before his promotion to
president the following year
([Box Office Pro](https://www.boxofficepro.fr/promotion-pour-aurelien-bosc-ardavan-safaee-et-olivier-grandjean-chez-pathe/)).
In a November 2024 interview, he described the French film industry as having "become cool again," pointing
to a slate that included "Monte-Cristo," "Emilia Pérez" and "Monsieur Aznavour"
([Écran Total](https://ecran-total.fr/2024/11/29/ardavan-safaee-president-de-pathe-films-le-cinema-est-redevenu-cool/)).

## Testimony on industry conduct

Safaee testified to a French National Assembly committee investigating violence in the film industry,
describing what he called "a considerable change in mentalities" at Pathé over his roughly five years
leading the company. He told the committee that Pathé had introduced conduct codes addressing physical and
moral violence, deployed on-set production staff to reinforce reporting procedures, required dual referents
of both genders on productions, and extended protective measures to off-set teams such as costume and
set-design departments
([Assemblée Nationale](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/17/comptes-rendus/cecine/l17cecine2425043_compte-rendu.pdf)).

As one example, he described a 2020 production in which Pathé received a complaint of sexual assault
against a director; the company removed the director from the set and from post-production work, while
managing the contractual complexities that arose around the director's authorship rights on the finished
film. He also discussed how the company weighs legal responsibility, reputational risk and economic factors
when deciding whether to continue working with someone facing an allegation
([Assemblée Nationale](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/17/comptes-rendus/cecine/l17cecine2425043_compte-rendu.pdf)).

## What this profile does not claim

This profile describes Safaee's own account, given under oath to a parliamentary committee, of how Pathé
responded institutionally to a 2020 complaint. No source read here independently investigates or confirms
the underlying allegation, names the director involved, or establishes what became of that person
afterward, and none of that is asserted here.
