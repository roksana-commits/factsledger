## Overview

Ardavan Safaee has been president of Pathé Films, one of France's largest film production and distribution
companies, since June 2019, succeeding Jérôme Seydoux in the role. He joined the Pathé group in 2015 as
director of production and became director general of Pathé Films in February 2018 before his promotion to
president the following year
([Box Office Pro](https://www.boxofficepro.fr/promotion-pour-aurelien-bosc-ardavan-safaee-et-olivier-grandjean-chez-pathe/)).
In a November 2024 interview, he described the French film industry as having "become cool again," pointing
to a slate that included "Monte-Cristo," "Emilia Pérez" and "Monsieur Aznavour"
([Écran Total](https://ecran-total.fr/2024/11/29/ardavan-safaee-president-de-pathe-films-le-cinema-est-redevenu-cool/)).

## Testimony on industry conduct

Safaee testified to a French National Assembly committee investigating violence in the film industry,
describing what he called "a considerable change in mentalities" at Pathé over his roughly five years
leading the company. He told the committee that Pathé had introduced conduct codes addressing physical and
moral violence, deployed on-set production staff to reinforce reporting procedures, required dual referents
of both genders on productions, and extended protective measures to off-set teams such as costume and
set-design departments
([Assemblée Nationale](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/17/comptes-rendus/cecine/l17cecine2425043_compte-rendu.pdf)).

As one example, he described a 2020 production in which Pathé received a complaint of sexual assault
against a director; the company removed the director from the set and from post-production work, while
managing the contractual complexities that arose around the director's authorship rights on the finished
film. He also discussed how the company weighs legal responsibility, reputational risk and economic factors
when deciding whether to continue working with someone facing an allegation
([Assemblée Nationale](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/17/comptes-rendus/cecine/l17cecine2425043_compte-rendu.pdf)).

## Career before Pathé

Before joining Pathé in 2015, Safaee worked at production companies including Elzevir Films and Bonne
Pioche, and co-founded his own production company with actor Grégoire Colin, according to an interview
published on Bpifrance's "La French Touch" platform
([La French Touch](https://www.la-frenchtouch.fr/comment-renforcer-le-cinema-francais-a-linternational/)).
A separate interview, also published by Bpifrance, describes the same pre-Pathé history in near-identical
terms
([Bpifrance](https://www.bpifrance.fr/nos-actualites/ardavan-safaee-pathe-films-au-debut-de-la-crise-etait-tous-sideres)).
Because both accounts trace back to the same broader publisher, and no independent tier1 or tier2 outlet
confirming these specific companies or the co-founding was found despite searching, this detail is treated
here as single-publisher sourcing rather than independently corroborated.

## International strategy and partnerships

In a February 2022 interview, Safaee laid out three priorities he said were guiding Pathé. On risk-taking,
he said the company wanted to "go higher, take more risks, make even bigger films, always oriented toward
the international market," citing "Eiffel," "Notre-Dame brûle" (directed by Jean-Jacques Annaud),
"Astérix" and "Les Trois Mousquetaires" as examples. On talent retention, he said Pathé negotiates its own
deals with talent and relies on the fast decision-making typical of family-run companies to "recreate a
family, a home" for them, which he said mattered "in a world where the algorithm is king." On
cross-industry partnerships, he pointed to an augmented-reality experience built around "Notre-Dame brûle"
in collaboration with Ubisoft as a possible model for pooling resources across creative industries
([La French Touch](https://www.la-frenchtouch.fr/comment-renforcer-le-cinema-francais-a-linternational/)).
This interview is the only source found for these stated views, so they are presented here as Safaee's own
account rather than independently verified strategy.

## Producer credits and upcoming slate

UniFrance, the official French body for exporting French cinema abroad, lists Safaee's current role at
Pathé Films as "CEO" in its English-language directory and separately credits him as a producer at Tsilaosa
Films. Its filmography attributes 49 titles to him between 2016 and 2027, aggregated as 38 co-producer
credits, 9 executive-producer credits, one associate-producer credit and one foreign-producer credit,
without specifying which category applies to any individual film. Its listing includes four titles dated
2026 — "De Gaulle : Liberté," "De Gaulle : Résistance," "L'Enragé" and "The Unknown" — and one dated 2027,
"Wake Me Up," though it does not specify his individual producing role on any of the five
([UniFrance](https://en.unifrance.org/directories/person/361759/ardavan-safaee)).
