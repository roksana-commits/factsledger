## Overview

Ankit Baiyanpuria, born Ankit Singh on 31 August 1998 in Bayanpur village, Haryana, is an Indian
fitness content creator with a background in wrestling. He is best known for popularizing the "75
Hard Challenge" fitness regimen alongside traditional Indian ("desi") workout content aimed at an
Indian audience ([Khel Now](https://khelnow.com/mma/ankit-baiyanpuria-arman-tsarukyan-grappling-exhibition)).
His work was recognized at India's inaugural National Creators Award, where he won "Best Health and
Fitness Creator" in a ceremony presented by Prime Minister Narendra Modi at Bharat Mandapam
([DNA India](https://www.dnaindia.com/india/report-ankit-baiyanpuria-national-creators-award-best-health-fitness-creator-narendra-modi)).

## A grappling exhibition, not a professional MMA record

In 2026, Baiyanpuria was scheduled to take part in a grappling exhibition against UFC lightweight
fighter Arman Tsarukyan. Sports coverage of the matchup was explicit that Baiyanpuria holds no
professional MMA record — the bout is presented as an exhibition tied to his profile as a fitness
personality, not a professional MMA credential
([Khel Now](https://khelnow.com/mma/ankit-baiyanpuria-arman-tsarukyan-grappling-exhibition)).
