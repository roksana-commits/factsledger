## Overview

Kiran Nazish is a Pakistani journalist who worked across print, radio and television before becoming a
foreign and war correspondent covering conflicts including in Iraq. She studied economics and mathematics
to the master's level while pursuing journalism alongside her studies
([Lenfest Institute](https://www.lenfestinstitute.org/solutions-resources/women-in-journalism-qa-with-kiran-nazish/)).

## Founding the Coalition For Women In Journalism

Around 2015, after years of conflict reporting, Nazish suffered a breakdown and was hospitalized with
panic attacks while also receiving threats from Pakistani intelligence agencies. "I am a journalist who
sacrificed a lot for my career... I couldn't transcend certain things," she has said. Following her
hospitalization, she interviewed experienced women journalists and found a recurring pattern: a lack of
mentorship and support. That research led her to found the Coalition For Women In Journalism (CFWIJ), a
nonprofit built around mentorship, research and advocacy for women in the field
([Lenfest Institute](https://www.lenfestinstitute.org/solutions-resources/women-in-journalism-qa-with-kiran-nazish/)).

## Documenting violence against women journalists

Under Nazish, CFWIJ built a press-freedom newsroom tracking violations against women journalists across
128 countries, arguing that gender had been largely missing from legacy press-freedom organizations'
coverage. In May 2021, the coalition's research documented 70 cases of violence against women journalists
in a single month, spanning physical attacks, detentions and threats tied to online harassment campaigns,
and specifically recorded the killing of Palestinian journalist Reema Saad and the abduction of Nigerian
journalist Amra Ahmed Diska ([Journalism.co.uk](https://www.journalism.co.uk/podcast/kiran-nazish-founder-of-cfwij-on-addressing-attacks-on-women-journalists/s399/a842389/)).

## What this profile does not claim

Both sources used here are interview/Q&A formats in which Nazish describes her own experience and her
organization's work in her own words; this profile does not independently verify the specific case counts
or incidents CFWIJ reports, beyond noting that both outlets are editorially independent trade/nonprofit
publications rather than CFWIJ's own channels.
