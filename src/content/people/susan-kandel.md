## Overview

Susan Kandel, born October 31, 1961, in Los Angeles, worked for about seven years as an art
critic for the Los Angeles Times and edited art/text magazine, while also teaching art
history and theory at UCLA, the Art Center College of Design and UC Santa Barbara. She holds
a history degree from Williams College (1983) and an M.A. in art history from NYU's
Institute of Fine Arts
([Encyclopedia.com](https://www.encyclopedia.com/arts/educational-magazines/kandel-susan-1961)).

## Fiction career

Kandel later turned to fiction, writing the five-book "Cece Caruso" mystery series
(2004-2007) for William Morrow, featuring a vintage-clothing-obsessed biographer of mystery
writers as its detective. The debut, "I Dreamed I Married Perry Mason," and its sequel,
"Not a Girl Detective," both received independent critical notice from Kirkus Reviews, and
the debut was also reviewed by Publishers Weekly
([Kirkus Reviews](https://www.kirkusreviews.com/book-reviews/susan-kandel/i-dreamed-i-married-perry-mason/);
[Publishers Weekly](https://www.publishersweekly.com/978-0-06-058105-3)).

Note: this profile is about the art critic and novelist. It is not related to a different
Susan Kandel, a New Jersey nursery-school educator who died in 2016.
