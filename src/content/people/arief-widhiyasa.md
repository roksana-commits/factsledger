## Overview

Arief Widhiyasa is an Indonesian entrepreneur identified by Forbes as a co-founder and chief executive of [Agate](/organizations/agate-game-company/), a video game developer headquartered in Bandung, West Java. Agate itself was founded on April 1, 2009 ([The Jakarta Post](https://www.thejakartapost.com/news/2017/08/22/agate-studio-story-perseverance-dreamers.html)); Forbes named Widhiyasa "Cofounder & CEO, Agate Studio" in its 2016 "30 Under 30 Asia" list, in the Consumer Tech category, when he was 28 ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/pictures/gegd45efke/arief-widhiyasa/)).

## As Agate's chief executive

In September 2013, as Agate's CEO, Widhiyasa spoke to press about the company's newly announced partnership with the Japanese publisher Square Enix to bring the web-based game "Sengoku IXA" to Indonesia, describing the deal as accelerating "transfer pengetahuan dari developer game yang sudah dewasa ke developer game yang masih baru" (knowledge transfer from a mature game developer to a newer one) ([IDN Times/Duniaku](https://duniaku.idntimes.com/geek/culture/febrianto-nur-anwari/igs-2013-konferensi-pers-agate-studio-dan-square-enix-luncurkan-sengoku-ixa-di-indonesia)).

## Transition to Chairman

On June 1, 2022, Widhiyasa stepped down as Agate's chief executive to become its Chairman, with then-Chief Operating Officer [Shieny Aprilia](/people/shieny-aprilia/) named as his successor. Merdeka.com quoted him saying of Aprilia: "Shieny bersama saya selalu ada di garda terdepan" (Shieny has consistently been at the forefront alongside me) ([Merdeka.com](https://www.merdeka.com/teknologi/agate-umumkan-shieny-aprilia-jadi-ceo-baru-agate.html)).

## Mythic Protocol

By September 2023, Widhiyasa had co-founded Mythic Protocol, a web3 game studio, together with Igor Tanzil, a former Agate chief marketing officer and chief creative officer. On September 12, 2023, the gaming trade press PocketGamer.biz reported that Mythic Protocol had closed an oversubscribed $6.5 million seed round, co-led by Shima Capital and Alpha JWC, with participation from Saison Capital, GDV Venture, Planetarium Labs, Arcane Group, Presto Labs, Marblex, Emurgo Ventures, Hyperithm and unnamed angel investors. The funding was earmarked for completing and launching "Riftstorm," a cross-platform action-shooter roguelite for mobile, console and PC, and for building out a blockchain-based "Decentralised Universal Meta" and a set of shared assets called "Legacy" ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/mythic-protocol-raises-65m-towards-blockchain-collaboration-and-cross-platform-ambitions/)). Widhiyasa was quoted saying the game industry "undergoes a new cycle every 25 years like clockwork," attributing a current cycle to distributed computing and AI; BeyondGames.biz separately reported that Mythic Protocol's team had grown to more than 130 people globally, including hires from Microsoft, Samsung and Caravan Studio ([BeyondGames.biz](https://www.beyondgames.biz/41949/mythic-protocol-raises-6-5m-for-multi-platform-games-blockchain-and-decentralised-universal-meta/)).

## What this profile does not claim

No source read states the exact date Widhiyasa personally joined or co-founded Agate; this profile combines The Jakarta Post's independently documented April 2009 founding date for the company with Forbes' separate identification of him as a co-founder and CEO as of 2016, rather than asserting a single sourced fact that covers both. The Jakarta Post's own 2017 account of Agate's founding names only Aditia Dwiperdana among its 18 co-founders and does not mention Widhiyasa. Mythic Protocol's precise founding date is not stated in any source read. This profile does not state Widhiyasa's date or place of birth, and does not cover any activity after the events described above. It is not to be confused with the unrelated French tabletop role-playing game publisher "Studio Agate" — this profile concerns only a leader of the Indonesian, Bandung-based video game developer Agate.
