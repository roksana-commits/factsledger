## Overview

Jeremiaha Maluze is a 21-year-old footballer from Duisburg, Germany, who took an unusually long
route to the Bundesliga. After coming through the youth academies of Rot-Weiß Oberhausen, KFC
Uerdingen and MSV Duisburg, he spent the 2023/24 season playing semi-professionally for SV Straelen
and Mühlheimer FC in the fifth-tier Oberliga Niederrhein. A breakout season at fourth-tier FC
Rot-Weiß Erfurt followed, where he recorded 3 goals and 10 assists in 26 games — form strong enough
to earn him a first-team contract with Bundesliga club Eintracht Frankfurt in 2025. Sporting
director Timmo Hardung said at the time that Maluze "has made great progress in recent months" and
has "the attributes to continue on his path in professional football"
([kicker.de](https://www.kicker.de/jerimiaha-maluze/spieler)).

## A competitive debut

In August 2026, Maluze made his first competitive appearance for Eintracht Frankfurt's first team,
coming on as a 68th-minute substitute for team captain Robin Koch in a DFB-Pokal first-round match
against SC St. Tönis at the Grotenburg-Stadion in Krefeld — roughly 15 kilometers from his home
city of Duisburg, where his mother and eight friends watched him play while his father followed
from Nigeria. Frankfurt won the match 11–0. Head coach Adi Hütter praised his passing and defensive
positioning afterward, saying "he can become a good player," while noting his timing in man-marking
situations still needs work
([Wetterauer Zeitung](https://www.wetterauer-zeitung.de)). Eintracht Frankfurt announced an
extension of his contract to 2031 later that month, on 29 August 2026
([Eintracht Frankfurt](https://en.eintracht.de)).
