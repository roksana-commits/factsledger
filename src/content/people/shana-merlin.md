## Overview

Shana Merlin is an Austin, Texas-based improv performer, teacher and producer whose career
the Austin Chronicle, the city's alternative newsweekly, has documented continuously since
at least 2000. In a review of her one-woman show that year, the paper praised her
"youngest-child adorability and wry observations"
([The Austin Chronicle](https://www.austinchronicle.com/search/keywords:Shana+Merlin/)).

## Merlin Works and Austin improv

In 2008, Merlin opened the Merlin Works Institute for Improvisation alongside Gnap! Theater
Projects, an expansion the Chronicle covered as a notable moment for the city's improv
scene. She also co-founded the long-running improv duo Get Up, performing with Shannon
McCormick, and became a member of the troupe Girls Girls Girls. In 2010, she helped produce
the first Ladies Are Funny Festival with other local comedians. By 2014, the Chronicle
named Merlin Works among "The Five Families of Austin improv," identifying Merlin as its
guiding force
([The Austin Chronicle](https://www.austinchronicle.com/search/keywords:Shana+Merlin/)).
