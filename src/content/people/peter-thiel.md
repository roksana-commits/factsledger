## Overview

Peter Andreas Thiel is an American entrepreneur and venture capitalist, born October 11, 1967, in Frankfurt am Main, West Germany, and raised from around age one in the United States ([Britannica Money](https://www.britannica.com/money/Peter-Thiel)). He studied philosophy at Stanford University, graduating in 1989 and founding the campus publication The Stanford Review, before earning a law degree from Stanford Law School in 1992 ([Britannica Money](https://www.britannica.com/money/Peter-Thiel)). He went on to co-found the payments company that became PayPal, co-found the data-analytics firm Palantir Technologies, co-found the venture capital firm Founders Fund, and become an early investor in Facebook ([Britannica Money](https://www.britannica.com/money/Peter-Thiel)). On December 11, 2015, Thiel was named among the group of private investors — including [Elon Musk](/people/elon-musk/) and Sam Altman — whose pledges backed the launch of [OpenAI](/organizations/openai/) ([MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2020/02/17/844721/ai-openai-moonshot-elon-musk-sam-altman-greg-brockman-messy-secretive-reality/)).

## PayPal

In 1998, Thiel co-founded Confinity, a company originally built around payments between Palm Pilot handheld devices. In 1999, Confinity merged with Elon Musk's online-banking startup X.com; the combined company was later renamed PayPal, with Thiel serving as chief executive and chairman and describing its ambition as building "a new world currency" for internet money transfers. In 2002, eBay acquired PayPal for $1.5 billion, a deal that made Thiel a multimillionaire ([Britannica Money](https://www.britannica.com/money/Peter-Thiel)).

## Palantir and Founders Fund

In 2004, Thiel co-founded Palantir Technologies, a data-analytics company that has worked with government agencies including the CIA as well as commercial clients such as banks doing fraud detection; Palantir went public via a direct listing in 2020 ([Britannica Money](https://www.britannica.com/money/Peter-Thiel); [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/profile/peter-thiel/)). That same year, he became an early investor in Facebook ([Britannica Money](https://www.britannica.com/money/Peter-Thiel)) — a stake Forbes reports he has since mostly sold, alongside his departure from Meta's board ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/profile/peter-thiel/)). In 2005, Thiel co-founded the venture capital firm Founders Fund, whose portfolio has included Airbnb, Lyft, and SpaceX ([Britannica Money](https://www.britannica.com/money/Peter-Thiel)); Forbes attributes part of Thiel's current wealth to Founders Fund's stakes in companies including Stripe and SpaceX ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/profile/peter-thiel/)).

## OpenAI's founding

On December 11, 2015, OpenAI was announced as a nonprofit artificial intelligence research organization backed by pledges totaling more than $1 billion from a group of private investors ([MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2020/02/17/844721/ai-openai-moonshot-elon-musk-sam-altman-greg-brockman-messy-secretive-reality/); [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/ericmack/2015/12/11/elon-musk-sam-altman-peter-thiel-others-launch-open-a-i-with-1-billion-donation/)). Thiel is named in each of the four contemporaneous or later accounts read for this profile as part of that investor group. MIT Technology Review's account states that "the venture would start with $1 billion from private investors, including Musk, Altman, and PayPal cofounder Peter Thiel" — grouping Thiel with the financial backers, distinct from its description of Greg Brockman as chief technology officer and Ilya Sutskever as research director ([MIT Technology Review](https://www.technologyreview.com/2020/02/17/844721/ai-openai-moonshot-elon-musk-sam-altman-greg-brockman-messy-secretive-reality/)). GeekWire similarly reported that Musk "is joined by his old PayPal co-founder Peter Thiel and early PayPal board member Reid Hoffman in backing the project," while a separate Forbes article listed Thiel among those "donating to support OpenAI" ([GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2015/non-profit-ai-research-facility-backed-by-elon-musk-peter-thiel-created-to-protect-against-corperate-ai/); [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/theopriestley/2015/12/11/elon-musk-and-peter-thiel-launch-openai-a-non-profit-artificial-intelligence-research-company/)). None of the four sources read for this profile describes Thiel personally using the word "co-founder" or "founder" of OpenAI; each identifies him specifically as a financial backer or investor.

## Political and philanthropic activity

Thiel has funded high-profile litigation and political campaigns. Britannica reports that he secretly financed Hulk Hogan's invasion-of-privacy lawsuit against Gawker Media; the resulting judgment helped drive Gawker into bankruptcy, and the funding became public in 2016, the same year Thiel became a vocal public supporter of Donald Trump's presidential campaign ([Britannica Money](https://www.britannica.com/money/Peter-Thiel)).

Thiel later played a formative role in the political career of J.D. Vance. The two met when Vance, then a Yale Law School student, attended a lecture Thiel gave on American technological stagnation; Thiel subsequently employed Vance as a venture capitalist at his firm, Mithril Capital. In February 2021, Thiel brought Vance to his first in-person meeting with Donald Trump at Mar-a-Lago, and in 2022 Thiel contributed $15 million to Vance's U.S. Senate campaign in Ohio — described by CBS News as "a staggering amount from a single individual for a Senate race" — while also directing at least $200,000 to a super PAC supporting Vance through a group he controls, Per Aspera Policy ([CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/jd-vance-trump-vp-peter-thiel-billionaire/)).

## Wealth

Forbes's real-time billionaires index listed Thiel's net worth at $34.7 billion as of September 10, 2026, attributing it to his historical Facebook stake, his holdings through Founders Fund (including stakes in Stripe, valued at $159 billion as of February 2026, and SpaceX), and his stake in Palantir ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/profile/peter-thiel/)).
