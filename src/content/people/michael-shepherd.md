## Overview

J. Michael Shepherd is an American banking lawyer and executive who spent nearly two decades in leadership roles at Bank of the West and its parent organizations before moving into a series of corporate board positions. Before joining the bank, he was general counsel at Shawmut National Corp. and later at The Bank of New York Company, Inc., and earlier held U.S. government legal posts including Senior Deputy Comptroller of the Currency, Associate Counsel to the President of the United States, and Deputy Assistant Attorney General ([citybiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/454751/discover-financial-services-appoints-j-michael-shepherd-to-its-board/)). He holds an undergraduate degree from Stanford University and a law degree from the University of Michigan Law School ([Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco](https://www.frbsf.org/our-district/press/news-releases/2013/michael-shepherd-reappointed-federal-advisory-council/)).

## Bank of the West and BancWest Corporation

Shepherd joined Bank of the West in 2004 as Executive Vice President, General Counsel and Chief Administrative Officer ([SEC EDGAR](https://sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/36377/000095013405010923/f09343exv99.htm)), and was promoted to Senior Executive Vice President in May 2005 with expanded oversight of the bank's risk management and credit administration functions in addition to its legal and compliance activities ([SEC EDGAR](https://sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/36377/000095013405010923/f09343exv99.htm)). He became President and Chief Operating Officer in 2006, then President and Chief Executive Officer of Bank of the West in January 2008 ([Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco](https://www.frbsf.org/news-and-media/news-articles/press-releases/2011/10/shepherd-appointment-federal-advisory-council/); [citybiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/454751/discover-financial-services-appoints-j-michael-shepherd-to-its-board/)). In April 2009 he additionally became President and CEO of BancWest Corporation, the Hawaii-based holding company that also oversees First Hawaiian Bank ([Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco](https://www.frbsf.org/news-and-media/news-articles/press-releases/2011/10/shepherd-appointment-federal-advisory-council/)).

While leading Bank of the West, Shepherd represented the Federal Reserve's Twelfth District on the Federal Reserve Board's Federal Advisory Council, a body of one commercial-banking representative from each of the twelve Federal Reserve Districts that meets with the Board of Governors several times a year to discuss economic and banking conditions. He was appointed to the council in October 2011, succeeding Russell Goldsmith of City National Bank, and was reappointed in 2013 to a third one-year term beginning in January 2014 ([Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco](https://www.frbsf.org/news-and-media/news-articles/press-releases/2011/10/shepherd-appointment-federal-advisory-council/); [Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco](https://www.frbsf.org/our-district/press/news-releases/2013/michael-shepherd-reappointed-federal-advisory-council/)); he later served as the council's president ([Family Wealth Report](https://www.familywealthreport.com/article.php/Change-At-The-Top-Of-Bank-Of-The-West)). He also served on the FDIC's Advisory Committee on Economic Inclusion during this period ([Federal Reserve Bank of San Francisco](https://www.frbsf.org/our-district/press/news-releases/2013/michael-shepherd-reappointed-federal-advisory-council/)).

## Transition to BNP Paribas USA and later board roles

In June 2016, Shepherd stepped down as CEO of Bank of the West and became chairman of BNP Paribas USA, a new holding company BNP Paribas created for its U.S. subsidiaries, while remaining chairman of Bank of the West and BancWest Corporation; he was succeeded as [Bank of the West's president and CEO by Nandita Bakhshi](/people/nandita-bakhshi/) ([Family Wealth Report](https://www.familywealthreport.com/article.php/Change-At-The-Top-Of-Bank-Of-The-West)). He held the BNP Paribas USA chairmanship until February 2023, when BMO completed its acquisition of Bank of the West from BNP Paribas ([citybiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/454751/discover-financial-services-appoints-j-michael-shepherd-to-its-board/)).

Alongside his banking role, Shepherd was elected to the board of directors of CoreLogic on June 21, 2019, expanding that company's board from 11 to 12 directors ([SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/36047/000003604719000078/exhibit9916252019.htm)); he also served as a director of First Hawaiian, Inc. ([citybiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/454751/discover-financial-services-appoints-j-michael-shepherd-to-its-board/)). On August 14, 2023, he joined the board of directors of Discover Financial Services, serving on its Risk Oversight Committee; Discover's board chair Tom Maheras cited his "significant expertise in financial services" and "valuable background in public affairs and regulatory matters" ([citybiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/454751/discover-financial-services-appoints-j-michael-shepherd-to-its-board/)). He has also served as a director of Pacific Mutual Holding Company ([citybiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/454751/discover-financial-services-appoints-j-michael-shepherd-to-its-board/)).
