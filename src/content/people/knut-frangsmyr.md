## Overview

Knut Frängsmyr is a Swedish executive and lawyer by training, with law degrees from Uppsala University
and Stockholm University. He spent roughly eleven years at the fintech company Klarna, moving through
Chief Legal Officer, Chief Financial Officer and Chief Technology Officer roles before becoming Chief
Operating Officer and Deputy CEO
([Realtid](https://www.realtid.se/bank-fintech/knut-frangsmyr-blir-ny-vd-pa-avanza/)). He is best known
publicly for an unusually short tenure as CEO of the Swedish online broker Avanza in late 2023.

## Klarna to Avanza

On 6 September 2023, Avanza announced Frängsmyr — then Klarna's COO and Deputy CEO — as its incoming
CEO, succeeding Rikard Josefson
([Breakit](https://www.breakit.se/artikel/37535/han-blir-ny-vd-for-avanza);
[Avanza press release](https://investors.avanza.se/en/media/press/2023/knut-frangsmyr-appointed-new-ceo-of-avanza/)).
He formally took over the role on 6 November 2023.

## A one-week tenure and its fallout

Roughly a week after taking over, Frängsmyr left the CEO role
([Breakit](https://www.breakit.se/artikel/38284/knut-frangsmyr-lamnar-vd-rollen-pa-avanza-efter-en-vecka)).
According to Börsvärlden, Avanza withdrew its internal approval of him as a suitable executive after
communication with Sweden's Financial Supervisory Authority (Finansinspektionen) about fines connected to
renovation work on his private home
([Börsvärlden](https://borsvarlden.com/artiklar/sven-hagstromer-far-kritik-av-di-och-aktiespararna-efter-vd-fiasko-i-avanza)).
Neither the amount nor the specific nature of those fines is disclosed in that reporting.

The episode drew public criticism of Avanza's board. Börsvärlden reports that Dagens Industri's editorial
page called it "the result of multiple parties' failures" and argued that board chairman Sven Hagströmer
should resign, noting the board had mishandled two of its three most recent CEO recruitments; it also
reports that Aktiespararna's chief legal officer, Sverre Linton, said the rapid departure "damages trust
in Avanza's board"
([Börsvärlden](https://borsvarlden.com/artiklar/sven-hagstromer-far-kritik-av-di-och-aktiespararna-efter-vd-fiasko-i-avanza)).

## After Avanza

Frängsmyr went on to become CEO of Real Alliance, a holding company, in 2024, and in 2025 joined Epidemic
Sound — an EQT-owned music-licensing company valued at more than SEK 12 billion — as Chief of Music
([EFN](https://efn.se/avanzas-tidigare-vd-knut-frangsmyr-blir-chef-for-eqt-bolag)).

## What this profile does not claim

This profile does not state what the underlying renovation-related fines were for, their amount, or
whether Frängsmyr disputed them — no source read for this profile discloses those details, only that
Finansinspektionen's communication about them was the reason Avanza gave internally for withdrawing its
approval of him.
