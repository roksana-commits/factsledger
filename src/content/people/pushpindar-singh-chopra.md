## Overview

Pushpindar Singh Chopra (1943-2021) was an Indian aviation historian, journalist and publisher. In 1974 he founded *Vayu Aerospace & Defence Review*, a magazine covering Indian aviation and defence affairs, and went on to write a multi-volume history of the Indian Air Force known as the "Himalayan Eagles" trilogy ([The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/musings/encyclopaedia-of-indian-aviation-258447)).

## Aviation history and journalism

Through *Vayu*, Chopra built a reputation as a documentarian of Indian military aviation history. In 1985, drawing on his own research, he publicly disputed a claim made by the Pakistan Air Force about the 1965 India-Pakistan war ([The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/musings/encyclopaedia-of-indian-aviation-258447)).

## Later work and death

Beyond aviation, Chopra founded *Nishaan Nagaara*, a magazine devoted to Sikh heritage ([The Sikh Foundation International](https://www.sikhfoundation.org/he-left-his-nishaan/)). He died in 2021, and his death and career were noted in the aviation trade press independent of his own publication ([ProQuest-indexed obituary](https://www.proquest.com/openview/5986b4c0c06821da74042c1ce96df236)).
