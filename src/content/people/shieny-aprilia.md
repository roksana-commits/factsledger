## Overview

Shieny Aprilia is an Indonesian entrepreneur and one of the co-founders of [Agate](/organizations/agate-game-company/), a video game developer headquartered in Bandung, West Java, founded on April 1, 2009. Recalling the company's origins, she told The Jakarta Post: "We built this company because all the cofounders wanted to work in the gaming industry, but at that time, there were no game companies in Indonesia that could hire all of us" ([The Jakarta Post](https://www.thejakartapost.com/business/2024/08/19/executive-column-agate-gears-up-to-capture-local-global-gaming-market.html)).

## Career at Agate

According to Hybrid.co.id, Aprilia began at Agate as a programmer before moving into management, and most recently held an operations-leadership role before her 2022 promotion ([Hybrid.co.id](https://hybrid.co.id/post/ceo-agate/)). A 2022 interview published by the digital agency Suitmedia's The Spectrum Talks describes a more detailed internal path — game programmer, then finance manager, then HR manager, before becoming chief operating officer — and states that in 2014 she received the "Indonesia Young Woman Future Business Leaders" award from SWA Magazine ([The Spectrum Talks/Suitmedia](https://medium.com/the-spectrum-talks/shieny-aprilia-co-founder-coo-of-agate-international-c897fa53ee0f)); because this is a company-adjacent interview series rather than independent newsroom reporting, these specific details are treated as provisional (see "What this profile does not claim").

## Becoming chief executive

On June 1, 2022, Aprilia was promoted from chief operating officer to chief executive of Agate, succeeding co-founder [Arief Widhiyasa](/people/arief-widhiyasa/), who became Chairman. Merdeka.com reported that her appointment made her one of a small group of women leading major Indonesian companies, citing World Bank data that put women's share of Indonesian CEO positions at roughly 2% as of 2020; Aprilia said the company would expand into metaverse development services while continuing its game-development work ([Merdeka.com](https://www.merdeka.com/teknologi/agate-umumkan-shieny-aprilia-jadi-ceo-baru-agate.html)). Widhiyasa was quoted saying: "Shieny bersama saya selalu ada di garda terdepan" (Shieny has consistently been at the forefront alongside me) ([Merdeka.com](https://www.merdeka.com/teknologi/agate-umumkan-shieny-aprilia-jadi-ceo-baru-agate.html)). Hybrid.co.id reported that she framed her strategic focus around integrating Agate's game-development expertise with metaverse solutions, and expressed a goal of Indonesia becoming a source of gaming talent and ideas rather than only a consumer market ([Hybrid.co.id](https://hybrid.co.id/post/ceo-agate/)).

## As chief executive

By August 2024, Aprilia remained chief executive as Agate employed about 200 people. In an interview with The Jakarta Post, she said demand for gaming-industry careers in Indonesia would keep growing "as more young people in Indonesia play video games," and that the company wanted "to continue thriving, creating opportunity for them to work in the gaming industry." The interview also touched on fundraising and expansion plans and her views on AI's role in the gaming industry's future ([The Jakarta Post](https://www.thejakartapost.com/business/2024/08/19/executive-column-agate-gears-up-to-capture-local-global-gaming-market.html)).

It is not to be confused with the unrelated French tabletop role-playing game publisher "Studio Agate" — this profile concerns only a leader of the Indonesian, Bandung-based video game developer Agate.
