## Overview

Mohit Aron is an Indian-American computer scientist and entrepreneur working in distributed systems and
enterprise data management. He co-founded Nutanix and founded Cohesity, serving as the latter's chief executive
until 2022.

## Early life and education

Aron grew up in Chandigarh, described by Forbes as India's "most modern and affluent city, 250 kilometers north of
Delhi", built after the 1947 partition by Indian, American and European architects. He studied at the Indian
Institutes of Technology in Delhi before taking a doctorate in computer science at Rice University in Houston
([Forbes, 10 December 2020](https://www.forbes.com/sites/richkarlgaard/2020/12/10/how-mohit-arons-indian-hometown-inspired-his-unicorn-company/)).

Rice University's engineering school records that he "graduated with a Ph.D. in computer science from Rice
University with a focus on distributed systems"
([Rice University](https://engineering.rice.edu/mohit-aron)).

## Career

### Google and Aster Data

Aron joined Google in 2003, where he worked as a lead engineer on the company's file system technology
([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/richkarlgaard/2020/12/10/how-mohit-arons-indian-hometown-inspired-his-unicorn-company/)).
Business Insider quotes him on the scale of that period: "When I started, there were 600 employee at Google. When
I left, Google was 3,000 employees"
([Business Insider via Inc.](https://www.inc.com/business-insider/cohesity-founder-mohit-aron.html)).

He was subsequently an early employee at Aster Data Systems, which Teradata acquired in 2011 for $263 million
([Business Insider via Inc.](https://www.inc.com/business-insider/cohesity-founder-mohit-aron.html)).

### Nutanix

In 2009 Aron co-founded Nutanix, serving as its chief technology officer
([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/richkarlgaard/2020/12/10/how-mohit-arons-indian-hometown-inspired-his-unicorn-company/)).
Business Insider reported that he left the company before its initial public offering while remaining a
significant shareholder.

### Cohesity

Aron founded Cohesity in 2013. In December 2020 Forbes reported the company's valuation at $2.5 billion in its
then-latest funding round, with estimated revenues of $300 million.

On 2 August 2022, Blocks & Files reported that Aron stepped down as chief executive, with Sanjay Poonen, formerly
chief operating officer at VMware, taking over. Aron moved to the roles of chief technology officer and chief
product officer, saying: "As we scale, it is important to me to have a tighter focus on where I spend my time to
have the greatest impact"
([Blocks & Files](https://www.blocksandfiles.com/data-management/2022/08/02/cohesity-founder-and-ceo-mohit-aron-steps-down-sanjay-poonen-takes-over/1603336)).

On 14 May 2024, Blocks & Files reported a further change: Aron moved from chief strategy and technology officer to
**founder emeritus**, with Cohesity stating that he would "offer strategic and architectural input, participate in
external speaking engagements, and represent the company in customer visits". Craig Martell, previously chief
digital and AI officer at the United States Department of Defense, joined as chief technology officer
([Blocks & Files](https://blocksandfiles.com/2024/05/14/mohit-aron-cohesitys-founder-steps-back-as-new-cto-climbs-aboard/)).

## Recognition

Rice University awarded Aron its Outstanding Engineering Alumni Award in 2018
([Rice University](https://engineering.rice.edu/mohit-aron)).
