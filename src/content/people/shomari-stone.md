## Overview

Shomari Stone is an American broadcast journalist who has worked in major television news markets in Miami and Washington, D.C. Trade reporting documents his move from WRC-TV to CNBC in 2022 and his subsequent appointment at WTTG in Washington in 2023 ([Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/tvnewser/shomari-stone-joins-cnbc-as-reporter-for-shepard-smiths-newscast/) [Next TV](https://www.nexttv.com/news/shomari-stone-joins-wttg-washington-as-reporter-and-anchor)).

## Career

Stone's earlier reporting work included coverage of the 2005 federal air-marshals shooting at Miami International Airport. Later, he spent more than a decade in the Washington television market before becoming a CNBC correspondent for The News with Shepard Smith ([Adweek](https://www.adweek.com/tvnewser/shomari-stone-joins-cnbc-as-reporter-for-shepard-smiths-newscast/)). Next TV reported in April 2023 that he would join WTTG as a reporter and anchor, marking a return to local Washington broadcasting ([Next TV](https://www.nexttv.com/news/shomari-stone-joins-wttg-washington-as-reporter-and-anchor)).

## Recognition and public record

The National Capital Chesapeake Bay chapter of the National Academy of Television Arts and Sciences included Stone in its 2022 Capital Emmy nomination coverage ([Capital Emmy Awards](https://www.capitalemmys.tv/post/64th-capital-emmy-nominations-daytime-newscast-larger-markets-1-49)). His professional identity is also recorded in the U.S. Congressional Directory's press-galleries listing for the 117th Congress ([U.S. Government Publishing Office](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CDIR-2022-10-26/html/CPRT-2022-10-26-PRESSGALLERIES-5.htm)).
