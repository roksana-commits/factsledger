## Overview

Elaine Tierney is an American lightweight rower from West Chester, Pennsylvania, who rowed for Temple
University's varsity program for five years before going on to win medals at two consecutive World Rowing
Championships. A separate, unrelated public figure — a design historian at London's V&A Research Institute
— shares this name; this profile is specifically about the rower.

## From Temple to the U.S. national team

At Temple, Tierney was named the rowing program's Newcomer of the Year in 2018, its Women's Rowing Athlete
of the Year in 2020, raced on the U.S. Rowing U23 National Team in 2021, and closed her collegiate career
with a Second Team All-American Athletic Conference selection in 2022
([VISTA.Today](https://vista.today/2022/07/elaine-tierney-rowing-team/)). That year, racing for Vesper Boat
Club, she and partner Solveig Imsdahl won the lightweight women's pair at the USRowing Summer National
Championships — a result Tierney described as "a hard fought and well worked for race," adding that "we
kept ourselves internal and focused on our strokes like we do in practice"
([VISTA.Today](https://vista.today/2022/07/elaine-tierney-rowing-team/)). The win secured her a place on
the U.S. Senior National Team and a berth at that year's World Rowing Championships.

## World Championship medals

Tierney and Imsdahl went on to win the silver medal in the lightweight women's coxless pair at the 2022
World Rowing Championships in Račice, Czech Republic, finishing second in the A final. The following year,
Tierney won a bronze medal in the lightweight women's pair at the 2023 World Rowing Championships in
Belgrade, Serbia, finishing third in the final
([World Rowing](https://worldrowing.com/athlete/55455)).
