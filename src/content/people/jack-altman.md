## Overview

Jack Altman is an American technology entrepreneur and venture capital investor, best known as the co-founder and former chief executive of the HR and performance-management software company Lattice, and, since February 2026, as a general partner at the venture capital firm Benchmark ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/17/jack-altman-joins-benchmark-as-gp/)). He is the younger brother of [Sam Altman](/people/sam-altman/), chief executive of OpenAI, and is not the same person despite the shared surname ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/17/jack-altman-joins-benchmark-as-gp/); [Business Insider, via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/jack-altman-second-altman-brother-110001101.html)).

## Lattice (2015–2023)

Before founding Lattice, Altman worked as vice president of business development at the social-commerce company Teespring. In 2015 he co-founded Lattice with Eric Koslow; the company went through the [Y Combinator](/organizations/y-combinator/) startup accelerator program in 2016 ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/04/26/lattice-raises-another-15m-to-improve-performance-reviews)). By April 2019, Lattice had raised a $15 million Series B round led by Shasta Ventures and reported roughly 1,200 customers and 60 employees, including clients such as Reddit, Slack, Coinbase, and Glossier ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/04/26/lattice-raises-another-15m-to-improve-performance-reviews)). Altman described the product's origin in frustration with static, rarely-revisited quarterly goal-setting exercises: "We realized that with quarterly goal settings, OKRs, we would write them up and get the leadership together and then they would sit on a shelf and nothing would happen" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2019/04/26/lattice-raises-another-15m-to-improve-performance-reviews)).

The company continued to grow through the pandemic-era funding boom, raising $175 million in a Series F round in January 2022 that tripled its valuation to $3 billion and brought its customer base to roughly 3,550 businesses ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2022/01/19/lattice-raises-175m-more-at-a-3b-valuation-for-its-people-management-platform/)). Conditions tightened afterward: in January 2023, Lattice laid off 105 employees, mostly in recruiting and sales roles, and co-founder Eric Koslow left the company that year to pursue video content creation ([Business Insider, via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/jack-altman-second-altman-brother-110001101.html)).

In December 2023, after eight years running the company, Altman announced he would step down as CEO to become executive chairman, with Sarah Franklin — a 15-year Salesforce executive who had run its marketing and led its Platform and Trailhead businesses — named as his successor ([Business Insider, via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/jack-altman-second-altman-brother-110001101.html); [Lattice](https://lattice.com/blog/the-next-chapter)). Franklin formally became CEO on January 2, 2024. Altman said he would "partner with her and the rest of the leadership team closely to ensure a smooth and thoughtful handoff," adding, "I'm not going anywhere; I'll show up wherever I'm invited and try hard to stay out of the way where I'm not needed" ([Lattice](https://lattice.com/blog/the-next-chapter)). He explained the decision as a matter of temperament rather than company performance: "I love early stage — it's always what I've loved — and I happened to add another love," describing a "first inkling" of wanting to move on that emerged as the company scaled rapidly in 2021 ([Business Insider, via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/jack-altman-second-altman-brother-110001101.html)).

## Alt Capital and Benchmark (2024–2026)

After stepping back from day-to-day leadership at Lattice, Altman founded the venture capital firm Alt Capital, raising a $150 million Fund I in early 2024 and a $274 million Fund II in September 2025. The firm has backed roughly 52 companies, including Rippling, Antares Nuclear, and CompLabs ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/17/jack-altman-joins-benchmark-as-gp/)). On February 17, 2026, Altman joined Benchmark as a general partner, bringing his Alt Capital teammates with him — an unusual structural move for the traditionally flat-organized firm — while planning to retain his board seats at Alt Capital's portfolio companies. He had previously called his time building Alt Capital "the most rewarding" experience of his career, citing the appeal of "new ideas and being part of a team with a mission" ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/17/jack-altman-joins-benchmark-as-gp/)).

## Podcast

Altman hosts "Uncapped with Jack Altman," a weekly interview podcast produced under Alt Capital that launched in 2025, describing it as "conversations with people I admire about things I'm genuinely interested in." Its guests have included prominent technology and venture-capital figures such as Shopify's Tobi Lütke, 8VC's Joe Lonsdale, and DoorDash's Tony Xu ([Apple Podcasts](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/uncapped-with-jack-altman/id1801867202)).
