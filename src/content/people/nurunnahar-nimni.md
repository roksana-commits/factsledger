## Overview

Nurunnahar Nimni, from Rangpur, began mountaineering during a university field trip to Chandranath Hill in 2006 and completed formal training at the Himalayan Mountaineering Institute in Darjeeling in 2022. She studied geology at the University of Dhaka and works as a Principal Officer in the General Banking Division of Pubali Bank PLC, which sponsored her Himlung Himal and Everest expeditions ([The Business Standard](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/bangladeshi-female-mountaineer-nurunnahar-nimni-scales-mount-everest-1449751)).

## Everest summit

Departing Dhaka on April 11, 2026, Nimni reached Mount Everest's summit at 5:24 a.m. Nepal time on May 27, 2026, accompanied by Sherpa partners Dawa Nupu Sherpa and Lakpa Thinduk Sherpa. She became the third Bangladeshi woman to summit Everest — the first successful ascent by a Bangladeshi woman in 14 years, following Nishat Majumdar and Wasfia Nazreen, who both summited in 2012 ([The Business Standard](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/bangladeshi-female-mountaineer-nurunnahar-nimni-scales-mount-everest-1449751)). On June 15, 2026, Pubali Bank honored her at a reception titled "Honouring the Summit Conqueror," where CEO Mohammad Ali and other bank officials presented her with a commemorative crest and appreciation letter, and a documentary of her climb was screened ([Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/business/banks/412710/pubali-bank-honours-everest-conqueror-nurunnahar)).
