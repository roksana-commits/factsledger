## Overview

Fawn Johnson is a Washington, D.C.-based journalist and policy analyst whose documented work concerns Congress, public policy and education reporting. Her professional recognition includes First Prize in the Education Writers Association's 2011 National Awards for Education Reporting ([Education Writers Association](https://ewa.org/members-news/awards/2011-winners-of-the-national-awards-for-education-reporting)).

## Policy and education reporting

Johnson's reporting has been connected with education policy and the legislative process. The Washington Post cited her work in a 2015 discussion of congressional strategy and political leadership ([The Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/news/powerpost/wp/2015/06/15/the-daily-202-do-not-underestimate-jeb-bush/)). A U.S. Government Publishing Office Congressional Print also cites her reporting in its discussion of legislative procedure ([U.S. Government Publishing Office](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CPRT-113SPRT89394/html/CPRT-113SPRT89394.htm)).

## Public-affairs work

C-SPAN's catalogue records Johnson's appearances in public-affairs programming, and PBS Washington Week lists her as a policy analyst in an October 2014 programme ([C-SPAN](https://www.c-span.org/person/fawn-johnson/1010446/) [PBS Washington Week](https://www.pbs.org/weta/washingtonweek/video/2014/10/north-korea-blusters-jobs-the-economy-and-political-wrap)). These records support a public professional profile in policy journalism, but they do not by themselves establish every later employment detail.
