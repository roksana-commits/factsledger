## Overview

Süleyman Dilbirliği, born in 1926, was a non-commissioned officer in the Turkish Army who
served in the Korean War, arriving in Korea with Turkish forces under United Nations
command on 16 October 1950 after earlier postings in Kahramanmaraş and İskenderun
([Hürriyet](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/kelebek/televizyon/suleyman-astsubay-kimdir-suleyman-dilbirliginin-gercek-hikayesi-40967608)).

## Finding Ayla

During a harsh Korean winter, Dilbirliği found an abandoned girl, about four or five years
old, in freezing conditions and brought her into his unit. He named her Ayla, for her
"round, moon-like face"; her birth name was Kim Eunja. He cared for her for about a year
before his unit returned to Turkey. Korean law at the time prevented him from taking her
with him, and she was placed in a school in Suwon established by Turkish soldiers
([Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/asia-pacific/turkish-foster-father-of-korean-war-orphan-remembered/1333413);
[Hürriyet](https://www.hurriyet.com.tr/kelebek/televizyon/suleyman-astsubay-kimdir-suleyman-dilbirliginin-gercek-hikayesi-40967608)).

## Reunion and later life

The two lost contact for sixty years. In 2010, around the 60th anniversary of the Korean
War, South Korean officials reunited Dilbirliği and Ayla in Seoul's Ankara Park. They
corresponded afterward, and met in person once more in 2012 during Ayla's visit to Turkey.
Dilbirliği died in 2017 at age 91. The following year, Turkey's Consulate General in Chicago
and the Gene Siskel Film Center held a screening of "Ayla: The Daughter of War," a film
depicting his story, to mark the first anniversary of his death
([Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/asia-pacific/turkish-foster-father-of-korean-war-orphan-remembered/1333413);
[Anadolu Agency](https://www.aa.com.tr/en/turkey/turkish-foster-father-of-korean-war-orphan-dies-at-91/995820)).
