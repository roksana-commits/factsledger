## Overview

Johan Raja Lawak, born Mohd Yazid Lim bin Mohamad Aziz on 13 June 1984, is a Malaysian comedian, actor and
radio presenter. He placed third in the first season of the comedy competition Raja Lawak in 2007 and went
on to host the show from its second season onward, later forming the comedy duo "Jozan" with Zizan Razak,
which finished runner-up on Maharaja Lawak. He has spent more than a decade as a presenter on Hot FM's
morning show "Bekpes Hot."

## An on-set injury and its aftermath

In 2010, Johan sustained facial injuries during a film production for Juara Filem Production Sdn Bhd,
directed by Mohd Javed Mohd Nizar. Malaysia's High Court later ruled in 2014 that the director and
production company must pay Johan and his wife, Rozlinawaty Ismail, a combined RM170,100 in general
damages, trauma compensation and costs. The presiding judge noted that as an actor Johan's income depended
on his appearance, and that the permanent scarring had affected his career
([mStar](https://www.mstar.com.my/lokal/semasa/2014/07/07/johan-raja-lawak)). The following year, in 2011,
he had co-hosted the TV3 comedy series "Edisi Khas," which passed three million viewers and earned a 2011
Screen Award nomination ([mStar](https://www.mstar.com.my/spotlight/hiburan/2011/10/15/edisi-khas-johan-raja-lawak-cecah-3-juta-penonton)).

## From comedy to entrepreneurship

By the 2020s, Johan had shifted much of his public focus toward business. In October 2023 he opened the
restaurant "Itik ke Ayam?" in Taman Melawati, and by 2025 was running a smoked duck and chicken
vending-machine business with Datuk Fazley Yaakob, targeting 100 machines in its first year. His wife —
referred to in this coverage as Ozlynn — manages product quality and customer feedback for the venture,
while he continues his long-running Hot FM morning show
([Lady Bosz](https://ladybosz.com/2025/04/04/johan-seronok-jadi-usahawan/)).
