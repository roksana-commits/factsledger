## Overview

Adam Stafford is a Scottish musician and filmmaker, born in Sunderland in 1982 and based in
Falkirk. He fronted the band Y'all Is Fantasy Island from 2005 to 2011 before releasing solo
albums, including "Build a Harbour Immediately" (2011, produced by Paul Savage at Chem19 Studios)
and "Fire Behind the Curtain" (2018), the latter reviewed favourably by The Skinny
([The Skinny](https://theskinny.co.uk/music/reviews/albums/adam-stafford-fire-behind-the-curtain)).
He also runs his own label, Wise Blood Industries.

## Directing work

Alongside music, Stafford has directed short films and music videos. His video for The Twilight
Sad's "Seven Years of Letters" won Best Video at the 2011 Scottish New Music Awards. His short film
"The Shutdown" went on to win seven international awards, and he later directed "No Hope for Men
Below," about a 1923 mining disaster
([Louder Than War](https://louderthanwar.com/new-artist-day-adam-stafford/)).
