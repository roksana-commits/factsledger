## Overview

Ioannis Vardoulakis (22 March 1949 – 19 September 2009) was a Greek civil engineer and geomechanicist. Born in
Chania, Crete, he was elected Professor of Mechanics at the National Technical University of Athens in 1990,
having previously held a professorship in geomechanics at the University of Minnesota
([ISSMGE](https://www.issmge.org/news/ioannis-vardoulakis-22031949--19092009)).

His work concerned how granular materials such as sand deform and fail — in particular the formation of shear
bands, narrow zones in which deformation concentrates before a soil mass gives way.

## Education

Vardoulakis took his diploma in civil engineering at the National Technical University of Athens in 1972 and
completed a doctorate in soil mechanics at the University of Karlsruhe in 1977, where he then worked as a research
fellow ([ISSMGE](https://www.issmge.org/news/ioannis-vardoulakis-22031949--19092009)).

## Research

The ISSMGE records his research as spanning stress instability, bifurcation theory, strain concentration, wave
propagation, micromechanics, elasticity, plasticity, fracture and thermomechanics, together with work on
liquefaction and slope-stability analysis.

Two contributions recur in the independent record. The first is the 1978 paper "Formation of shear bands in sand
bodies as a bifurcation problem", written with Goldscheider and Gudehus, which is identified as seminal in the
memorial published in *Granular Matter*
([Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10035-010-0231-6)). The second is the
**Arthur–Vardoulakis equation**, which came out of his biaxial testing work and is used to determine shear-band
orientation and to calibrate the out-of-plane shear modulus
([ALERT Geomaterials](https://alertgeomaterials.eu/data/school/2019/Day1/Introduction.pdf)).

ALERT Geomaterials also records his books *Bifurcation Analysis in Geomechanics* (1995, with Jean Sulem) and
*Cosserat Continuum Mechanics with Applications to Granular Media* (2018), and his earlier 1976 paper
"Equilibrium theory of shear bands in plastic bodies".

The ISSMGE additionally notes a study of the Parthenon Marbles among his work on the stability and mechanical
behaviour of ancient monuments.

## Awards

The ISSMGE obituary records two awards: the **Bishop Award** for geotechnical research from the Institution of
Civil Engineers in the United Kingdom, and a medal for **Distinguished Research in Geomechanics** from the
Japanese Geotechnical Association.

## Teaching

ALERT Geomaterials describes him as "a devoted and inspiring teacher" and records that he organised several
doctoral schools, including ALERT sessions in 2004 and 2006, LARAM in 2008 and CISM in 2003.

## Recognition after his death

Vardoulakis died on 19 September 2009 from brain damage following an accidental fall
([ISSMGE](https://www.issmge.org/news/ioannis-vardoulakis-22031949--19092009)).

In 2011, Gerd Gudehus — his co-author on the 1978 shear-band paper — published an "In memoriam" in *Granular
Matter* ([Springer](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10035-010-0231-6)). In October 2019, the 30th ALERT
Doctoral School was dedicated to his legacy
([ALERT Geomaterials](https://alertgeomaterials.eu/data/school/2019/Day1/Introduction.pdf)).
