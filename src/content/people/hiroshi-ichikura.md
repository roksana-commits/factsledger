## Overview

Hiroshi Ichikura is a Japanese advertising copywriter and creative director, born in Gunma
Prefecture in 1955. After graduating from Tsukuba University in 1978, he worked in Suntory's
advertising division and later at Nakahata Advertising before founding his own agency, Ichikura
Advertising Production, in 1990. Over his career he has written copy for major Japanese brands
including Suntory Malt, Panasonic, Sony's Walkman, Family Mart, Recruit and Sekisui House. In 2023,
he was inducted into the Tokyo Copywriters Club (TCC) Hall of Fame
([AdverTimes](https://www.advertimes.com)).
