## Overview

Ignacio Dülger is a Chilean lawyer, trained at Universidad de los Andes, whose political career
runs through José Antonio Kast's Republican Party (Partido Republicano). He began as youth-affairs
coordinator for the party's predecessor movement, Acción Republicana, in April 2018, and served as
territorial chief for Kast's 2021 presidential campaign
([La Tercera](https://www.latercera.com/la-tercera-pm/noticia/ignacio-dulger-la-apuesta-republicana-para-la-alcaldia-de-penalolen/)).
In 2023 he ran unsuccessfully as a Republican candidate for Chile's Consejo Constitucional
(Constitutional Council) in the Metropolitan Region
([El Líbero](https://www.ellibero.cl/en-cuatro-preguntas-ignacio-dulger)).

## From regional councilor to mayoral candidate

Dülger currently holds elected office as a regional councilor (Consejero Regional, or CORE) for the
Santiago Metropolitan Region. In June 2025, in that capacity, he publicly criticized Metropolitan
Region Governor Claudio Orrego, alleging more than CLP 3 billion in unjustified direct-contracting
spending by the regional government
([24 Horas](https://www.24horas.cl/nacional/consejero-dulger-por-gobernador-orrego)). In 2026, the
Republican Party named him its candidate for mayor of Peñalolén in that October's municipal
elections ([La Tercera](https://www.latercera.com/la-tercera-pm/noticia/ignacio-dulger-la-apuesta-republicana-para-la-alcaldia-de-penalolen/)).

## What this profile does not claim

The allegation of unjustified spending by Governor Orrego's administration is reported here as
Dülger's own public statement, not as an independently confirmed finding — none of the sources
