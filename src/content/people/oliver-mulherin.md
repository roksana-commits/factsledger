## Overview

Oliver Mulherin is an Australian software engineer who has worked at the IOTA Foundation, Broadwing, SPARK Neuro, and Meta, and who is married to OpenAI chief executive [Sam Altman](/people/sam-altman/) ([Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/sam-altman-partner-oliver-mulherin-open-ai-1922107); [NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/sam-altman-oliver-mulherin-ollie-wedding-married-partner-boyfriend-rcna133512)).

## Education and early career

Mulherin studied computer science at the University of Melbourne, graduating in 2016, and worked on AI projects — including with the IOTA Foundation — while still a student ([Newsweek](https://www.newsweek.com/sam-altman-partner-oliver-mulherin-open-ai-1922107); [SheThePeople](https://www.shethepeople.tv/us/openai-ceo-sam-altman-oliver-mulherin-marriage-2378409)). After graduating, he worked as a software engineer at Broadwing and then at SPARK Neuro, a startup focused on dementia detection, before relocating from Sydney to San Francisco. He subsequently worked at Meta, reportedly from around 2020 to 2022 ([SheThePeople](https://www.shethepeople.tv/us/openai-ceo-sam-altman-oliver-mulherin-marriage-2378409)).

## Relationship and family

Mulherin and Sam Altman were first seen publicly together at a White House state dinner hosted by President Joe Biden for Indian Prime Minister Narendra Modi ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/sam-altman-oliver-mulherin-ollie-wedding-married-partner-boyfriend-rcna133512)). The couple married in a private beach ceremony in Hawaii on January 11, 2024 ([NBC News](https://www.nbcnews.com/tech/tech-news/sam-altman-oliver-mulherin-ollie-wedding-married-partner-boyfriend-rcna133512); [SheThePeople](https://www.shethepeople.tv/us/openai-ceo-sam-altman-oliver-mulherin-marriage-2378409)). On February 22, 2025, Altman announced on social media that he and Mulherin had had their first child, a son born via surrogacy who arrived early and spent time in the neonatal intensive care unit ([VnExpress International](https://e.vnexpress.net/news/tech/openai-ceo-sam-altman-reveals-birth-of-his-first-child-via-surrogacy-4853809.html)).
