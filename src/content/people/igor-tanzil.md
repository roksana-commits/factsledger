## Overview

Igor Tanzil is an Indonesian creative executive identified by the gaming trade press as a co-founder and Chief Creative Officer of [Confiction Labs](/organizations/confiction-labs/), a game studio that launched publicly in September 2023 under the name Mythic Protocol, and as a former chief marketing officer and chief creative officer of [Agate](/organizations/agate-game-company/), the Bandung video game developer ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/mythic-protocol-raises-65m-towards-blockchain-collaboration-and-cross-platform-ambitions/); [BeyondGames.biz](https://www.beyondgames.biz/41949/mythic-protocol-raises-6-5m-for-multi-platform-games-blockchain-and-decentralised-universal-meta/)).

## From advertising to game development

In an October 2023 interview, Tanzil described himself as "a writer – and former advertising copywriter," explaining that he saw consequence-driven storytelling as central to what makes writing enjoyable, and that he had initially been skeptical of web3 gaming, citing the play-to-earn title Axie Infinity as an example of short-term financial gains overshadowing game design ([BeyondGames.biz](https://www.beyondgames.biz/43705/shaping-the-future-of-web3-gaming-interview-with-igor-tanzil-co-founder-of-mythic-protocol/)). A later interview, published by the gaming trade outlet Games.gg in September 2024, has him giving a fuller account of that path: he said he initially studied filmmaking in the United States, discovered an aptitude for writing through a college copywriting class, and returned to Indonesia for personal reasons, after which he moved into game marketing before eventually co-founding a game studio ([Games.gg](https://games.gg/news/future-of-game-development/)).

## At Mythic Protocol and Confiction Labs

By September 2023, Tanzil had co-founded Mythic Protocol alongside [Arief Widhiyasa](/people/arief-widhiyasa/), Agate's former chief executive, when the company announced an oversubscribed $6.5 million seed round for its game "Riftstorm" and a blockchain-based asset system ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/mythic-protocol-raises-65m-towards-blockchain-collaboration-and-cross-platform-ambitions/)). On March 15, 2024, Tanzil wrote on the company's own blog that "our entire company is shifting from Mythic Protocol to Confiction Labs," tracing the venture's beginnings to 2021 and describing the rebrand as an effort to broaden the company's vision of "Collaborative Entertainment" beyond a single game universe ([Confiction Labs, via Medium](https://medium.com/confiction-labs/lets-talk-conviction-fe14167929ae)). As Confiction Labs' Chief Creative Officer, he told Games.gg in 2024 that "the ideal vision of Collaborative Entertainment is that anyone can have the agency to choose how they interact with an IP," describing a design philosophy meant to let players and creators shape a game's world and be rewarded for doing so ([Games.gg](https://games.gg/news/future-of-game-development/)).

## What this profile does not claim

No source read gives Tanzil's exact dates of employment at Agate, a specific date he left to co-found Mythic Protocol, or his date or place of birth. A professional-network aggregator, The Org, lists a series of more specific pre-Agate roles for him — at a company called "ShopKetti," at "Critical Forge," at "Batavian Studio," and as a freelance copywriter for the advertising agency DDB — but no independently reported source read corroborates those specific employers, so this profile does not state them as fact. Sources are not fully consistent on whether "co-founder" or a purely executive title best describes his role at Mythic Protocol/Confiction Labs; this profile follows PocketGamer.biz and BeyondGames.biz's 2023 identification of him as a co-founder while also noting his formal title of Chief Creative Officer. This profile does not cover any activity by Tanzil outside his roles at Agate and Mythic Protocol/Confiction Labs.
