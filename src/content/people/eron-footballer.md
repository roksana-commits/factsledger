## Overview

Eron, full name Eronildo dos Santos Rocha, is a Brazilian footballer born 16 July 1998 in Salvador, Bahia. A forward developed in the youth system of EC Vitória, he became known for his goal-scoring form during the 2023 Campeonato Brasileiro Série D season with Caxias ([Bahia Notícias](https://www.bahianoticias.com.br/esportes/noticia/67540-revelado-pelo-vitoria-eron-e-negociado-para-o-futebol-japones-apos-artilharia-da-serie-d)).

## 2023 Série D season

During the 2023 Série D season, Eron scored repeatedly for Caxias, including the goal that clinched the club's promotion in a match against Portuguesa-RJ, and finished the competition as its top scorer with 14 goals ([Bahia Notícias](https://www.bahianoticias.com.br/esportes/noticia/66489-autor-do-gol-do-acesso-do-caxias-eron-se-isola-como-maior-artilheiro-de-uma-edicao-da-serie-d); [Bahia Notícias](https://www.bahianoticias.com.br/esportes/noticia/65933-eron-faz-mais-dois-gols-assume-artilharia-da-serie-d-e-caxias-encaminha-classificacao)).

## Move to Japan

Following his scoring title, Eron transferred from Caxias to Vegalta Sendai of Japan's J2 League in December 2023 ([Bahia Notícias](https://www.bahianoticias.com.br/esportes/noticia/67540-revelado-pelo-vitoria-eron-e-negociado-para-o-futebol-japones-apos-artilharia-da-serie-d)).
