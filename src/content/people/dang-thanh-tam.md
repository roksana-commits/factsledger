## Overview

Dang Thanh Tam is a Vietnamese industrialist who chairs Kinh Bac City Development Holding Corporation (KBC), one of Vietnam's largest industrial real estate developers, and Saigontel, a telecommunications infrastructure company ([The Real Deal](https://therealdeal.com/international/2025/10/15/trump-orgs-vietnam-resort-highlights-ties-with-billionaire/)). Kinh Bac City is the parent company of Hung Yen Hospitality, the Vietnamese partner in the Trump Organization's licensed golf and residential project in Hung Yen province, [Trump International, Vietnam](/organizations/trump-international-vietnam/) ([Vietnam Investment Review](https://vir.com.vn/construction-begins-on-15-billion-trump-organization-golf-complex-in-hung-yen-128932.html)).

## Business career

Dang was reported to be 61 years old as of October 2025, and cross-referenced Vietnamese corporate records put his date of birth as April 15, 1964, in Ho Chi Minh City ([The Real Deal](https://therealdeal.com/international/2025/10/15/trump-orgs-vietnam-resort-highlights-ties-with-billionaire/); [CafeF](https://cafef.vn/du-lieu/ceo/ceo_00058/dang-thanh-tam.chn)). The Investor's business coverage identifies him as chairman of both Kinh Bac City and [Saigontel](/organizations/saigontel/) (Saigon Telecommunication & Technologies Corporation), and reports that he served a five-year term as a delegate to Vietnam's National Assembly from 2011 to 2016 ([The Investor](https://theinvestor.vn/famous-vietnamese-entrepreneurs-born-in-year-of-the-dragon-d8597.html)).

The Real Deal reported that Dang's business empire became overleveraged in the early 2010s, and that his fortunes rebounded significantly during Donald Trump's first presidential term, as the U.S.-China trade war pushed manufacturers — including Foxconn and LG — to shift production to Vietnam to avoid tariffs; Kinh Bac City's revenue rose 161% between 2017 and 2021 as Vietnam's exports to the United States roughly doubled over the same span ([The Real Deal](https://therealdeal.com/international/2025/10/15/trump-orgs-vietnam-resort-highlights-ties-with-billionaire/)).

## The Trump-licensed project in Hung Yen

Dang attended the May 21, 2025 groundbreaking ceremony for the $1.5 billion golf and residential complex that Kinh Bac subsidiary Hung Yen Hospitality is developing under license from the Trump Organization, saying that "the two partners share the same vision of transforming Hung Yen into a leading golf and lifestyle destination in Asia. With extensive experience in developing world-class golf courses, both sides are excited to realise this vision" ([Vietnam Investment Review](https://vir.com.vn/construction-begins-on-15-billion-trump-organization-golf-complex-in-hung-yen-128932.html)).

Kinh Bac City had already paid the Trump Organization $5 million in licensing fees for the project as of October 2025 reporting ([The Real Deal](https://therealdeal.com/international/2025/10/15/trump-orgs-vietnam-resort-highlights-ties-with-billionaire/)). The Real Deal also reported that Trump's second-term tariffs initially hurt Dang's business — Kinh Bac shares fell 27% in one week when a 46% tariff was imposed on Vietnamese goods — before a June trade deal that reduced the tariff to 20% restored investor confidence.
