## Overview

Yaqoub Al-Rifai was appointed Kuwait's Minister of Finance on February 2, 2026, as part of a cabinet reshuffle ordered by Emir Sheikh Meshal that affected eight ministerial positions, including the appointment of Sheikh Jarrah Jaber Al Ahmad Al Sabah as Foreign Minister. The National noted that Kuwait's cabinet has undergone several reshuffles and parliamentary dissolutions in recent years amid political disputes ([The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/gulf/2026/02/02/kuwait-appoints-new-foreign-and-finance-ministers-in-cabinet-reshuffle/)).

## Ministerial activities

In June 2026, Al-Rifai led Kuwait's delegation to the 47th Ministerial Council meeting of the OPEC Fund for International Development in Vienna, congratulating the Fund on its 50th anniversary and affirming that Kuwait, as a founding member, "takes pride in its continued contribution." He held separate discussions with OPEC Fund President Dr Abdulhamid Al-Khalifa on capacity-building cooperation and met with Kuwaiti professionals working at the Fund ([Times Kuwait](https://timeskuwait.com/minister-al-rifai-champions-kuwaits-development-vision-at-opec-fund-council-meeting/)). The following month, he issued ministerial decrees appointing four new directors general at the Finance Ministry — covering financial and tax affairs, economic affairs, public services and procurement, and state property — describing the move as part of an effort to "complete its leadership structure" and strengthen national talent in senior roles ([Times Kuwait](https://timeskuwait.com/finance-ministry-names-four-new-directors-general-in-leadership-shake-up/)).
