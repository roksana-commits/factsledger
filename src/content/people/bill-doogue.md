## Overview

Bill Doogue is an Australian criminal defence lawyer and the founding director of Doogue + George Defence
Lawyers, a Melbourne firm. According to the firm's own materials, he has been an Accredited Criminal Law
Specialist with the Law Institute of Victoria since 1998, and began his career with 2.5 years at the Aboriginal
Legal Service in the Northern Territory before building the firm from a small office in Broadmeadows
([Doogue + George](https://www.criminal-lawyers.com.au/lawyer-profiles/bill-doogue);
[Lawyers Weekly](https://www.lawyersweekly.com.au/biglaw/42690-building-a-practice-in-conversation-with-bill-doogue-and-perry-q-wood)).
His stated areas of specialisation include major tax fraud, white-collar crime, bribery and corruption, and
extradition matters ([Doogue + George](https://www.criminal-lawyers.com.au/lawyer-profiles/bill-doogue)).

## Case work reported independently

In December 2024, the Newcastle Herald reported Doogue as the defence lawyer for Perry Kouroumblis, a man accused
in the Easey Street murders case in Melbourne, quoting him telling the court that his client "had no custody
management issues" ([Newcastle Herald](https://www.newcastleherald.com.au/story/8837203/easey-street-families-stare-down-alleged-double-killer/)).

From 2025, Doogue has been named in national and international press as part of the legal team representing Erin
Patterson during her mushroom-poisoning murder trial and subsequent appeal in Victoria, including by NPR in the
United States ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2025/05/02/nx-s1-5383874/erin-patterson-murder-trial-poison-mushroom-australia))
and by ABC News, which reported in August 2026 that "Patterson has retained her solicitor, Bill Doogue, and senior
associate Ophelia Hollway" for her Court of Appeal hearing
([ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-08-19/erin-patterson-mushroom-murder-appeal-explainer/107049830)). As
of writing, that appeal is ongoing and unresolved.
