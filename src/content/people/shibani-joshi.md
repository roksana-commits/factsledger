## Overview

Shibani Joshi is an American business and technology journalist, born in Oklahoma City in
1975. She worked at CNN Financial and in strategy roles at Disney/ABC Media Networks before
spending about six years as a technology reporter and markets-show contributor for Fox
Business Network and Fox News. She holds an MBA from Harvard Business School and later
worked independently as a communications advisor and executive coach, including at Stanford's
Graduate School of Business, and teaches at the University of Oklahoma's Price College of
Business.

## A fact-checked on-air claim

In February 2013, while reporting for Fox Business, Joshi said Germany "gets a lot more
sun than we do" when explaining the country's solar-energy adoption. Slate's Future Tense
blog independently checked the claim against U.S. Department of Energy solar-irradiance
data and reported that most of the United States in fact receives more sunlight than
Germany, naming and quoting Joshi directly in its report
([Slate](https://slate.com/technology/2013/02/fox-news-solar-power-expert-claims-germany-gets-more-sun-than-the-u-s.html)).
