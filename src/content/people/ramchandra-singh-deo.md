## Overview

Ramchandra Singh Deo was a member of the former ruling family of Koriya, a princely state that is now a
district in Chhattisgarh, India, and went on to become the newly created state's first finance minister
([Scroll.in](https://scroll.in/latest/887297/ramchandra-singh-deo-the-first-finance-minister-of-chhattisgarh-dies-at-88)).
He was born on 13 February 1930 in Baikunthpur, Koriya, and held both a master's degree in science and a
doctorate in life science
([Scroll.in](https://scroll.in/latest/887297/ramchandra-singh-deo-the-first-finance-minister-of-chhattisgarh-dies-at-88)).
Before entering politics, he worked as a photographer for the filmmaker Satyajit Ray
([Scroll.in](https://scroll.in/latest/887297/ramchandra-singh-deo-the-first-finance-minister-of-chhattisgarh-dies-at-88)).

## Political career

Deo, a Congress party leader, had been active in politics since the 1960s and was elected as a Member of
the Legislative Assembly six times, serving in various capacities in the undivided state of Madhya
Pradesh, including as its Water Resources Minister
([The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/archive/nation/news-detail-623671);
[Business Standard/PTI](https://www.business-standard.com/article/pti-stories/veteran-congress-leader-ramchandra-singhdeo-dead-118072000848_1.html)).
Contemporary reporting described him as an expert on water resources and environmental issues who was
instrumental in building several irrigation projects
([The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/archive/nation/news-detail-623671)).
When Chhattisgarh was carved out of Madhya Pradesh as a separate state in November 2000, Chief Minister
Ajit Jogi appointed him to the finance portfolio, making him Chhattisgarh's first finance minister
([Scroll.in](https://scroll.in/latest/887297/ramchandra-singh-deo-the-first-finance-minister-of-chhattisgarh-dies-at-88);
[Business Standard/PTI](https://www.business-standard.com/article/pti-stories/veteran-congress-leader-ramchandra-singhdeo-dead-118072000848_1.html)).
He also authored books on economic matters and on the Maoist-affected Bastar region
([Scroll.in](https://scroll.in/latest/887297/ramchandra-singh-deo-the-first-finance-minister-of-chhattisgarh-dies-at-88)).

## Death

Deo died on 20 July 2018 at around 1:30 am at a private hospital in Raipur — named by The Tribune and the
PTI wire report as Ramkrishna Care Hospital — at the age of 88, after a prolonged illness during which he
suffered two heart strokes while hospitalised
([The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/archive/nation/news-detail-623671);
[Business Standard/PTI](https://www.business-standard.com/article/pti-stories/veteran-congress-leader-ramchandra-singhdeo-dead-118072000848_1.html)).
His last rites were performed at his native place in Koriya district
([The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/archive/nation/news-detail-623671)). Chhattisgarh's
then chief minister, Raman Singh, paid tribute, describing his life as characterised by simplicity and
austerity and noting he had served the nation and society for five decades
([The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/archive/nation/news-detail-623671)).
