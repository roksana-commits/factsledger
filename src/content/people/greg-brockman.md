## Overview

Greg Brockman is an American computer scientist and entrepreneur, a co-founder of the artificial intelligence company OpenAI and its president ([Fortune, Nov. 2025](https://fortune.com/2025/11/05/openai-greg-brockman-ai-infrastructure-data-center-master-builder); [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2026/an-interview-with-openai-president-greg-brockman-about-astra-and-alignment/)). He studied mathematics at Harvard University, transferred to MIT after his sophomore year, and left MIT without a degree in 2010 to join the payments company Stripe ([Fortune, Nov. 2025](https://fortune.com/2025/11/05/openai-greg-brockman-ai-infrastructure-data-center-master-builder); [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2026/an-interview-with-openai-president-greg-brockman-about-astra-and-alignment/)). His own 2018 biography, submitted for congressional testimony, likewise describes him as having "studied mathematics at Harvard and computer science at MIT" ([U.S. House of Representatives hearing record](https://docs.house.gov/meetings/SY/SY15/20180626/108474/HHRG-115-SY15-Bio-BrockmanG-20180626.pdf)).

Before college, Brockman placed sixth in the Society for Science's 2007 Science Talent Search for a paper titled "Asymptotic Behavior of Certain Ducci Sequences" ([Society for Science](https://www.societyforscience.org/alumni/notable/greg-brockman/)).

## Stripe

Brockman joined Stripe, then an early-stage payments startup, in 2010 ([Fortune, Nov. 2025](https://fortune.com/2025/11/05/openai-greg-brockman-ai-infrastructure-data-center-master-builder)). In his own account, published on his blog in October 2014, he joined as an engineer and was formally named chief technology officer roughly a year and a half before that post — placing his appointment to CTO around early 2013 ([Brockman's blog](https://blog.gregbrockman.com/figuring-out-the-cto-role-at-stripe)). Fortune's November 2025 profile instead describes him as joining Stripe in 2010 already as its "first CTO" ([Fortune, Nov. 2025](https://fortune.com/2025/11/05/openai-greg-brockman-ai-infrastructure-data-center-master-builder)); this profile notes both accounts rather than resolving the discrepancy. Brockman left Stripe in 2015 ([Fortune, Nov. 2025](https://fortune.com/2025/11/05/openai-greg-brockman-ai-infrastructure-data-center-master-builder); [Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2026/an-interview-with-openai-president-greg-brockman-about-astra-and-alignment/)).

## OpenAI

### Founding and early role

OpenAI launched on December 11, 2015, as a nonprofit artificial intelligence research lab; TechCrunch's launch-day report named Sam Altman and Elon Musk as co-chairs and identified Brockman, previously Stripe's CTO, as "taking the same position" — chief technology officer — at OpenAI ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2015/12/11/non-profit-openai-launches-with-backing-from-elon-musk-and-sam-altman)). By the time he testified before a U.S. House of Representatives committee in June 2018, Brockman's submitted biography described him as OpenAI's co-founder and CTO, an investor in more than 30 startups, and a board member of the Stellar digital-currency system ([U.S. House of Representatives hearing record](https://docs.house.gov/meetings/SY/SY15/20180626/108474/HHRG-115-SY15-Bio-BrockmanG-20180626.pdf)). By November 2023, he was president of OpenAI and chairman of its board ([TechCrunch, Nov. 17, 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/11/17/greg-brockman-quits-openai-after-abrupt-firing-of-sam-altman/)); no source read for this profile confirms the exact date his title changed from CTO to president.

### The November 2023 board crisis

On November 17, 2023, OpenAI's board fired Sam Altman as CEO and removed Brockman as board chairman. Hours later, upon learning the news, Brockman resigned from OpenAI entirely, posting on X: "But based on today's news, I quit. Genuinely wishing you all nothing but the best" ([TechCrunch, Nov. 17, 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/11/17/greg-brockman-quits-openai-after-abrupt-firing-of-sam-altman/)).

Three days later, on November 20, 2023, Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella announced that Brockman and Altman would join Microsoft to lead a new advanced AI research team, with several other former OpenAI staff following them ([TechCrunch, Nov. 20, 2023](https://techcrunch.com/2023/11/20/openai-co-founders-sam-altman-and-greg-brockman-to-join-microsoft/)). Nearly all of OpenAI's employees signed a letter demanding the board's resignation and the reinstatement of both Altman and Brockman, threatening to leave for Microsoft themselves otherwise. On November 21, 2023, OpenAI reinstated Altman as CEO under a restructured board, and Brockman returned to the company alongside him ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2023/11/22/1214621010/openai-reinstates-sam-altman-as-its-chief-executive)).

### The 2024 sabbatical

On or around August 5, 2024, Brockman announced he was taking a leave from OpenAI through the end of the year, describing it as his "first time to relax since co[-]founding OpenAI nine years ago" ([Fortune, Aug. 2024](https://www.fortune.com/2024/08/06/openai-sam-altman-executive-departures-tech-exodus)). He returned on or around November 12, 2024 — about three months later — announcing on X that his "longest vacation" was complete and he was "back to building OpenAI" ([NBC New York/CNBC](https://www.nbcnewyork.com/news/business/money-report/openai-co-founder-greg-brockman-returns-after-three-months-of-leave/5978809/); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/openai-co-founder-greg-brockman-204349334.html)). Reporting at the time said Brockman and Altman were working out a new role for him focused on OpenAI's toughest technical challenges ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/openai-co-founder-greg-brockman-204349334.html), citing Bloomberg News). Fortune's November 2025 profile of Brockman independently corroborates this same August-2024-to-November-2024 timeline for the leave ([Fortune, Nov. 2025](https://fortune.com/2025/11/05/openai-greg-brockman-ai-infrastructure-data-center-master-builder)). This profile is explicit on the point because it differs from an end date of November 2025 that appears in some other reference sources: the contemporaneous reporting gathered here — his own November 2024 return announcement, corroborating wire coverage from that same week, and Fortune's later retrospective account — is unambiguous that the sabbatical ran from early August 2024 to mid-November 2024, not into 2025.

### The Musk lawsuit

Elon Musk's lawsuit against OpenAI, Sam Altman, and Microsoft — alleging a breach of OpenAI's founding nonprofit mission — went to trial in federal court in Oakland, California in April 2026. Brockman testified as a witness, alongside Musk, Altman, Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella, and former OpenAI board members Helen Toner and Tasha McCauley; the jury unanimously dismissed all claims on May 18, 2026 as filed too late ([PBS NewsHour](https://www.pbs.org/newshour/nation/jury-sides-with-openai-saying-elon-musks-lawsuit-was-not-filed-on-time)). Sources disagree on whether Brockman was ever formally named as a defendant in the case at an earlier stage; this profile states only his confirmed role as a trial witness (see "What this profile does not claim," and the more detailed account of the litigation in this site's Elon Musk profile).

### 2025–2026: infrastructure and leadership

By November 2025, Fortune described Brockman as OpenAI's "builder-in-chief," overseeing what it called the most ambitious infrastructure buildout in the industry — data centers and computing capacity, including the Stargate project, across multiple U.S. states and internationally ([Fortune, Nov. 2025](https://fortune.com/2025/11/05/openai-greg-brockman-ai-infrastructure-data-center-master-builder)). In a September 2026 interview, Brockman said his focus that year had shifted toward building out OpenAI's leadership team rather than hands-on technical work, saying of coding, "I love coding, I love that feeling of flow state and just building and creating," but that he had "replaced it with something I love even more" ([Stratechery](https://stratechery.com/2026/an-interview-with-openai-president-greg-brockman-about-astra-and-alignment/)).
