## Overview

Cipto Adiguno is an Indonesian game-industry executive best known as a co-founder of [Ekuator Games](/organizations/ekuator-games/), the Bandung studio behind the "Celestian Tales" role-playing game series ([kumparan.com](https://kumparan.com/kumparantech/perusahaan-game-agate-asal-bandung-akuisisi-ekuator-games-rp-5-miliar-1548402660735036544)). When [Agate](/organizations/agate-game-company/) acquired Ekuator on January 5, 2019 for approximately Rp5 billion, Adiguno became Agate's Vice President of Consumer Games ([Bisnis Indonesia](https://teknologi.bisnis.com/read/20190126/266/882764/perusahaan-gim-agate-akuisisi-ekuator-games-ini-targetnya-di-kancah-dunia)). He went on to lead Indonesia's national game-industry trade association and later co-founded a publishing venture for regional indie studios.

## Chairman of the Indonesian Game Association

By June 2021, Adiguno was serving as Ketua Umum (Chairman) of Asosiasi Game Indonesia (AGI), a term detikInet described as running "periode 2019-2024"; he told the outlet the association's main achievements had been co-developing Indonesia's game-rating system (IGRS) with the Ministry of Communication and Informatics, running the "Archipelageek" program with the Ministry of Tourism and Creative Economy, holding annual "Game Prime" industry events, and drafting national competency standards (SKKNI) for game-industry workers ([detikInet](https://inet.detik.com/games-news/d-5606202/4-cara-memajukan-industri-game-di-indonesia)). In January 2024, as government discussion of a presidential regulation on the domestic game industry advanced, Adiguno told ANTARA News, "Kami sangat-sangat mendukung adanya Perpres ini, dan kita harap bisa secepatnya diterbitkan" (We strongly support this regulation and hope it can be issued soon), arguing it would help align previously conflicting government programs ([ANTARA News Mataram](https://mataram.antaranews.com/berita/318873/ketua-agi-harap-perpres-tentang-industri-gim-dalam-negeri-segera-terbit)). Later that month, he told the gaming outlet IndoGamers that a proposal requiring game publishers to hold a formal Indonesian legal entity had not actually been submitted to the Ministry of Law and Human Rights and remained only at an early discussion stage ([IndoGamers](https://indogamers.com/news/36521/30012024/ketua-asosiasi-game-indonesia-sebut-isu-publisher-wajib-berbadan-hukum-keliru)).

At the August 8, 2024 launch of Indonesia's "Hari Game Nasional" (National Game Day), tied to the implementation of Presidential Regulation No. 19 of 2024 on accelerating the national game industry, Adiguno told VOI that a number of Indonesian games were succeeding internationally, "especially in coal markets such as the United States and Europe," particularly in horror, relaxation and visual-novel genres, while naming access to funding as the industry's biggest constraint against foreign competitors ([VOI](https://voi.id/en/economy/406065)). On August 17, 2024, at the Baparekraf Game Prime 2024 event, Adiguno formally handed the AGI chairmanship to Shafiq Husein as his term ended; ggwp.id's report on the handover identified him at that point as a former Chief Strategy Officer at Agate ([ggwp.id](https://www.ggwp.id/gaming/others/shafiq-husein-ketua-umum-agi-00-c57ll-1p4gx9/amp)).

## Indiesche Partners

In March 2025, Adiguno co-founded Indiesche Partners together with Guillaume Jamet, a former Vice President and head of publishing at Plug In Digital who had previously established the indie labels Dear Villagers and PID Games. The venture aims to mentor Indonesian and Southeast Asian indie studios and connect them with funding and global publishing partners, focusing on premium PC and console games with production budgets under $300,000 and drawing on local culture, mythology and history; its first officially supported project is "Acts of Blood," a beat-'em-up from the new studio Eksil ([ggwp.id](https://ggwp.id/media/geek/game/indiesche-partners-diluncurkan)). A separate report on the launch corroborated the venture's structure and Jamet's publishing background ([thelazy.media](https://thelazy.media/2025/03/26/indiesche-partners-26032025/)).

## What this profile does not claim

Sources disagree on the exact sequence of Adiguno's job titles at Agate after 2019 — reported at various points as Vice President of Consumer Games, Chief Strategy Officer, and (on a self-submitted event speaker-bio page) Chief Business Officer, Agate International — and no single source read traces how or when one title changed to the next, so this profile reports each title as given by its respective source rather than asserting one fully dated career ladder. One industry blog, Ardisaz, separately describes his earlier AGI role as "vice president" for five years plus board membership from 2016 to 2019, which conflicts with the multiply-corroborated account (detikInet, ANTARA, IndoGamers, VOI and ggwp.id) that he chaired AGI as Ketua Umum for a "periode 2019-2024"; this profile follows the latter, more widely corroborated account and states the discrepancy rather than silently resolving it. No source read gives his date or place of birth, his educational background, or further detail on Ekuator Games' founding beyond what is documented in this site's companion profile of that studio.
