## Overview

Celeste Katz — who publishes under the byline Celeste Katz Marston — is an American
political journalist with a career spanning The Providence Journal, the New York Daily
News, Mic, Glamour and Newsweek. In 2018, Glamour hired her as senior political reporter
after roughly 15 years at the New York Daily News
([WWD](https://wwd.com/feature/glamours-editor-in-chief-unveils-new-hires-1202641837/)).
During the 2016 U.S. presidential election, she co-hosted "Special Relationship," a podcast
produced with The Economist, which Poynter independently named among the year's notable
political journalism
([Poynter](https://www.poynter.org/reporting-editing/2016/heres-some-of-the-best-political-journalism-of-2016/)).

## Newsweek departure

In February 2020, Katz departed Newsweek alongside the outlet's editor-in-chief and
executive editor. Columbia Journalism Review reported that Katz had been involved in
reporting on internal Newsweek matters, including a Manhattan DA raid on the company and a
senior executive's leave of absence, and that the firings carried what CJR described as
"the appearance of retaliation" — a characterization CJR itself presented as an impression
of the situation rather than a settled fact
([Columbia Journalism Review](https://www.cjr.org/the_media_today/newsweek-firings.php)).

## Later career

Katz later joined The Concord Bridge, a nonprofit newspaper in Concord, Massachusetts, as
news editor in 2023, becoming managing editor in 2024 and Editor-in-Chief in April 2025.
