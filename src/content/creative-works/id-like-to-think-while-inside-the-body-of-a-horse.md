## Overview

"I'd Like to Think While inside the Body of a Horse" is a 1964 gelatin-silver photograph by the Japanese Surrealist photographer Kansuke Yamamoto (1914-1987). According to the J. Paul Getty Museum's own collection record, it was originally published in the Asahi Shimbun on 9 February 1964 as a photographic illustration to a poem of the same title by Hideo Oguma ([J. Paul Getty Museum](https://www.getty.edu/art/collection/objects/247763/kansuke-yamamoto-i%27d-like-to-think-while-inside-the-body-of-a-horse-japanese-1964/)).

## Collection and exhibition history

The work is held by the J. Paul Getty Museum under accession number 2009.17.18 ([J. Paul Getty Museum](https://www.getty.edu/art/collection/objects/247763/kansuke-yamamoto-i%27d-like-to-think-while-inside-the-body-of-a-horse-japanese-1964/)). A vintage silver-gelatin print bearing the artist's estate stamp was exhibited at Michael Hoppen Gallery in London in 2024, covered by independent art-press criticism ([Musée Magazine](https://museemagazine.com/culture/2024/12/10/kansuke-yamamoto-blending-reality-and-imaginary)).
