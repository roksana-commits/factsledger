## Overview

"Celestian Tales: Realms Beyond" is a turn-based role-playing game developed by the Indonesian studios [Ekuator Games](/organizations/ekuator-games/) and [Agate](/organizations/agate-game-company/), released on Steam on May 7, 2020 and published by Digital Tribe. It is the sequel to "Celestian Tales: Old North," Ekuator's 2015 Kickstarter-funded debut, and carries over save data letting players start at the previous game's maximum level.

## Crowdfunding and delayed development

Ekuator Games launched a Kickstarter campaign for the game on August 21, 2017, seeking $15,000 to complete what the studio said was roughly the final quarter of development after self-funding most of the game using proceeds from "Old North"; the campaign described a story following six knights serving a young heir amid palace intrigue and hundreds of politically motivated non-player characters, combined with turn-based combat and a hand-drawn, animated-pixel-art visual style ([Cliqist](http://cliqist.com/2017/09/15/kickstarter-game-week-celestian-tales-realms-beyond/)). The campaign closed on September 20, 2017 having raised $21,857 from 912 backers, well past its goal ([Kickstarter](https://www.kickstarter.com/projects/ekuatorgames/celestian-tales-realms-beyond)). The game had originally been targeted for release around December 2017, but that date slipped; when Agate acquired Ekuator Games in January 2019, Agate committed to funding the remaining development so Ekuator could meet its outstanding Kickstarter obligations to backers, and the game finally shipped in May 2020 ([Hybrid.co.id](https://hybrid.co.id/post/agate-akuisisi-ekuator-games-jamin-masa-depan-seri-celestian-tales/)).

## Setting and design

The game is framed around a smaller-scale, character-driven conflict rather than a conventional fantasy save-the-world plot: its own storefront description says it has "no world-eating evil to slay," instead following six knights protecting a young noble heir through court intrigue and morally ambiguous choices. Visually, it pairs hand-painted, watercolor-style environments with pixel-animated character sprites, a continuation of the art direction Ekuator used in "Old North." It shipped as a Windows-only, DRM-free release with cloud saves and controller support, in English only ([GOG.com](https://www.gog.com/en/game/celestian_tales_realms_beyond)).

## Critical reception

Reviewing the finished game for RPGFan, Audra Bowling scored it 78/100, recommending it to players who like "shorter RPGs" with "mature, personal, and slower-moving narratives that focus on world-building and politics," and praising its "gorgeous visuals, strong narrative, well-rounded gameplay" across a roughly 15-16 hour playtime built around turn-based combat, a shared experience pool, crafting and weapon-forging, and story choices with moral consequences ([RPGFan](https://www.rpgfan.com/review/celestian-tales-realms-beyond/)). Save or Quit's review gave a "Save for Later" verdict, similarly praising the story and characters but flagging glitchy textures and character-pathing problems in fullscreen mode that it suggested might be resolved in future patches ([Save or Quit](https://saveorquit.com/2020/06/09/review-celestian-tales-realms-beyond/)).
