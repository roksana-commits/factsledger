## Overview

The Great Public Schools (GPS) Association of Queensland runs an interschool rugby union
competition among the state's group of elite private schools, with a history reported to run to
roughly a century. In 2018, The Southport School's campaign for an 11th title was covered by the
Gold Coast Bulletin against that long-running backdrop, while The Courier-Mail separately reported
on that year's premiership race involving schools such as Anglican Church Grammar School
([Gold Coast Bulletin](https://www.goldcoastbulletin.com.au); [The Courier-Mail](https://www.couriermail.com.au/sport/rugby/gps-rugby-gregory-terrace-must-overcome-history-to-topple-rivals/news-story/81ba2d252b256b81cedc3d98b03f6519)).

## A development pipeline, and a rivalry with rugby league

Beyond school-level bragging rights, GPS rugby functions as a recognized pathway into
representative and professional rugby. A 2023 ABC News report on the state of Australian rugby
specifically discussed the competition's role in this pipeline, and the pressure it faces from
rugby league clubs recruiting players out of the same schools
([ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2023-06-14/australian-rugby-union-wallabies-wallaroos-grassroots-schools/102464748)).
