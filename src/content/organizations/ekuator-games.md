## Overview

Ekuator Games was a Bandung, West Java-based Indonesian video game studio, founded by Cipto Adiguno, Ray Suryadiptya, Nathalia Noor and Denny Eka Saputro, best known for the crowdfunded role-playing game series "Celestian Tales" ([kumparan.com](https://kumparan.com/kumparantech/perusahaan-game-agate-asal-bandung-akuisisi-ekuator-games-rp-5-miliar-1548402660735036544)). Hybrid.co.id describes the studio as founded by former employees of [Agate](/organizations/agate-game-company/), the larger Bandung developer that would later acquire it ([Hybrid.co.id](https://hybrid.co.id/post/agate-akuisisi-ekuator-games-jamin-masa-depan-seri-celestian-tales/)).

## Celestian Tales

Ekuator's first release, "Celestian Tales: Old North," was funded through Kickstarter and reviewed by the English-language outlet RPGamer in August 2015. The review described a turn-based combat system built around six playable protagonists sharing a single experience pool, no traditional magic system, and roughly ten hours of largely linear playtime; it praised the game's "nice visuals" and "generally solid, if uninspired, gameplay" but concluded the story "simply feels like a prologue to something bigger than a full title standing on its own," awarding it 2.5 out of 5 ([RPGamer](https://rpgamer.com/review/celestian-tales-old-north-review/)). The studio also released a DLC pack, "Howl of the Ravager," and some of its team contributed to Agate's "Valthirian Arc: Hero School Story" before the two companies combined ([Hybrid.co.id](https://hybrid.co.id/post/agate-akuisisi-ekuator-games-jamin-masa-depan-seri-celestian-tales/)). The studio won several regional awards for its work — Most Promising Game at the 2014 Game Developer Gathering (GDG), Best Narrative and Best Audio at GDG 2015, Best Narrative at IN.Game Jogja 2015, and Best Local Developer at Toys & Games Republic 2015 — and by 2019 was described as having been named among Tech in Asia's "10 Best Indonesian Games" of 2016 ([TeknoJurnal](https://teknojurnal.com/agate-akuisisi-ekuator-games/); [Bisnis Indonesia](https://teknologi.bisnis.com/read/20190126/266/882764/perusahaan-gim-agate-akuisisi-ekuator-games-ini-targetnya-di-kancah-dunia)). A sequel, "Celestian Tales: Realms Beyond," had originally been targeted for release around December 2017 but was delayed ([Hybrid.co.id](https://hybrid.co.id/post/agate-akuisisi-ekuator-games-jamin-masa-depan-seri-celestian-tales/)).

## Acquisition by Agate

On January 5, 2019, Agate acquired Ekuator Games for approximately Rp5 billion. Founder Cipto Adiguno became Agate's Vice President of Consumer Games, telling reporters he believed the combination would let the companies "create higher quality games and bring Indonesia's name to the world gaming industry" ([Bisnis Indonesia](https://teknologi.bisnis.com/read/20190126/266/882764/perusahaan-gim-agate-akuisisi-ekuator-games-ini-targetnya-di-kancah-dunia); [kumparan.com](https://kumparan.com/kumparantech/perusahaan-game-agate-asal-bandung-akuisisi-ekuator-games-rp-5-miliar-1548402660735036544)). Agate's then-CEO Arief Widhiyasa said the deal aimed to combine "the talent forces at Agate with talents from Ekuator" to speed up and improve the quality of Agate's consumer-game output ([kumparan.com](https://kumparan.com/kumparantech/perusahaan-game-agate-asal-bandung-akuisisi-ekuator-games-rp-5-miliar-1548402660735036544)). As part of the deal, Agate committed to funding the remaining development of "Celestian Tales: Realms Beyond" so as to fulfill Ekuator's outstanding Kickstarter obligations, with the game's intellectual property becoming Agate's property; Hybrid.co.id described Ekuator as continuing afterward as "a separate division but integrated with Agate" ([Hybrid.co.id](https://hybrid.co.id/post/agate-akuisisi-ekuator-games-jamin-masa-depan-seri-celestian-tales/)).

## What this profile does not claim

No source read states Ekuator Games' founding year or its team size at any point in its history; the earliest independently dated activity found is the August 2015 review of "Celestian Tales: Old North." This profile does not describe Ekuator's precise legal or operational status after 2019 beyond Hybrid.co.id's characterization of it as an integrated but separate division, since no source gives a fuller or more current account. It is not to be confused with the unrelated French tabletop role-playing game publisher "Studio Agate" — this profile concerns a studio acquired by the Indonesian, Bandung-based video game developer Agate.
