## Overview

CS Drobeta is a football club based in Drobeta-Turnu Severin, Mehedinți County, Romania, founded in 2025 by the city's municipality and county council as a publicly funded club ([Sport.ro](https://www.sport.ro/fotbal-intern/sunt-doua-echipe-cu-acelasi-nume-chiar-si-la-drobeta-turnu-severin-una-dintre-ele-este-antrenata-de-florin-soava.html)). Drobeta-Turnu Severin has a history of multiple, separately constituted clubs carrying very similar names; this profile concerns specifically the 2025-founded municipal club identified by the debut-season record described below, not the city's earlier football clubs.

## Debut season

In its first season, CS Drobeta won the Mehedinți county championship (Liga 4) with a record of 24 wins, 1 draw and no losses, and a goal difference of 168-15, under a named coach and club president ([ProSport](https://liga2.prosport.ro/liga-4/cs-drobeta-este-noua-campioana-judeteana-de-la-mehedinti-19389737)). The club completed a league-and-cup double by defeating a rival club also based in Drobeta-Turnu Severin, CS Drobeta 2024, by a score of 7-0 in the county cup final ([ProSport](https://liga2.prosport.ro/liga-4/nou-infiintata-cs-drobeta-a-izbutit-eventul-la-mehedinti-19390724)).

## Two clubs, one city

Independent sports reporting specifically notes that Drobeta-Turnu Severin currently has two football clubs with near-identical names — this publicly funded club and the separate CS Drobeta 2024 — distinguished by their funding and ownership structures ([Sport.ro](https://www.sport.ro/fotbal-intern/sunt-doua-echipe-cu-acelasi-nume-chiar-si-la-drobeta-turnu-severin-una-dintre-ele-este-antrenata-de-florin-soava.html)).

## What this profile does not claim

This profile is deliberately scoped to the 2025-2026 season record confirmed by independent Romanian sports-press reporting. It does not assert any continuity between this club and Drobeta-Turnu Severin's earlier, separately constituted football clubs (including a former FC Drobeta-Turnu Severin active 1958-2011 and a CS Turnu Severin active 2007-2013), and it does not independently confirm the club's ownership structure, budget, or plans beyond its debut season.
