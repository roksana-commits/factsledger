## Overview

First Hawaiian, Inc. is the holding company for First Hawaiian Bank, Hawaii's oldest bank, which traces its founding to August 17, 1858, when Charles Reed Bishop and William A. Aldrich opened Bishop & Co. Savings Bank on the Honolulu waterfront — described as only the second bank to open west of the Rocky Mountains ([Encyclopedia.com](https://www.encyclopedia.com/books/politics-and-business-magazines/first-hawaiian-inc)). The institution changed names repeatedly over the following century, becoming The Bank of Bishop and Co., Ltd. in 1919, Bishop First National Bank of Honolulu in 1929, Bishop First National Bank of Hawaii in 1933, Bishop National Bank of Hawaii in 1956, First National Bank of Hawaii in 1960, and finally First Hawaiian Bank in 1969 ([Encyclopedia.com](https://www.encyclopedia.com/books/politics-and-business-magazines/first-hawaiian-inc)). It grew through acquisitions in the early 1990s, purchasing First Interstate of Hawaii, Inc. in 1991 (about $900 million in assets) and Pioneer Federal Savings Bank in 1993 (about $650 million in assets) ([Encyclopedia.com](https://www.encyclopedia.com/books/politics-and-business-magazines/first-hawaiian-inc)).

## Merger with Bank of the West and BNP Paribas ownership

In 1998, First Hawaiian merged with San Francisco-based Bank of the West to form BancWest Corporation, combining Hawaii's oldest bank with a mainland California institution under a single holding structure ([Honolulu Star-Bulletin](https://archives.starbulletin.com/2002/09/29/special/story4.html)). By 2001, France's BNP Paribas — then described as the world's fifth-largest bank — acquired full ownership of BancWest for $2.4 billion, making First Hawaiian Bank a wholly owned subsidiary of a French banking group even as it kept its Hawaii-based branding and community focus ([Honolulu Star-Bulletin](https://archives.starbulletin.com/2002/09/29/special/story4.html)). [J. Michael Shepherd](/people/michael-shepherd/), who later led BancWest's Bank of the West subsidiary as president and CEO from 2008 and chaired BNP Paribas USA from 2016, previously served as a director of First Hawaiian, Inc. during this period of BNP Paribas ownership ([citybiz](https://www.citybiz.co/article/454751/discover-financial-services-appoints-j-michael-shepherd-to-its-board/); [SEC EDGAR](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1393612/000119312523212472/d573571dex992.htm)).

## 2016 return to public markets

In July 2016, First Hawaiian, Inc. filed to raise up to $558 million through an initial public offering on Nasdaq, selling roughly 21 million shares priced between $21 and $23, as part of a broader capital-raising strategy BNP Paribas had announced in December 2015; BNP Paribas chose to spin off the bank in a share offering rather than sell it outright, and was set to retain about 85% ownership once the offering closed, with the bank itself, then holding about $19 billion in assets, receiving none of the IPO proceeds ([American Banker](https://www.americanbanker.com/news/first-hawaiian-plans-558m-ipo)). The offering, underwritten by a syndicate including JPMorgan, Barclays and Credit Suisse, returned First Hawaiian to public markets after 15 years as a wholly owned BNP Paribas subsidiary ([American Banker](https://www.americanbanker.com/news/first-hawaiian-plans-558m-ipo)).
