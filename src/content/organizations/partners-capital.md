## Overview

Partners Capital is an outsourced chief investment officer (OCIO) firm that manages money on behalf of
endowments, foundations, family offices and wealthy individuals rather than running its own branded
funds. It was incorporated in England on 29 August 2001 — first under the name Law 2338 Limited, then
briefly as Partner Wealth Management Limited, before settling on its current name by early 2002
([UK Companies House](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/04277937)).
Early backers included the Rothschild Foundation and Apax Capital founding partner Sir Ronald Cohen
([Institutional Investor](https://www.institutionalinvestor.com/article/2cz096w6yb7a4zm07ydj4/corner-office/partners-capital-found-a-partner-of-its-own-in-general-atlantic)).

## A generational transition, not a sale

By 2024 the firm managed roughly $50 billion and was led by CEO Arjun Raghavan, with Paul Dimitruk
among its co-founders. That year, private equity firm General Atlantic acquired a minority stake in
Partners Capital through one of its funds. Institutional Investor reports the deal was structured
specifically to manage succession: it gives liquidity to founding partners and early shareholders, many
of them in their seventies and eighties, while retaining current senior staff through equity incentives
carrying a six-year lockup and reserving shares for future partners. Raghavan described the logic as
resetting "the economics" to "more easily achieve the generational transfer"
([Institutional Investor](https://www.institutionalinvestor.com/article/2cz096w6yb7a4zm07ydj4/corner-office/partners-capital-found-a-partner-of-its-own-in-general-atlantic)).

## What this profile does not claim

Neither source read for this profile discloses the size of General Atlantic's stake or what it paid for
it — only that the investment came through a fund vehicle and was designed around succession rather than
a change of control.
