## Overview

The Lemkin Institute for Genocide Prevention is a Philadelphia-based nonprofit whose documented work concerns genocide prevention, early warning and human-security advocacy. Independent reporting describes the organization as having been founded in 2017 as the Iraq Project for Genocide Prevention and Accountability before adopting its present name in 2021 ([Jewish Exponent](https://www.jewishexponent.com/philadelphia-institutes-use-of-the-term-genocide-leads-to-legal-dispute/)). Public nonprofit records identify the current legal entity and its federal tax-exempt status ([ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/871787869)).

## History and activities

The organization's early work was connected to documenting atrocities and pursuing accountability in Iraq. Later reporting describes a broader focus on genocide-risk monitoring and public alerts, including the Red Flag Alerts associated with its work ([The Jerusalem Post](https://www.jpost.com/international/article-869005)). A United Nations information-system archive also records materials associated with the institute, while an academic discussion in the Harvard Human Rights Journal places the organization within contemporary debates about Raphael Lemkin's legacy ([UNISPAL](https://www.un.org/unispal/document/lemkin-institute-for-genocide-prevention-archives/) [Harvard Human Rights Journal](https://harvardhrj.com/2025/06/reading-lemkin-today/)).

## Naming dispute

In 2025 and 2026, independent outlets reported a dispute between the organization and members of Raphael Lemkin's family over the use of his name. The family has argued that the institute's public positions do not represent Lemkin's legacy; those claims are reported here as allegations, not as an adjudicated finding ([Jewish Exponent](https://www.jewishexponent.com/philadelphia-institutes-use-of-the-term-genocide-leads-to-legal-dispute/) [The Media Line](https://themedialine.org/headlines/lemkin-family-presses-pennsylvania-over-genocide-institutes-use-of-name/)). The final outcome of the complaint was not confirmed in the sources reviewed.

## What this profile does not claim

This profile does not determine whether the institute's political or legal positions accurately represent Raphael Lemkin's views. It also does not state the current size, budget or operational reach of the organization beyond the facts documented in the listed sources.
