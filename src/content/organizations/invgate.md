## Overview

InvGate is a Buenos Aires-founded software company providing a SaaS platform for IT service management and IT asset management, serving enterprises and mid-sized organizations ranging from about 50 to 50,000 employees. Founded in 2009, the company operated without outside investment for 14 years before seeking institutional capital ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/07/12/invgate-it-management-35m-latin-america-enterprise/)).

## First institutional funding round

In July 2023, InvGate raised $35 million in its first growth round, led by Riverwood Capital with participation from Endeavor Catalyst. At the time, the company reported sustained 60% year-over-year growth over the previous three years and operations spanning 57 countries, with offices in Buenos Aires, Mexico, the United States and the United Kingdom. Its customer base, concentrated in Latin America and the United States, was reported to include organizations such as NASA, the U.S. Army, KPMG, Toyota and Globant. CEO Ariel Gesto said the capital would fund global expansion through customer acquisition, new local offices, and product development ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2023/07/12/invgate-it-management-35m-latin-america-enterprise/)).
