## Overview

The All Pueblo Council of Governors (APCG) is an Albuquerque-based body representing 20 Pueblo Nations in New Mexico and one in Texas. Through 2025 and 2026 it has led a sustained public campaign to permanently protect the Greater Chaco region — including Chaco Canyon, a UNESCO World Heritage Site sacred to the Pueblo peoples — from future oil and gas leasing, pressing for a permanent 10-mile protective buffer around the canyon ([Native News Online](https://nativenewsonline.net/sovereignty/all-pueblo-council-of-governors-applauds-reintroduction-of-legislation-to-protect-chaco-canyon)).

## The Chaco Canyon campaign

Chairman James R. Mountain has framed the renewed legislative push as the product of "countless meetings, cultural resource studies, and tireless advocacy," with the reintroduced protective bill backed by Senator Ben Ray Luján, Representative Teresa Leger Fernández and the broader New Mexico Congressional Delegation ([Native News Online](https://nativenewsonline.net/sovereignty/all-pueblo-council-of-governors-applauds-reintroduction-of-legislation-to-protect-chaco-canyon)). At a Capitol news conference in July 2026, council chair Joey Sanchez voiced frustration at the Interior Department's response, saying: "We asked for a meeting, but we never got it. I don't know if you're here in town, but you know we're here. We're ready to be with you. We ask you to come and visit," referring to Interior Secretary Doug Burgum ([High Country News](https://www.hcn.org/articles/pueblo-leaders-new-mexico-lawmakers-speak-out-to-protect-chaco-canyon/)).
