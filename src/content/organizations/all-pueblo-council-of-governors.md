## Overview

The All Pueblo Council of Governors (APCG) is an Albuquerque-based body representing 20 Pueblo Nations in New Mexico and one in Texas. Through 2025 and 2026 it has led a sustained public campaign to permanently protect the Greater Chaco region — including Chaco Canyon, a UNESCO World Heritage Site sacred to the Pueblo peoples — from future oil and gas leasing, pressing for a permanent 10-mile protective buffer around the canyon ([Native News Online](https://nativenewsonline.net/sovereignty/all-pueblo-council-of-governors-applauds-reintroduction-of-legislation-to-protect-chaco-canyon)).

## The Chaco Canyon campaign

Native News Online identifies James R. Mountain as the council's "Chairman," framing the renewed legislative push as the product of "countless meetings, cultural resource studies, and tireless advocacy," with the reintroduced protective bill backed by Senator Ben Ray Luján, Representative Teresa Leger Fernández and the broader New Mexico Congressional Delegation ([Native News Online](https://nativenewsonline.net/sovereignty/all-pueblo-council-of-governors-applauds-reintroduction-of-legislation-to-protect-chaco-canyon)). At a Capitol news conference in July 2026, High Country News identifies Joey Sanchez as council "chair," reporting his frustration at the Interior Department's response: "We asked for a meeting, but we never got it. I don't know if you're here in town, but you know we're here. We're ready to be with you. We ask you to come and visit," referring to Interior Secretary Doug Burgum ([High Country News](https://www.hcn.org/articles/pueblo-leaders-new-mexico-lawmakers-speak-out-to-protect-chaco-canyon/)). The two sources' leadership titles for the same mid-2026 period are not reconciled here; this profile reports each attribution as its respective source states it rather than asserting a single uncontested titleholder.

## What this profile does not claim

Neither source read here establishes the council's founding date or broader institutional history; this profile is limited to its documented role in the 2025-2026 Chaco Canyon protection campaign rather than a full organizational history. Nor does this profile assert a single, uncontested chair or chairman of the council: High Country News and Native News Online name different people (Joey Sanchez and James R. Mountain, respectively) in overlapping 2026 coverage, and that conflict is flagged rather than resolved.
