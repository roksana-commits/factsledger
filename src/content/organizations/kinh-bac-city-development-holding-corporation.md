## Overview

Kinh Bac City Development Holding Corporation (ticker KBC) is a Vietnamese industrial real estate developer listed on the Ho Chi Minh Stock Exchange, incorporated on March 27, 2002 and headquartered at Que Vo Industrial Park in Bac Ninh province ([Vietstock](https://finance.vietstock.vn/KBC-kinh-bac-city-development-holding-corporation.htm?languageid=2)). It is chaired by Vietnamese industrialist [Dang Thanh Tam](/people/dang-thanh-tam/). Its subsidiary Hung Yen Hospitality is the Vietnamese developer behind [Trump International, Vietnam](/organizations/trump-international-vietnam/), the Trump Organization's licensed $1.5 billion golf and residential project in Hung Yen province ([Vietnam Investment Review](https://vir.com.vn/construction-begins-on-15-billion-trump-organization-golf-complex-in-hung-yen-128932.html)).

## Business and industrial parks

Kinh Bac operates industrial parks including Que Vo in Bac Ninh and Trang Due in Hai Phong. In January 2021, Vietnam's industrial parks attracted $1.23 billion in foreign direct investment — about half of the country's national FDI total that month — including a $750 million expansion by LG Electronics for TV screen production in Hai Phong and a $270 million project by Foxconn for tablet and laptop manufacturing in Bac Giang ([VnExpress International](https://e.vnexpress.net/news/business/companies/industrial-property-company-kinh-bac-city-eyes-record-profits-4253743.html)). As of a February 2025 report, the company controlled about 6,600 hectares of industrial land, roughly 5.09% of Vietnam's total industrial-land supply ([The Investor](https://theinvestor.vn/vietnams-leading-ip-developer-kbc-eyes-7-fold-profit-hike-in-2025-d14555.html)).

The Real Deal reported that Kinh Bac's revenue rose 161% between 2017 and 2021, as manufacturers shifted production to Vietnam to avoid tariffs during the U.S.-China trade war of Donald Trump's first presidential term ([The Real Deal](https://therealdeal.com/international/2025/10/15/trump-orgs-vietnam-resort-highlights-ties-with-billionaire/)).

## Recent financial performance

VietnamNet reported that Kinh Bac's Q3 2024 revenue rose 3.8-fold year over year to about VND950 billion, with post-tax profit of nearly VND202 billion, up sharply from VND18.5 billion a year earlier; nine-month 2024 revenue reached VND1.99 trillion with net profit of VND397 billion ([VietnamNet](https://vietnamnet.vn/en/kinh-bac-city-s-q3-profits-soar-as-trump-collaboration-looms-2338741.html)). The Investor reported that Kinh Bac's full-year 2024 results nonetheless fell well short of the company's own targets — net profit of VND450 billion was only about 11.5% of target, and revenue of VND3.33 trillion was about 37% of target — after which the company set a 2025 target of VND3.2 trillion in consolidated net profit, a sevenfold increase, and VND10 trillion in consolidated revenue, about triple the 2024 figure ([The Investor](https://theinvestor.vn/vietnams-leading-ip-developer-kbc-eyes-7-fold-profit-hike-in-2025-d14555.html)).

## The Trump-licensed project and 2025 tariff volatility

Kinh Bac subsidiary Hung Yen Hospitality broke ground with the Trump Organization on the $1.5 billion Hung Yen project on May 21, 2025, with Chairman Dang Thanh Tam saying the partners "share the same vision of transforming Hung Yen into a leading golf and lifestyle destination in Asia" ([Vietnam Investment Review](https://vir.com.vn/construction-begins-on-15-billion-trump-organization-golf-complex-in-hung-yen-128932.html)). By October 2025, Kinh Bac had already paid the Trump Organization $5 million in licensing fees for the project. The Real Deal reported that new U.S. tariffs in Trump's second term initially hurt the company — its shares fell 27% in one week when a 46% tariff was imposed on Vietnamese goods — before a June 2025 trade deal that lowered the tariff to 20% restored investor confidence ([The Real Deal](https://therealdeal.com/international/2025/10/15/trump-orgs-vietnam-resort-highlights-ties-with-billionaire/)).
