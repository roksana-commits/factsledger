## Overview

Tengo Project is a small development team operating within Natsume Atari, made up of roughly three core industry veterans, including artist Shunichi Taniguchi and composer Iku Mizutani, whose careers trace back to the 1990s. The team specializes in restoring and remastering games its own members originally developed, refining them with modern design sensibilities while keeping their retro aesthetics and difficulty ([Aftermath](https://aftermath.site/shadow-of-the-ninja-reborn-tengo-project-is-a-miracle/)).

## Catalogue

The team's releases include Wild Guns Reloaded (2014), a restoration of the 1994 SNES title Wild Guns with redrawn pixel art, new playable characters Doris and Bullet, and four-player cooperative play; The Ninja Saviors: Return of the Warriors, a ground-up remake of a Super Famicom beat 'em up expanded with a boss-sized fighter; and Pocky and Rocky Reshrined, a renovation of an earlier scrolling shooter ([Aftermath](https://aftermath.site/shadow-of-the-ninja-reborn-tengo-project-is-a-miracle/)). Time Extension separately credits the team with The Ninja Warriors, called "a masterclass in SNES revival," and Omega Five, a shoot 'em up previously released on Xbox Live Arcade. In 2024 the team released Shadow of the Ninja - Reborn, described as its most radical and ambitious remake to date. Working with publisher Winning Entertainment Group, it has also announced a remaster of the 1990 NES game Cybernetic Attack: Final Mission, expanding the roster with new characters ([Time Extension](https://www.timeextension.com/news/2026/09/s-c-a-t-is-back-tengo-project-returns-with-a-remaster-of-the-nes-classic-final-mission)).
