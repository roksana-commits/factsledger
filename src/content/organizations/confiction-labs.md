## Overview

Confiction Labs is a game studio led by [Arief Widhiyasa](/people/arief-widhiyasa/), also Chairman of the Bandung video game developer [Agate](/organizations/agate-game-company/), and by Igor Tanzil, a former Agate chief marketing officer and chief creative officer. The company first became publicly known in September 2023 under the name Mythic Protocol, when the gaming trade press PocketGamer.biz reported that it had raised an oversubscribed $6.5 million seed round co-led by Shima Capital and Alpha JWC, with participation from Saison Capital, GDV Venture, Planetarium Labs, Arcane Group, Presto Labs, Marblex, Emurgo Ventures and Hyperithm, to fund a cross-platform action-shooter roguelite called "Riftstorm" and a blockchain-based asset system then described as "Decentralised Universal Meta" ([PocketGamer.biz](https://www.pocketgamer.biz/mythic-protocol-raises-65m-towards-blockchain-collaboration-and-cross-platform-ambitions/)). BeyondGames.biz, reporting the same round, described the company as Singapore-based and its team as more than 130 people globally, including hires from Microsoft, Samsung and Caravan Studio ([BeyondGames.biz](https://www.beyondgames.biz/41949/mythic-protocol-raises-6-5m-for-multi-platform-games-blockchain-and-decentralised-universal-meta/)).

## From Mythic Protocol to Confiction Labs

On March 15, 2024, Tanzil wrote on the company's own blog that "our entire company is shifting from Mythic Protocol to Confiction Labs," describing a rebrand intended to carry the same intellectual property into a broader vision the company calls "Collaborative Entertainment," rather than a change of underlying team or ownership ([Confiction Labs, via Medium](https://medium.com/confiction-labs/lets-talk-conviction-fe14167929ae)). In a September 2024 interview, Widhiyasa said he stepped back from running Agate day to day after becoming exposed to web3 gaming in "late 2021," which he described as the origin of the project that became Confiction Labs ([Gaming.net](https://www.gaming.net/arief-widhiyasa-ceo-of-confiction-labs-interview-series/)). The company's own "About" page lists Widhiyasa as chief executive and Tanzil as Chief Creative Officer, alongside an art director, a chief technology officer and a chief product officer ([Confiction Labs](https://www.confiction.com/about-us/)).

## Riftstorm and ties to Agate

Confiction Labs' flagship game, "Riftstorm," continued development under the Confiction Labs name. A June 2025 report in the Indonesian financial-news outlet Kontan (via momsmoney.kontan.co.id) described "Riftstorm" as a joint undertaking of Agate and Confiction Labs, quoting Agate chief executive [Shieny Aprilia](/people/shieny-aprilia/) on the potential of Indonesia's game industry and Widhiyasa, identified there as Confiction Labs' CEO, calling it "a large-scale project demanding high commitment." The same report put the investment at more than Rp 100 billion (about $6.5 million, matching the 2023 seed figure), credited more than 120 Indonesian staff, and said a public demo had placed in the Top 50 most-played games during Steam Next Fest 2025 ([Kontan/momsmoney](https://momsmoney.kontan.co.id/news/agate-dan-confiction-labs-luncurkan-riftstorm-gim-looter-shooter-buatan-anak-bangsa)). "Riftstorm" entered Steam Early Access on August 11, 2026; the gaming outlet The Magic Rain credited the release to "Singapore-based studio" Confiction Labs after what it called nearly five years of development, and described the game's setting as a fictional "Mythic Protocol universe" — the origin of the company's earlier public name ([The Magic Rain](https://themagicrain.com/2026/08/riftstorm-launches-in-steam-early-access/)).

## What this profile does not claim

No source read gives an exact incorporation date for the company; "2021" as an origin year rests on Tanzil's own recollection and on Widhiyasa's account in a single trade-press interview, not on a company record or a contemporaneous independent report. Sources disagree on where the company is based, with BeyondGames.biz (2023) and The Magic Rain (2026) calling it Singapore-based and Gaming.net (2024) calling it an Indonesia-based studio; this profile does not resolve that conflict. This profile treats Tanzil's own March 2024 statement as the basis for describing "Mythic Protocol" and "Confiction Labs" as successive names of the same company, because no independent source directly addresses the relationship, though later reporting is consistent with it by describing the same founders and the same flagship game under the newer name. Reported team-size figures (130+ globally in 2023 versus more than 120 Indonesian staff in 2025) are not reconciled by any source read. This profile does not cover the company's finances, valuation, or any funding activity after the 2023 seed round.
