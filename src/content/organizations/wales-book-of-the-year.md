## Overview

Wales Book of the Year is an annual Welsh literary award administered by Literature Wales, presented in parallel English-language and Welsh-language categories covering fiction, poetry, creative non-fiction, and children's and young people's writing.

## 2026 winners

Literature Wales announced the 2026 winners on 9 July 2026. The English-language fiction award went to Anthony Shapland's debut novel "A Room Above a Shop" (Granta), recognised for its "tender, precise, poetic prose," a result independently reported by the Welsh news website Nation.Cymru ([Nation.Cymru](https://nation.cymru/culture/wales-book-of-the-year-2026-winners-announced/)). Other 2026 category winners named on Literature Wales's own award page include Laura Wainwright's "The Storm's Flora" (poetry), Joe Dunthorne's "Children of Radium: A Buried Inheritance" (creative non-fiction), and, in the Welsh-language categories, Sonia Edwards's "Tri" (fiction) and Mererid Hopwood's "Mae" (poetry) ([Literature Wales](https://www.literaturewales.org/our-projects/wales-book-year/wales-book-of-the-year-2026/wales-book-of-the-year-winners-2026/)).
