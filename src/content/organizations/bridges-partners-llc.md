## Overview

Bridges Partners LLC is a Delaware-incorporated public-relations and political-consulting firm. A U.S. Department of Justice Foreign Agents Registration Act filing identifies the firm in connection with an Israeli Ministry of Foreign Affairs-funded campaign known as Project Esther ([U.S. Department of Justice](https://efile.fara.gov/docs/7652-Exhibit-AB-20250926-1.pdf)).

## Corporate and regulatory record

The FARA filing records the firm under registration number 7652 and describes its ownership, foreign-principal relationship and campaign work. It also describes a contract connected to Havas Media Group Germany and a network of U.S.-based social-media influencers. These details are reported here as information in the filing, not as a finding that every participant had an independent legal obligation to register ([FARA registration statement](https://efile.fara.gov/docs/7652-Exhibit-AB-20250926-1.pdf)).

## Project Esther and public scrutiny

Law.com reported on legal representation associated with the campaign and described the reported $900,000 arrangement ([Law.com](https://www.law.com/international-edition/2025/10/07/pillsbury-advises-consultants-organising-900000-social-media-influencer-campaign-backed-by-israel/)). Public Citizen later described a complaint alleging that some U.S.-based influencers had not made sufficient foreign-agent disclosures; the complaint is an allegation, not a court judgment ([Public Citizen](https://www.citizen.org/article/fara-complaint-alleges-us-based-social-media-influencers-are-acting-as-agents-of-israel/)). The Quincy Institute has separately analyzed the campaign within a broader influence-strategy framework, but its wider estimates are presented as that organization's analysis rather than as settled fact ([Quincy Institute](https://quincyinst.org/research/the-eighth-front-inside-israels-1-billion-influence-campaign/)).

## What this profile does not claim

This profile does not conclude that Bridges Partners or any individual influencer violated FARA. It does not state the identities of undisclosed participants, the final legal outcome of the complaint, or a definitive total for the wider influence strategy beyond the figures attributed to the listed sources.
