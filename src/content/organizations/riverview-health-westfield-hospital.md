## Overview

Riverview Health Westfield Hospital opened on October 23, 2018, in Westfield, Indiana, as part of the Riverview Health system, with then-CEO Seth Warren discussing the hospital ahead of its grand opening ([Current Publishing](https://youarecurrent.com/2018/10/22/riverview-health-westfield-hospital-to-open-oct-23/); [Current Publishing](https://www.youarecurrent.com/2018/08/21/a-westfield-hospital-riverview-health-ceo-seth-warren-talks-grand-opening/)).

## A 2025 expansion tied to Westfield's growth

By May 2025, Riverview Health announced plans for a new 13,630-square-foot medical facility at the northeast corner of Indiana 32 and Spring Mill Road, combining primary care with specialized services alongside a recently announced endoscopy center at the hospital. The expansion was explicitly tied to Westfield's rapid growth — the city ranked as the sixth-fastest-growing city in the nation according to a 2023 U.S. Census Bureau report. CEO Dave Hyatt said the project "furthers our commitment to providing the community with readily available care." The development involves Innovcare/Innovative Healthcare Real Estate as developer, Wurster Construction as general contractor, Arc Design for architecture, Tesco for civil engineering, and GBC Bank for financing, with completion expected between late 2025 and early 2026 ([Current Publishing](https://youarecurrent.com/2025/05/05/riverview-health-plans-westfield-expansion/)).

## What this profile does not claim

This profile covers the hospital's 2018 opening and 2025 expansion as reported by Current Publishing; it does not independently verify Riverview Health's own historical or patient-volume claims beyond what these two articles state.
