## Overview

Agate is a video game developer headquartered in Bandung, West Java, Indonesia, founded on April 1, 2009 by Aditia Dwiperdana and 17 friends, most of whom had studied at the Bandung Institute of Technology (ITB), after the group's earlier attempts at game-making competitions in 2007 and 2008 had not succeeded ([The Jakarta Post](https://www.thejakartapost.com/news/2017/08/22/agate-studio-story-perseverance-dreamers.html)). Its first commercial release was a zombie-shooting title called "English Defender," which sold for US$500.

## Growth and notable games

Agate began with Flash-based Facebook games before shifting to Android app development in 2013 as mobile gaming grew. By 2017, the studio employed 110 people, had produced more than 200 games and gamification projects, and had won at least 19 awards, including the People's Choice Mochi's Award at the Flash Gaming Summit in San Francisco. It went on to release "Valthirian Arc: Hero School Story" in 2018 for PC, PlayStation 4 and Nintendo Switch, followed by titles including "Memories" (2019) and "Code Atma" (2020) ([The Jakarta Post](https://www.thejakartapost.com/news/2017/08/22/agate-studio-story-perseverance-dreamers.html)). By 2024, under CEO and co-founder Shieny Aprilia, the company employed about 200 people and described itself as Indonesia's largest homegrown game developer, operating within a domestic gaming market valued at $1.79 billion, of which Indonesia represents the largest share among Southeast Asian countries ([The Jakarta Post](https://www.thejakartapost.com/business/2024/08/19/executive-column-agate-gears-up-to-capture-local-global-gaming-market.html)).

## Partnerships and acquisitions

In September 2013, Agate partnered with the Japanese publisher Square Enix to launch the web-based game "Sengoku IXA" in Indonesia; then-CEO Arief Widhiyasa described the deal as a way to accelerate knowledge transfer from an established developer to a newer one ([IDN Times/Duniaku](https://duniaku.idntimes.com/geek/culture/febrianto-nur-anwari/igs-2013-konferensi-pers-agate-studio-dan-square-enix-luncurkan-sengoku-ixa-di-indonesia)). In April 2016, the company raised US$1 million in funding from the Indonesian venture firm Maloekoe Ventures to grow its serious-games and entertainment-games business ([DealStreetAsia](https://www.dealstreetasia.com/stories/indonesian-gaming-dev-agate-studios-raises-1m-from-maloekoe-ventures-36274)). In January 2019, Agate acquired the Bandung studio [Ekuator Games](/organizations/ekuator-games/), developer of "Celestian Tales," for about Rp5 billion, with Ekuator's CEO Cipto Adiguno moving into the role of Agate's Vice President of Consumer Games ([Bisnis Indonesia](https://teknologi.bisnis.com/read/20190126/266/882764/perusahaan-gim-agate-akuisisi-ekuator-games-ini-targetnya-di-kancah-dunia)). In June 2020, it released "Code Atma," an idle role-playing game built around Indonesian folklore creatures such as the kuntilanak and genderuwo, developed in partnership with Telkom Indonesia, Melon and Oolean ([ANTARA News](https://gorontalo.antaranews.com/berita/120702/agate-dan-telkom-rilis-game-mobile-bertema-hantu-indonesia)). In June 2021, Agate acquired Freemergency, a six-person Jakarta studio behind "Retrograde Arena" — winner of the Best Innovation and Rising Star Award at the SEA Game Awards, Level Up KL 2019 — marking its second studio acquisition after Ekuator Games ([Gamebrott](https://gamebrott.com/agate-akuisisi-freemergency-untuk-kembangkan-game-online-multiplayer)).

## Leadership transition

[Arief Widhiyasa](/people/arief-widhiyasa/) served as Agate's CEO through at least 2013 and into 2022. On June 1, 2022, he stepped down to become Chairman, and then-Chief Operating Officer [Shieny Aprilia](/people/shieny-aprilia/) was named CEO, with the company stating a focus on expanding into metaverse development services under her leadership ([Merdeka.com](https://www.merdeka.com/teknologi/agate-umumkan-shieny-aprilia-jadi-ceo-baru-agate.html)). By 2024, as reported above, Shieny Aprilia remained CEO as the company employed about 200 people.

## What this profile does not claim

This profile does not cover Agate's full catalogue of over 200 games and gamification projects, only those specifically named in the sources cited here. Several additional claims surfaced while checking Wikipedia's "Agate (game company)" article as a lead index — including specific corporate clients (Microsoft, Samsung, Coca-Cola), an Electronic Arts partnership, a 2011 "Indonesia Bermain" event, representing Indonesia at the 2012 Tokyo Game Show, and co-founding the Indonesia Game Developer Association (AGI) in 2013 — could not be confirmed through an independently fetched, non-Wikipedia source and are therefore omitted rather than asserted here. It is not to be confused with the unrelated French tabletop role-playing game publisher "Studio Agate," known for "Shadows of Esteren" and its current stewardship of the 7th Sea game line — a distinct company that happens to share a similar name, and is not covered by this profile.
