## Overview

The Zabalaza Theatre Festival is an annual theatre-development programme run by the Baxter
Theatre Centre in Cape Town, in partnership with the Western Cape Department of Cultural
Affairs and Sports (DCAS), and supported by funders including the Oppenheimer Memorial Trust
Fund and the City of Cape Town. It develops theatre practitioners from the Western Cape
through community mini-festivals and script readings that feed into an annual main festival
at the Baxter each March, showcasing around 50 new South African works. A mentorship
component, "Finest of the Fest," awards winning productions a two-week professional season
([Baxter Theatre Centre](https://baxter.uct.ac.za/zabalaza-theatre-festival)).

## 15th edition (2025)

Independent entertainment trade press covered the festival's 15th-anniversary edition,
held 14-22 March 2025 under the theme "Make Your Mark." That edition staged 15 commissioned
productions from emerging artists, with mentorship from established South African theatre
figures including Adrian Kohler, Lara Foot and Yael Farber
([BroadwayWorld South Africa](https://www.broadwayworld.com/south-africa/regional/Baxter-Zabalaza-Theatre-Festival)).

## 16th edition (2026)

The festival's 16th annual award ceremony, curated by Artistic Director Mdu Kweyama, was
held on 21 March 2026. "Kinnes van Somers" (directed by Tamsyn Alexander and Hanro Cloete)
and "Until I Arrive" (by Lindokuhle Melaphi) shared the Best Production award; Mnqobi Molefe
and Yolanda Ntanyana won Best Actor and Best Actress respectively; and Andiswa Anthony won
the Zoleka Helesi Award, given in memory of a late co-founder of the festival. Independent
coverage noted growing evening-performance attendance, with ticket revenue returning
directly to the participating artists and companies
([Theatre Scene Cape Town](https://www.theatrescenecpt.co.za/post/press-winners-16th-zabalaza-theatre-festival)).
