## Overview

Localazy is a Czech software localization and translation-management company founded by Václav Hodek, who had
previously developed Floating Apps, an Android multitasking application that reached 7.5 million downloads
([CzechCrunch](https://cc.cz/kazdy-ma-pravo-pouzivat-mobilni-aplikace-ve-svem-jazyce-veri-vaclav-hodek-jeho-nastroj-localazy-ziskava-desitky-milionu/)).
Hodek launched Localazy in March 2020 to address translation-management problems he had encountered with his
earlier app; within its first month public, the platform secured seed investment from Prague-based Lighthouse
Ventures, whose managing partner Michal Zálešák called it "a bet on a capable entrepreneur who has already
achieved success previously"
([CzechCrunch](https://cc.cz/kazdy-ma-pravo-pouzivat-mobilni-aplikace-ve-svem-jazyce-veri-vaclav-hodek-jeho-nastroj-localazy-ziskava-desitky-milionu/);
[Lupa.cz](https://www.lupa.cz/aktuality/brnenske-localazy-ziskalo-desitky-milionu-na-automaticke-preklady-aplikaci/)).

## Funding

In June 2023, Forbes Czech Republic reported a further funding round of approximately €500,000, led by the
Garage Angels group — including named investors Jiří Hlavenka and Karel Obluk — alongside 12Bullets, JIC Ventures
and returning investor Lighthouse Ventures, based in Slavkov u Brna
([Forbes Czech Republic](https://forbes.cz/zaujali-i-hlavenku-startupiste-od-brna-ziskali-milionovou-investici/)).
That brought the company's total reported funding to nearly €1 million across two rounds, with the company
describing itself as already profitable at the time.

## Expansion

In April 2024, Localazy expanded into the Polish market, with the company naming a client roster spanning
commercial and social-enterprise customers, including ROUVY, Scaleway, Plastic Bank, Donar Online and CARE,
independently reported by both AIN and Tech.eu
([AIN](https://en.ain.ua/2024/04/30/localazy-expands-into-polish-cee-markets/);
[Tech.eu](https://tech.eu/2024/04/30/localazy-brings-its-localised-translation-services-to-polish-brands/)).
