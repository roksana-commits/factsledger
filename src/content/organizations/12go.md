## Overview

12Go is a transport booking platform covering buses, trains, ferries and private transfers across
Southeast Asia, founded in 2012. It now lists more than 4,000 operators across roughly 50,000
routes in 26 Asian countries. In 2021 it joined the Bookaway Group, a travel-technology group whose
other brands include GetByBus and, from March 2022, Plataforma10
([PhocusWire](https://www.phocuswire.com/airasia-partners-12go-ground-transportation)).

## Corporate backing and a major airline partnership

In May 2022, Bookaway Group — 12Go's parent — raised a $35 million Series C funding round led by
Red Dot Capital Partners, bringing its total funding to $80 million under CEO Noam Toister
([PhocusWire](https://www.phocuswire.com/Bookaway-35M-Series-C)). In 2024, 12Go, under its own CEO
Alexey Abolmasov, partnered with AirAsia to add ground-transport booking directly inside AirAsia's
super app, giving 12Go access to the app's roughly 10.6 million monthly users
([PhocusWire](https://www.phocuswire.com/airasia-partners-12go-ground-transportation)).
