## Overview

Janakpur Bolts is a cricket franchise competing in the Nepal Premier League (NPL), Nepal's franchise Twenty20 competition, owned by Dream Sports. The team was launched for the league's inaugural 2024 season as a continuation of the same owner's earlier Lumbini All Stars franchise from the Nepal T20 League; general manager Niraj Khatiwada said the move would "take Nepali cricket to a new height through collaboration with CAN" while continuing "the legacy of Lumbini All Stars" ([The Kathmandu Post](https://kathmandupost.com/cricket/2024/10/02/can-planning-to-develop-npl-into-a-global-premier-event)). Aasif Sheikh served as the franchise's marquee player and captain.

## Inaugural NPL title

On December 21, 2024, Janakpur Bolts won the first Nepal Premier League title, defeating Sudurpaschim Royals by five wickets. Chasing a target of 185, the Bolts completed the run-chase in 19.2 overs, with Lahiru Milantha's 49-ball 87 and captain Aasif Sheikh's 33 off 19 balls leading the innings; Harsh Thaker added an unbeaten 30. Kishore Mahato and Mohammad Mohsin each took three wickets. For Sudurpaschim Royals, Saif Zaib (69) and Binod Bhandari (41) were the main contributors to their total of 184/9 ([LatestLY](https://www.latestly.com/socially/sports/cricket/janakpur-bolts-win-nepal-premier-league-2024-beat-sudurpaschim-royals-by-five-wickets-to-clinch-inaugural-title-6508302.html)).
