## Overview

BasicNeeds was a UK-founded international NGO working on mental health and epilepsy in the
context of poverty and development, founded in 2000 by Chris Underhill MBE. Its model
combined access to community-based mental-health treatment with livelihoods support, stigma
reduction and health-worker training — an approach documented in a 2012 peer-reviewed case
study of its Nepal program in the academic journal PLOS Medicine
([PLOS Medicine](https://ideas.repec.org/a/plo/pmed00/1001261.html)). It operated in
multiple countries across several continents at various points in its history.

## Merger into CBM UK

On 20 July 2017, BasicNeeds merged into CBM UK, a larger UK disability charity, after
failing to secure sufficient funding — its annual income of roughly £1.6 million compared
with CBM UK's approximately £6 million. Some UK-based jobs were cut in the merger, though
overseas program staff were retained, and BasicNeeds' chief executive, Adrian Sell, moved
into CBM UK's leadership
([Third Sector](https://www.thirdsector.co.uk/basicneeds-merged-christian-disability-charity/management/article/1440733)).
BasicNeeds is no longer registered as an independent charity; its programs continue as a
network under CBM UK.
