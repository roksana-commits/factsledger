## Overview

Sheer Logistics is a St. Louis-based managed transportation and freight brokerage company founded in 2009
by Joe Egertson, initially under the name SheerTrans Solutions. Rather than operating as a traditional
freight broker, the company positions itself as a fee-based, carrier- and mode-agnostic managed
transportation provider, offering strategic network analysis, procurement operations, business
intelligence, freight auditing and dock scheduling, built around its proprietary "Sheer Exchange"
integration platform, which the company says can onboard new clients in about eight weeks
([FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/freighttech-titans-invest-in-sheer-logistics-modern-approach-to-managed-transportation)).

## Investment and growth

On 7 October 2022, Woodlawn Partners led a debt and equity co-investment in the company alongside Monroe
Capital LLC, on undisclosed terms. Industry veterans Eddie Leshin and Brian Winshall — both formerly of
American Backhaulers and C.H. Robinson — joined as operating partners and board members, with Winshall
also becoming Chief Revenue Officer. Winshall described the company's positioning at the time: "Managed
transportation is often misrepresented" as basic brokerage work, whereas Sheer delivers "technology-driven,
fee-based solutions" aimed at "efficiency gains, cost reductions, service improvement"
([FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/freighttech-titans-invest-in-sheer-logistics-modern-approach-to-managed-transportation)).
Joel Gard became CEO in January 2024, and that April the company acquired the third-party logistics
provider CargoBarn to expand its brokerage specialties
([FreightWaves](https://www.freightwaves.com/news/sheer-logistics-acquires-cargobarn-to-expand-brokerage-specialties)).
