## Overview

Smiths News PLC is a United Kingdom newspaper and magazine wholesaler. The modern public-company structure emerged when W.H. Smith separated its news-distribution business in 2006, a change reported by the BBC, The Guardian and the Financial Times ([BBC News](http://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/business/5119944.stm) [The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/business/2006/jun/27/retail.money) [Financial Times](https://www.ft.com/content/3b5cb1fe-05a8-11db-9dde-0000779e2340)). Companies House provides the current legal-record context for the company ([Companies House](https://find-and-update.company-information.service.gov.uk/company/05195191)).

## Corporate history

The business has a longer historical connection to W.H. Smith's nineteenth-century news-vending operation, but that lineage should not be confused with the modern Smiths News legal entity. Contemporary reporting in 2006 described the planned separation of the news-distribution arm from W.H. Smith's retail activities. The company later used the Connect Group name during a period of diversification before returning to the Smiths News name, according to trade reporting ([BBC News](http://news.bbc.co.uk/2/hi/business/5119944.stm) [Discover Magazines](https://discovermagazines.co.uk/behind-big-business-smith-news/)).

## Distribution role

Trade coverage describes Smiths News as a major intermediary between publishers and retailers, moving newspapers and magazines through a large distribution network. Discover Magazines reported figures of approximately 21 million items per week and about 24,000 retailers; those figures are retained as dated trade-reporting estimates rather than current guarantees ([Discover Magazines](https://discovermagazines.co.uk/behind-big-business-smith-news/)).

## What this profile does not claim

This profile does not treat the 1792 W.H. Smith origin as the incorporation date of Smiths News PLC. It also does not present market share, distribution-centre counts or retailer numbers as permanent current metrics without a dated source.
