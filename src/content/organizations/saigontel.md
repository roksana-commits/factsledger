## Overview

Saigon Telecommunication & Technologies Corporation, known as Saigontel, is a Vietnamese infrastructure developer established in 2002 and headquartered at Quang Trung Software Park in District 12, Ho Chi Minh City; it began trading on the Ho Chi Minh Stock Exchange under ticker SGT on January 18, 2008 ([Vietstock](https://finance.vietstock.vn/SGT-ctcp-cong-nghe-vien-thong-sai-gon.htm?languageid=2)). The company is chaired by Vietnamese industrialist [Dang Thanh Tam](/people/dang-thanh-tam/), who also chairs the industrial park developer [Kinh Bac City Development Holding Corporation](/organizations/kinh-bac-city-development-holding-corporation/) ([The Investor](https://theinvestor.vn/famous-vietnamese-entrepreneurs-born-in-year-of-the-dragon-d8597.html)). Saigontel is described in its own business press coverage as a member of Saigon Investment Group (SGI).

## From telecom infrastructure to energy and data centres

Saigontel's core business began in telecommunications, information technology and infrastructure investment inside industrial parks and high-tech zones, alongside industrial and civil construction, per Vietstock's financial-data profile of the company. The company has since pushed into energy infrastructure: in March 2020 it signed a joint-venture agreement with SkyX Solar, a subsidiary of the investment firm VinaCapital, to develop more than 50MW of rooftop solar power across an initial 10 industrial parks in central and southern Vietnam, under a "zero capex, zero opex" model in which industrial tenants avoid upfront costs while cutting their energy bills and carbon footprint ([Vietnam Investment Review](https://vir.com.vn/vinacapital-ties-up-with-saigontel-to-develop-50mw-rooftop-solar-power-74783.html)).

More recently, Saigontel has moved into data centre development. On August 28, 2026, at the Tay Ninh Investment Promotion Conference, it was named alongside World Harmony Co., Ltd. and SAIF Eagle II Investment Co., Ltd. as a co-initiating investor in a planned $3.5 billion, 200MW AI data centre complex on about 20 hectares at Nam Tan Tap Industrial Park in Tay Ninh province, with the article describing Saigontel's strategy as organized around "core industrial park infrastructure, energy infrastructure, digital infrastructure and social infrastructure" ([Vietnam Investment Review](https://vir.com.vn/saigontel-joins-international-partners-for-35-billion-ai-data-centre-complex-in-tay-ninh-159749.html)).

## Financial performance and 2026 plans

Saigontel has not paid a shareholder dividend since 2010, instead reinvesting retained earnings into expansion. In 2025, net revenue reached about VND1.86 trillion ($70.65 million), up 1% year over year, while pre-tax profit more than doubled to nearly VND499.9 billion, about 25.5% above the company's own target. For 2026, Saigontel is targeting record net revenue of about VND2 trillion ($75.96 million), a 7.8% increase, and pre-tax profit of about VND500 billion, while pursuing a 32.4-hectare liquified natural gas port and storage project estimated at VND10 trillion and a rights offering of more than 148 million shares intended to roughly double the company's charter capital, partly to fund a stake in Vietnam Green Growth JSC ([The Investor](https://theinvestor.vn/saigontel-eyes-record-revenue-accelerates-lng-data-center-development-in-2026-d18855.html)).
