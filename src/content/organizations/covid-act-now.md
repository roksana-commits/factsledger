## Overview

Covid Act Now was a COVID-19 data and risk-tracking website, covidactnow.org, launched on 20 March 2020 by Max
Henderson, a former Google data scientist; Jonathan Kreiss-Tomkins, an Alaska state legislator; and Igor Kofman, a
former chief technology officer of Dropbox Paper
([Yale Daily News](https://yaledailynews.com/blog/2020/04/02/new-tool-helps-policymakers-address-covid-19/)). The
site drew roughly 8 million visitors in its first week and grew into an operation of about 50, largely volunteer,
staff, with support from James Tamplin, a co-founder of Firebase, who helped secure nonprofit status and lead
fundraising, and with input from Georgetown University's Center for Global Health Science and Security and
Stanford University's Clinical Excellence Research Center
([University of Wisconsin–Madison](https://engineering.wisc.edu/news/model-response-alum-helps-launch-covid-data-hub)).
The underlying organization registered as the 501(c)(3) nonprofit Act Now Coalition
([ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/850613766)).

## Use during the pandemic

In March 2020, Dallas County Judge Clay Jenkins cited a projection chart from covidactnow.org — showing that, under
only social-distancing measures, Texas hospitals could be overloaded by late April with as many as 430,000 deaths
statewide — in justifying a shelter-in-place order
([CBS News DFW](https://www.cbsnews.com/dfw/news/accurate-coronavirus-curve-graphs/)). The same report noted that
health-data expert Harry Glorikian cautioned that testing shortfalls limited the accuracy of any model at the time,
while also noting that "every projection... is going to outstrip the number of beds we have in the United States"
([CBS News DFW](https://www.cbsnews.com/dfw/news/accurate-coronavirus-curve-graphs/)). In 2022, a paper on public
health surveillance indicators published via the Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences, associated with
Carnegie Mellon University's Delphi Group, cited Covid Act Now as one of the public dashboards built using CDC
testing-positivity data
([PNAS / Delphi Group](https://www.stat.cmu.edu/~ryantibs/papers/covidcast-indicators.pdf)).

## Financial history

IRS filings compiled by ProPublica's Nonprofit Explorer show Act Now Coalition's reported annual revenue as
approximately $1.55 million in 2020, $1.98 million in 2021, $1.54 million in 2022, $711,000 in 2023, and
approximately $10.05 million in 2024
([ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer](https://projects.propublica.org/nonprofits/organizations/850613766)). No source
read for this profile ties the 2024 increase to COVID-19 work specifically.

## Winding down

The project's own GitHub repository states that it "is archived and winding down," with data snapshots frozen as
of May 2024, and that its authenticated API is scheduled for permanent shutoff on 11 March 2026
([Act Now Coalition, GitHub](https://github.com/act-now-coalition/covid-data-model)). The same organization's
current website describes ongoing work on a voter-registration project unrelated to COVID-19 tracking.

## What this profile does not claim

A co-founder's own biography states that Covid Act Now "advised officials in 25+ states and the military" and
"collaborated with the CDC and White House." No independent source corroborating those specific claims was found,
so they are not repeated here. No named major donor is identified; only aggregate annual revenue figures from
public tax filings are available.
