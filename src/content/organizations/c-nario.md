## Overview

C-nario was an Israeli digital-signage and video-wall software company founded in 2002 by
Rami Bahar as a spin-off of Disk-In, backed by Carmel Ventures (now Viola Ventures) and Opus
Capital. Its products, including C-nario Messenger, managed and distributed digital-signage
content for clients such as banks, airports and stock exchanges
([AVNetwork](https://www.avnetwork.com/avnetwork/black-box-network-services-receives-circle-of-excellence-award-from-shoretel)).

## Acquisition

On 27 October 2011, C-nario was acquired by YCD Multimedia in a stock transaction,
accompanied by a $6 million funding round led by Carmel Ventures and Opus Capital. The
combined company claimed to serve more than 2,000 brands across over 40 countries — a
figure the independent trade columnist Dave Haynes greeted with open skepticism at the time
([Digital Signage Today](https://www.digitalsignagetoday.com/news/ycd-picks-up-c-nario/);
[Sixteen:Nine](https://invidis.com/sixteen-nine/2011/10/27/more-consolidation-ycd-buys-c-nario/)).
