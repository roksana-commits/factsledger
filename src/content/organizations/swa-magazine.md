## Overview

SWA Magazine (published as Majalah SWA, and also operating as SWA Media Inc.) is an Indonesian business media organization best known not for a single flagship story but for a recurring calendar of corporate-recognition award programs that Indonesian national and regional press cover year after year. Independent reporting identifies Kemal Effendi Gani as the publication's editor-in-chief and, in later coverage, its Group Chief Editor, in this role from at least September 2018 through November 2024 ([Harian Times](https://hariantimes.com/read-392-2018-09-13-kemal-gani-insya-allah-kami-akan-dapat-jalankan-amanah-ini-.html); [JPNN.com](https://m.jpnn.com/news/belasan-perusahaan-ini-raih-penghargaan-dari-majalah-swa-dan-business-digest)). In September 2018, Harian Times reported that Gani, then identified as SWA's editor-in-chief, was elected chairman of the Forum Pemimpin Redaksi (Forum Pemred), a professional association of Indonesian editors, for the 2018-2021 term, after winning 22 of 38 votes in a Jakarta election.

## Award and recognition programs

SWA's most consistently documented activity is organizing named business-recognition programs. In November 2025, Antara and CNN Indonesia both reported that SWA magazine, jointly with the corporate-governance body Indonesia Independent Cipta Governansi (IICG), held the Corporate Governance Perception Index (CGPI) Award 2025 ceremony in Jakarta on November 25, 2025, at which state bank Bank Negara Indonesia (BNI) received a "The Most Trusted Company" designation for governance, transparency and risk-management practices ([Antara](https://www.antaranews.com/berita/5297989/bni-meraih-predikat-the-most-trusted-company-pada-cgpi-award-2025); [CNN Indonesia](https://www.cnnindonesia.com/ekonomi/20251129131727-625-1300808/bni-raih-predikat-the-most-trusted-company-di-ajang-cgpi-award-2025)). A year earlier, in November 2024, JPNN.com reported that SWA magazine and its associated Business Digest jointly presented the Indonesia Most Reputable Companies Award 2024, Indonesia Most Reputable Companies Champions 2024 and Indonesia Digital Marketing Champions 2024 to more than a dozen companies across sectors including healthcare, insurance, banking and e-commerce, with Gani describing the awards as recognizing companies that build and maintain reputation while succeeding in digital marketing. Separately, RM.id reported in July 2021 that Gani, by then described as SWA's Group Chief Editor, set out selection criteria during a webinar for SWA's "Indonesia Living Legend Companies and Indonesia Living Legend Brands" program, which requires honored companies and brands to be at least 50 years old, still growing, and major players in their industry ([RM.id](https://rm.id/baca-berita/etalase-bisnis/85033/rahasia-perusahaan-tetap-bertahan-di-usia-50-tahun-lebih)).

## The Young Woman Future Business Leaders award

SWA also organizes a recurring "Young Woman Future Business Leaders" competition aimed at recognizing emerging female business leaders in Indonesia. NusaBali reported that SWA's 2018 edition, decided at a ceremony at the Shangri-La Hotel Jakarta on May 31, 2018, named Ni Kadek Winie Kaori — founder and CEO of PT Sasjam Riri (Kaori Group) — as winner over finalists from the banking, transportation and telecommunications sectors, based on judging by a panel drawn from Astra International, Multistrada, Accenture Indonesia and Binus Business School, with a scholarship for an Executive Master of Management program at Binus Business School as the prize; NusaBali described the competition's aim as strengthening the role of female business leaders in Indonesian companies and institutions ([NusaBali](https://www.nusabali.com/berita/31767/kadek-kaori-raih-award-young-woman-future-business-leaders-2018)). This is the same award program that, according to a source used on this site's profile of [Shieny Aprilia](/people/shieny-aprilia/), recognized her in 2014 as an "Indonesia Young Woman Future Business Leaders" honoree while she was a co-founder and rising manager at the Bandung game developer Agate; this profile independently confirms the award program's existence and recurrence but did not locate an independent source naming Aprilia as a recipient (see "What this profile does not claim").

## What this profile does not claim

This profile does not state SWA magazine's founding date, founders or original publisher, even though Wikipedia's English and Indonesian articles describe a 1985 founding under the name "Swasembada." Those claims were used only to identify what to look for, and because no independent, non-Wikipedia source confirming that history could be located and fetched during this research pass, they are left out rather than restated secondhand from Wikipedia. This profile also does not independently confirm Shieny Aprilia's reported 2014 "Indonesia Young Woman Future Business Leaders" award, which on this site rests on a single company-adjacent interview rather than on any source used here. Finally, this profile treats "SWA Media Inc.," "Majalah SWA" and "PT Swasembada Media Bisnis" as referring to the same overall media organization, consistent with how the independent sources read use these names, but it could not confirm the precise legal relationship between them.
