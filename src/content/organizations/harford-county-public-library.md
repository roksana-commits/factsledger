## Overview

Harford County Public Library (HCPL) is the public library system serving Harford County,
Maryland, tracing its origin to 1885, when the Ladies Auxiliary of Emmanuel Church opened
the Bel Air Circulating Library on Main Street with 200 books
([Harford County Public Library](https://hcplonline.org/belairhistory.php)). The Harford
County Library Association formed in 1912 and opened the county's first free library in two
rooms above a Main Street tailor shop; the Harford County Library Board of Trustees assumed
control of the system in 1946.

## Scale and leadership

As of 2015, the system operated 11 branches with more than 187,000 registered borrowers and
annual circulation exceeding 4.4 million items
([Patch](https://patch.com/maryland/belair/harford-county-public-library-receives-10000-grant-comcast-foundation)).
Mary Hastler has served as CEO since 2010. The Daily Record profiled her in 2019, when she
credited the library's early adoption of e-books, e-audiobooks and free certificate courses
through platforms including Lynda.com, Cisco Networking Academy and Rosetta Stone as central
to reshaping the library's public image
([The Daily Record](https://thedailyrecord.com/2019/04/02/mary-hastler-3/)). In 2020, the
University of Maryland's College of Information, citing Baltimore Sun coverage, reported
Hastler's election as chair of the Maryland Humanities board, her Maryland Library
Association Outstanding Member Award (2018) and Daily Record Icon Honors Award (2018), and
the library's recognition as "Innovator of the Year" four times under her leadership
([University of Maryland](https://info.umd.edu/news/baltimore-sun-harford-county-library-ceo-named-new-chairwoman-maryland-humanities-board)).

## Programs and pandemic response

In April 2015, the Comcast Foundation awarded the library a $10,000 grant to expand its
"Little Leapers 3.5" digital-literacy camp, a program for preschoolers built around
tablets loaded with STEAM-focused educational apps, aimed at teaching both children and
their caregivers healthy technology habits
([Patch](https://patch.com/maryland/belair/harford-county-public-library-receives-10000-grant-comcast-foundation)).
In March 2020, the library closed all branches through the end of the month as a COVID-19
precaution. The Baltimore Sun/Aegis quoted Hastler calling it "a difficult decision," and
reported that the library waived fines and fees and extended due dates while maintaining
24/7 digital access to e-books, films and research databases through its website
([The Baltimore Sun/Aegis](https://www.baltimoresun.com/coronavirus/cng-ag-harford-libraries-closed-20200313-rvdwumlx4bez7itbdzfd35lhqi-story.html)).
