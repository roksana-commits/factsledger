## Overview

Clyde Metro is a proposed, multi-decade, multi-billion-pound mass transit system for the Glasgow City Region, first identified as a national transport investment priority in Transport Scotland's Strategic Transport Projects Review 2 (STPR2), published in 2022 ([Glasgow City Council](https://www.glasgow.gov.uk/article/2558/Clyde-Metro)). It envisages an integrated network combining existing rail and Glasgow Subway infrastructure with new lines, and is being developed across eight local authorities in the Glasgow City Region ([Glasgow City Council](https://www.glasgow.gov.uk/article/2558/Clyde-Metro)). The National Wealth Fund, which has provided advisory support on funding and governance options, describes the project's potential to connect more than 1.5 million people to employment, education and health services over a roughly 30-year investment horizon ([National Wealth Fund](https://www.nationalwealthfund.org.uk/news-and-publications/local-government-case-studies/delivering-better-connectivity-through-mass-transit-in-glasgow/)).

## Leadership and funding

On November 24, 2023, Strathclyde Partnership for Transport (SPT) was designated to lead development of Clyde Metro's Case for Investment (CFI) — described as the project's next critical phase, establishing a strategic business case and arrangements for initial delivery. Glasgow City Council leader Susan Aitken called Clyde Metro "the transformational, ambitious and deliverable public transport system that the Glasgow metropolitan region has been waiting for" ([Glasgow City Council](https://www.glasgow.gov.uk/article/7812/Clyde-Metro-Development-Takes-Major-Step-Forward)). On March 5, 2024, the Glasgow City Region approved £12.155 million in City Deal funding — split equally between the Scottish and UK Governments — to accelerate CFI development, with SPT adding a further £2.2 million of its own ([Glasgow City Council](https://www.glasgow.gov.uk/article/8401/City-Deal-funding-to-advance-Clyde-Metro-development)). No funding beyond this development stage has been secured.

## Current status as of September 2026

CFI Stage 1 was completed in March 2025, producing a "case for change," a vision and objectives, and a set of corridor-based network options for further study ([Glasgow City Council](https://www.glasgow.gov.uk/article/2558/Clyde-Metro)). As of SPT's most recent published board papers, from November 2025, the project was in CFI Stage 2: a Preliminary Options Appraisal of corridor alignment options was under way, an Environmental Impact Assessment consultation with statutory bodies had been completed and was under review, and Equality Impact Assessment scoping had identified accessibility and multi-modal integration as key themes. SPT told its board that public consultation on shortlisted network options was planned for February-March 2026, and that delivery was "on track and on budget," with the full Case for Investment expected to complete in early 2027 ([SPT](https://www.spt.co.uk/media/y11bv4lf/sp281125_agenda10.pdf)). Transport Scotland's most recent national delivery-plan update lists continued Clyde Metro CFI development as one of SPT's stated priorities for 2026 ([Transport Scotland](https://www.transport.gov.scot/publication/national-transport-strategy-fifth-delivery-plan/regional-transport-partnerships-2025-progress-and-2026-priorities/)). That same Transport Scotland document separately describes SPT as "aiming to deliver a transformational mass transit system by 2027" — looser wording that sits awkwardly against SPT's and Glasgow City Council's own, more precise statements that only the business case, not the built system, is due by that date.

## Relationship to the Glasgow Airport rail link

Transport Scotland's 2022 Clyde Metro proposal subsequently absorbed the long-running, twice-scrapped [Glasgow Airport Link](/places/glasgow-airport-link/) as one of many components under evaluation, rather than as a standalone project with its own funding or timeline. As of September 2026, no source read for this profile identifies which specific mode, corridor or phase of the eventual Clyde Metro network an airport connection would occupy.

## What this profile does not claim

This profile does not claim that any version of Clyde Metro will be built, since as of its most recent independently verified status the scheme remains in business-case development with no construction funding secured. It does not resolve the discrepancy between Transport Scotland's 2027 "delivery" language and SPT's and Glasgow City Council's more precise account of 2027 as only the Case for Investment's completion date, and it does not attempt to predict which of the network options under appraisal will ultimately be chosen.
