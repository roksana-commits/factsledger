## Overview

The Cathedral of Saint Joseph is a Chaldean Catholic cathedral in Ankawa, a Christian-majority district of Erbil in the Iraqi Kurdistan Region.

## Sheltering displaced Christians, 2014

In 2014, as the Islamic State advanced across the Nineveh Plain, the cathedral sheltered and held Mass for thousands of displaced Christians, documented in independent international photojournalism from the period ([CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/pictures/the-displaced-christians-of-erbil-iraq/)).

## 2018 Vatican visit

In December 2018, Cardinal Pietro Parolin, the Vatican's Secretary of State, celebrated Holy Mass at this cathedral during an official visit to Iraq, according to the Holy See's own press bulletin ([Holy See Press Office](https://press.vatican.va/content/salastampa/en/bollettino/pubblico/2018/12/27/181227a.pdf)).

## What this profile does not claim

This profile is limited to two independently verified events — the cathedral's role sheltering displaced Christians in 2014 and a 2018 Vatican visit — each confirmed by a source read in full. It does not assert the cathedral's construction date, founding archbishop, or architectural details, which circulate in weaker, unverified reference sources; a separate article describing a different, newer church in Ankawa was identified during research and excluded here to avoid misattributing its claims to this cathedral.
