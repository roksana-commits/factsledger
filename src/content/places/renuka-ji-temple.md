## Overview

Renuka Lake and its adjoining temple sit in Sirmaur district, Himachal Pradesh, India, about
37 km from Nahan and 60 km from Paonta Sahib
([Sirmaur District Administration](https://hpsirmaur.nic.in/tourist-place/renuka-ji/)). This
profile covers that physical lake-temple complex and the pilgrimage activity built around it —
not Renuka as an abstract figure in Hindu mythology, who in tradition is the consort of the sage
Jamadagni and mother of Parashurama.

The lake itself is horseshoe-shaped and, according to the district government's own tourism
page, is ringed by boating and fishing facilities operated by the state tourism department, a
government-run mini zoo housing species such as the Himalayan black bear and barking deer, and
trekking routes into the surrounding forest
([Sirmaur District Administration](https://hpsirmaur.nic.in/tourist-place/renuka-ji/)).

## Ramsar wetland status

On 8 November 2005, the wetland at Renuka Lake was formally designated a Wetland of
International Importance under the Ramsar Convention. The Ramsar Sites Information Service
records the designated site as 20 hectares, fed by a stream flowing into the Giri river, and
notes that it supports 103 recorded bird species and 19 fish species. The same record states
that the lake "has high religious significance and is named after the mother of Hindu sage
Parshuram," and that it is managed under a dual designation as a Reserve Forest and Wildlife
Sanctuary ([Ramsar Sites Information Service](https://rsis.ramsar.org/ris/1571)).

## The Shri Renuka Ji Fair

The site hosts an annual multi-day fair timed to Kartik Purnima. In 2024 the fair ran for five
days, from 11 to 15 November. Ahead of it, Sirmaur Deputy Speaker Vinay Kumar chaired a planning
meeting with Deputy Commissioner Sumit Khimta and Nahan Sub-Divisional Magistrate Saleem Azam
(secretary of the fair committee), covering plastic-free enforcement, streetlighting, traffic
and parking, healthcare deployment, a dedicated bathing ghat for women, and security
([The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/himachal/deputy-speaker-admn-chalk-out-plan-for-shri-renuka-ji-fair/)).

Himachal Pradesh Chief Minister Sukhvinder Singh Sukhu inaugurated that year's fair on 12
November 2024 by carrying the traditional palanquin at Jamu Koti village, a ritual The Tribune's
coverage frames around the fair's founding narrative: that King Sahasrabahu killed the sage
Jamadagni after being refused the sage's divine cow, that a grief-stricken Renuka drowned
herself in the lake, and that Jamadagni's son Parashurama avenged his father and vowed to be
reunited with his mother at the site each year — a reunion the fair is said to commemorate
([The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/himachal/5-day-renuka-ji-fair-gets-underway/)).

## What this profile does not claim

No source read for this profile gives a construction date or architectural history for the
temple building itself, so none is asserted here. The two government sources used here also
give different figures for the site's "area" — 2.5 km on the district tourism page versus 20
hectares in the Ramsar record — and this profile does not attempt to reconcile them, since they
may describe different boundaries (a tourist zone versus a formally delimited wetland). No
fair-attendance figures were located in the sources used, and no year-by-year fair history
beyond 2024 is asserted.
