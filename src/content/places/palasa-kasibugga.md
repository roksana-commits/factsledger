## Overview

Palasa-Kasibugga is a municipality of twin towns in Srikakulam district, Andhra Pradesh, India. At the 2011 Census of India, its population was recorded at 57,507, with a literacy rate of 75.68%, above the state average of 67.02% ([City Population](https://citypopulation.de/en/india/andhrapradesh/srikakulam/2841101000__palasa_kasibugga/)).

## 2025 temple stampede

On 1 November 2025, a stampede at the privately run Sri Venkateswara Swamy temple in Kasibugga killed nine people, eight women and a boy, according to India's official government news service ([News on AIR](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/stampede-at-venkateswara-swamy-temple-at-kashibugga-in-andhra-pradesh-claims-9-lives)). The temple, built to hold around 3,000 people, was overwhelmed by roughly 25,000 devotees who had gathered for Ekadashi observances; an iron grill along the single shared entry-and-exit passage collapsed near a steep stairway, triggering the crush, according to Andhra Pradesh's minister for medical and health as reported by The Federal ([The Federal](https://thefederal.com/category/states/south/andhra-pradesh/venkateswara-temple-stampede-srikakulam-214173)).

In the aftermath, police registered a case under the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita and barred further entry to the temple, after finding that its organizers had neither sought the mandatory permissions nor requested police protection for the event, according to The Tribune ([The Tribune](https://www.tribuneindia.com/news/india/day-after-9-deaths-in-stampede-police-bar-entry-at-kasibugga-temple)). Chief Minister N. Chandrababu Naidu ordered a full investigation and directed officials to ensure speedy medical care for the injured ([News on AIR](https://www.newsonair.gov.in/stampede-at-venkateswara-swamy-temple-at-kashibugga-in-andhra-pradesh-claims-9-lives)).
