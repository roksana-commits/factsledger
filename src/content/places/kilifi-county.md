## Overview

Kilifi County is a coastal county in Kenya, formed in 2010 through the merger of Kilifi District and Malindi District. It had a population of 1,453,787 across 12,245.9 square kilometres, with Kilifi town as its capital and Malindi as its largest town ([Council of Governors, Kenya](https://maarifa.cog.go.ke/node/803)).

## Geography and heritage

The county lies north and northeast of Mombasa along Kenya's Indian Ocean coast and includes tourist beach areas such as Kikambala, Watamu, Malindi and Kilifi. It is also home to the Gedi ruins, a historical site of mosques and tombs dating from the 11th to the 17th century ([Council of Governors, Kenya](https://maarifa.cog.go.ke/node/803)).

## 2025 New York Times travel list

In 2025, Kilifi, Kenya was named on the New York Times' annual "52 Places to Go" travel list. Independent accounts of the list differ on its exact numbered position: Time Out reports it as the 31st entry, while The Travel Sisters report it as 32nd ([Time Out](https://www.timeout.com/news/these-are-the-52-places-to-go-in-2025-according-to-the-new-york-times-010725); [The Travel Sisters](https://thetravelsisters.com/new-york-times-52-places-to-go-in-2025/)).
