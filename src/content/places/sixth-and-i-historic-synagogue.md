## Overview

Sixth & I Historic Synagogue is a 1908 synagogue building in Washington, D.C. that was restored and reimagined in 2004 as a nonmembership Jewish congregation and cultural venue, blending religious services with concerts, comedy and public talks.

## Construction and early history

Adas Israel Congregation purchased the corner lot at 6th and I Streets NW in 1905 and began construction the following year; the sanctuary, built in a mix of Moorish, Romanesque and Byzantine Revival styles with three domes, was dedicated on 6 January 1908 ([Sixth & I](https://www.sixthandi.org/about/history/)). This early history is independently corroborated by an account in a Times of Israel blog post ([The Times of Israel](https://blogs.timesofisrael.com/a-synagogue-reimagined-as-a-cultural-hub-sixth-i-washington-dc/)).

## From church to cultural venue

After Adas Israel relocated to upper Northwest Washington in 1945, Turner Memorial A.M.E. Church purchased the building in 1951 and used it for more than fifty years. When the building went up for sale in 2002, three local developers bought it within 24 hours to prevent its loss, and a restoration culminated in an April 2004 rededication as Sixth & I, a nonmembership synagogue and cultural center ([Sixth & I](https://www.sixthandi.org/about/history/); [The Times of Israel](https://blogs.timesofisrael.com/a-synagogue-reimagined-as-a-cultural-hub-sixth-i-washington-dc/)).

## Programming and attendance

Sixth & I draws about 80,000 visitors a year across a program that mixes Jewish religious services with stand-up comedy, concerts, book talks and podcast recordings; its High Holy Days services sell out above 3,000 people, and roughly 10,000 people attend Sabbath services annually. Its ticketed, nonmembership model has drawn speakers including Ruth Bader Ginsburg, Elizabeth Warren and Caitlyn Jenner, and other synagogue leaders have said they loosened their own programming because of Sixth & I's influence, according to a 2018 syndicated newspaper feature ([Arkansas Democrat-Gazette](https://www.pressreader.com/usa/arkansas-democrat-gazette/20180106/281870118828554)).
