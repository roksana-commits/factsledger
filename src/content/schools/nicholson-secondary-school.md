## Overview

**Nicholson Secondary School**, also known as Nicholson College or Nicholson Higher Secondary School, is a private, English-medium co-educational institution located in the Sallaghari area of Bhaktapur, Nepal [Edusanjal](https://edusanjal.com/school/nicholson-higher-secondary-school/) [Collegenp](https://www.collegenp.com/school/nicholson-secondary-school). The school's campus is situated in Sallaghari-1, Bhaktapur, reflecting its proximity to the administrative boundaries of Suryabinayak and Bhaktapur municipalities [Edusanjal](https://edusanjal.com/school/nicholson-higher-secondary-school/) [Colleges Nepal](https://www.collegesnepal.com/nicholson-college-hss/). Established in 1999 A.D., the school has completed over two decades of operation in the education sector [Collegenp](https://www.collegenp.com/school/nicholson-secondary-school). It was named in honor of Sir Charles Nicholson, a prominent figure known for his contributions to the establishment of the University of Sydney [Official Website](https://www.nicholson.edu.np/).

## Recognition

The institution is recognized for its commitment to international standards in education. Nicholson Secondary School received the **British Council ISA Full Award** for the documented 2019-20 cycle [British Council Nepal](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf). The award was officially presented by the British Council Nepal in October 2020, acknowledging the school's efforts to embed an international dimension into its curriculum and foster global citizenship among its students [British Council Nepal](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf) [Spotlight Nepal](https://www.spotlightnepal.com/2020/10/21/23-nepali-schools-receive-british-councils-international-school-award-2020/).

While the school maintains active leadership across its academic wings, official documentation reviewed for this profile does not explicitly confirm the specific name of the current Principal for the Secondary wing [Official Website](https://www.nicholson.edu.np/). ## Educational programmes

A distinguishing feature of the school is its adoption of the **Rato Bangala Foundation (RBF) pedagogy**, which emphasizes child-centered, active learning methodologies designed to foster critical thinking and holistic development [Official Facebook Page](https://www.facebook.com/nicholsonschoolandKinderworld/). The school offers a comprehensive range of academic programs:

*   **Nicholson Kinderworld**: A dedicated preschool wing offering classes from Playgroup (PG) to Preparatory-II (Prep-II) [Edusanjal](https://edusanjal.com/school/nicholson-higher-secondary-school/).
*   **Secondary School**: Comprehensive education from Grade 1 through Grade 10, affiliated with the National Examinations Board (NEB) [Edusanjal](https://edusanjal.com/school/nicholson-higher-secondary-school/) [Colleges Nepal](https://www.collegesnepal.com/nicholson-college-hss/).
*   **Higher Secondary (+2)**: Specializing in the Management stream, with options for Computer Science, Hotel Management, and Business Studies [Colleges Nepal](https://www.collegesnepal.com/nicholson-college-hss/) [Shiksha Sanjal](https://shikshasanjal.com/college/nicholson-college-bhaktapur).
*   **Bachelor Level**: A Bachelor of Business Studies (BBS) program affiliated with Tribhuvan University [Official Website](https://www.nicholson.edu.np/).

To support these programs, the school provides an online library system and specialized labs for Hotel Management and Computer Science [Official Website](https://www.nicholson.edu.np/) [Shiksha Sanjal](https://shikshasanjal.com/college/nicholson-college-bhaktapur).
