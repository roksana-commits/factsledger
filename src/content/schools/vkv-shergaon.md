## Overview

Vivekananda Kendra Vidyalaya Shergaon (VKV Shergaon) is a senior secondary residential school located in Shergaon village, West Kameng District, Arunachal Pradesh [1]. Established in 1981, the institution is managed by the Vivekananda Kendra Vidyalayas Arunachal Pradesh Trust (VKVAPT) [1] [4]. The school occupies a campus area of 60,702 square meters and provides educational facilities for students from Class I to XII [3] [4].

## Recognition and accreditation

The school is formally affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), holding affiliation number 2230024 and school code 35023 [1]. It is recognized as a senior secondary level institution [1]. In the 2022-23 academic session, the school reported a 100% pass rate for both Class X and Class XII Board Examinations [3].

## Leadership and governance

VKV Shergaon is governed by the VKV Arunachal Pradesh Trust (VKVAPT) [4]. As of 2026, the principal of the institution is Vijay Kumar Tehariya [2]. The school maintains mandatory public disclosures regarding its infrastructure and academic staffing, reporting a faculty of 19 teachers as of 2023 [3].

## Academic programmes

The institution offers academic programmes from primary to senior secondary levels [4]. At the senior secondary level (Classes XI and XII), the school provides Science streams, specifically Physics-Chemistry-Mathematics (PCM) and Physics-Chemistry-Biology (PCB) [4]. The campus includes specialized facilities such as five laboratories and fifteen classrooms to support these programmes [3].

## Community activity

The school actively participates in national and regional celebrations, including the 150th Vande Mataram anniversary in January 2026 and the 80th Independence Day in August 2026 [5]. Its location in Shergaon is occasionally highlighted in regional media during local environmental events, such as significant snowfall [7]. In 2026, the school was part of broader public discussions reported by *The Arunachal Times* regarding fee adjustments within the VKV network in the state [6].

## Research or professional work

Students at VKV Shergaon have achieved recognition in national scientific platforms. Notably, in 2013, student Km. Habung Asun's project was selected among the "Top 20 Science Projects" at the National Children's Science Congress (NCSC) held in Bhopal [8].
