## Overview

Yuhuatai Senior High School is a public senior high school in Yuhuatai District, Nanjing, Jiangsu Province, China. In 2022 it was selected as a "High-Quality Demonstration High School" cultivator candidate, with full provincial recommendation following in 2023; reporting describes it as the only school in Nanjing to have received the region's "Outstanding Teaching Quality Contribution Award" nine times across eight years ([Sina Finance](https://finance.sina.cn/2023-05-31/detail-imyvrxmp0505147.d.html)). In the 2022 admissions cycle, 85% of its students improved their high-school entrance exam ranking by more than 2,500 positions compared with their junior-high placement ranking, and its graduates have been admitted to universities including Tsinghua, Tongji, Renmin, Nanjing University and Southeast University ([Sina Finance](https://finance.sina.cn/2023-05-31/detail-imyvrxmp0505147.d.html)).

## 2026 admissions expansion

In May 2026, the school became one of 13 Nanjing schools recruiting citywide rather than only from its home district for the first time — up from 9 schools with citywide recruitment the previous year. At the school's open day, Party Secretary Zhao Guanghui spoke on the school's approach to admissions, Admissions Director Huang Guiqiang addressed applicants, and Ao Cheng, Vice Director of the Nanjing Municipal Education and Enrollment Examination Institute, explained the citywide policy change. Coverage reported that applicants ranked 15,000th or better citywide had "very strong hope" of admission, compared with roughly the 10,700th position the previous year under the school's former district-only admissions ([Yangzi Evening News](https://www.yzwb.net/news/shaonianhi/202605/t20260517_353737.html)).
