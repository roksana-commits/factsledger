# Regent Residential School

## Overview
Regent Residential School is a child-centered educational institution located in Dhapakhel, Lalitpur, Nepal [1](https://www.regentschool.edu.np/) [2](https://edusanjal.com/school/regent-international-school/). Established in 2010 (2067 B.S.), the school focuses on experiential learning and global educational standards [1](https://www.regentschool.edu.np/). The institution maintains its primary campus in Dhapakhel (Lalitpur-23) and secondary facilities in the Nakhipot and Ramailotar areas (Lalitpur-14) [1](https://www.regentschool.edu.np/).

## Recognition
The school received the British Council ISA Full Award for the documented 2019–2020 cycle [3](https://www.spotlightnepal.com/2020/10/21/23-nepali-schools-receive-british-councils-international-school-award-2020/) [5](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/programmes/education/connecting-classrooms/international-school-award). This recognition was officially announced in October 2020, highlighting the institution's efforts to embed international dimensions within its curriculum [3](https://www.spotlightnepal.com/2020/10/21/23-nepali-schools-receive-british-councils-international-school-award-2020/). The ISA initiatives at the school were coordinated by Vice-Principal Ramji Aacharya [1](https://www.regentschool.edu.np/).

## Leadership
The institution is led by Founder and Director Nawaraj Sanjel, with Dr. Vidhya Pokhrel serving as the Principal [1](https://www.regentschool.edu.np/). The leadership team emphasizes a mission to foster curiosity and critical thinking skills beyond traditional textbook learning [1](https://www.regentschool.edu.np/).

## Educational programmes
Regent Residential School utilizes a modern pedagogical framework, including the 5E Instruction Model (Engage, Explore, Explain, Elaborate, and Evaluate) and Inquiry-Based Pedagogy [1](https://www.regentschool.edu.np/). The curriculum is designed to encourage problem-centered experimentation and hands-on activities [1](https://www.regentschool.edu.np/). Additionally, the school mandates Fine Art classes for all students to promote creativity and self-expression [1](https://www.regentschool.edu.np/).

The school is an authorized operator of the Duke of Edinburgh’s International Award, providing students with opportunities for skill development and community service [1](https://www.regentschool.edu.np/). In February 2024, the institution established a partnership with the Confucius Institute at Kathmandu University to facilitate cultural and linguistic exchange programs [4](https://www.facebook.com/RegentResidentialSchool/).

## Community activity
The school hosts the Regent Model United Nations (MUN), an institutional conference that provides students with a platform for diplomatic training and discussion of global issues [1](https://www.regentschool.edu.np/) [4](https://www.facebook.com/RegentResidentialSchool/). Furthermore, the school has documented educational trips to South Korea and maintains partnerships with schools in the United Kingdom to enhance its international ethos [1](https://www.regentschool.edu.np/).
