# Overview

St. Francis de Sales Higher Secondary School, Dhemaji (SFS Dhemaji), is a prominent Catholic missionary co-educational institution located in Dhemaji, Assam, India [1] [2]. Established in **1979** during the International Year of the Child, the school was founded by **Fr. Varkey Valiadathu** following an invitation from the Bishop of Tezpur to establish a mission in the region [1] [2]. The school is managed by the **Missionaries of St. Francis de Sales (MSFS)**, specifically under the Dibrugarh Province [1].

The institution began in a modest "Assam type" building with only 12 students and has since grown into a significant educational hub in the Dhemaji district [1]. It provides education from primary through senior secondary levels, maintaining a central location in Dhemaji town on land originally procured from the local community [1].

# Recognition and accreditation

SFS Dhemaji is officially recognized by the **Government of Assam** and holds valid affiliations with state education boards. The school is listed in the official affiliation records of the **Assam State School Education Board (ASSEB)** for the 2026-27 academic session [3].

# Leadership and governance

The school operates as a Christian minority private institution under the governance of the **Bishop of Tezpur**, administered through a registered charitable society [1]. The **Missionaries of St. Francis de Sales (MSFS)** provide the administrative and spiritual leadership, with the Dibrugarh Province overseeing the school's ministry [1].

The founding Principal, **Fr. Varkey Valiadathu**, served from 1979 to 1985, laying the foundation for the school's growth [1]. The institution's governance structure ensures adherence to both missionary values and the educational standards set by the Assam state government [1] [2].

# Academic programmes

SFS Dhemaji offers a comprehensive academic curriculum following the guidelines of the **Secondary Education Board of Assam (SEBA)** for secondary levels and the **Assam Higher Secondary Education Council (AHSEC)** for higher secondary levels [3] [6]. The school has a history of academic performance in the state-level **High School Leaving Certificate (HSLC)** examinations, with documented success dating back to at least 2010 [8].

In 2026, the school was recognized by **EducationWorld** as one of the top schools in Assam, achieving a high institutional score that reflects its sustained academic and infrastructure standards [10]. The curriculum is designed to support the holistic development of students, preparing them for higher education and professional careers [12].

# Community activity

The school is an active participant in the social and cultural life of Dhemaji. It regularly organizes events such as the **Annual General Exhibition**, which often features participation from local government officials, including the Additional Deputy Commissioner (ADC) of Dhemaji [11].

Student achievements extend beyond academics; in 2025, a student from SFS Dhemaji, Aadi Singha, was selected as a **Youth Ambassador**, garnering regional media attention [9]. The school's engagement with the community is further evidenced by its large student body, which was reported to be around 2,000 students served by 60 teachers as of recent institutional records [1].
