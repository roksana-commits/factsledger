## Overview

Shree Manakamana Ratna Ambika Secondary School (Nepali: मनকমনা रत्न अम्बিকা माध्यमिक विद्यालय) is a community-based educational institution located in Makalu Tole, Khandbari Municipality-9, Tumlingtar, within the Sankhuwasabha district of Koshi Province, Nepal [School Profile: Manakamana Ratna Ambika Secondary School](https://www.collegenp.com/school/manakamana-ratna-ambika-secondary-school-sankhuwasabha) [Manakamana Ratna Ambika School Opens Historical Museum](https://www.collegenp.com/news/manakamana-ratna-ambika-school-opens-historical-museum). Established in 1959 AD (2016 BS) as Shri Manakamana Primary School, the institution was named in honor of the deity Mata Manakamana Devi [School Profile: Manakamana Ratna Ambika Secondary School](https://www.collegenp.com/school/manakamana-ratna-ambika-secondary-school-sankhuwasabha) [Manakamana Ratna Ambika School Opens Historical Museum](https://www.collegenp.com/news/manakamana-ratna-ambika-school-opens-historical-museum). The school has since expanded significantly, attaining lower secondary status in 1973 and secondary level approval in 1994 [School Profile: Manakamana Ratna Ambika Secondary School](https://www.collegenp.com/school/manakamana-ratna-ambika-secondary-school-sankhuwasabha). In 2017, the Government of Nepal designated the institution as a **Model School**, a status that has facilitated enhanced infrastructure development and educational support [School Profile: Manakamana Ratna Ambika Secondary School](https://www.collegenp.com/school/manakamana-ratna-ambika-secondary-school-sankhuwasabha) [Shree Manakamana Ratna Ambika Secondary School Official Website](http://manakamanara.edu.np/). The campus occupies a substantial area of over 120 ropanis and includes ten major buildings, specialized laboratories, and a library [Shree Manakamana Ratna Ambika Secondary School Official Website](http://manakamanara.edu.np/) [Manakamana Ratna Ambika School Opens Historical Museum](https://www.collegenp.com/news/manakamana-ratna-ambika-school-opens-historical-museum).

## Recognition

The school achieved international recognition when it received the **British Council ISA Full Award** for the documented 2019–2020 cycle [British Council Nepal Press Release - 20 October 2020](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf). This award acknowledges the institution's success in integrating international themes into its curriculum and fostering global citizenship [British Council Nepal Press Release - 20 October 2020](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf). Additionally, its designation as a Model School by the Ministry of Education in 2017 marks it as a key development site for public education in the region [School Profile: Manakamana Ratna Ambika Secondary School](https://www.collegenp.com/school/manakamana-ratna-ambika-secondary-school-sankhuwasabha).

## Leadership

As a government-supported community school, the institution operates under the regulatory framework of the National Examination Board (NEB) of Nepal [Manakamana Ratna Ambika School Opens Historical Museum](https://www.collegenp.com/news/manakamana-ratna-ambika-school-opens-historical-museum). The school is currently supported by a dedicated staff of 32 members who manage an enrollment of approximately 1,442 students [Manakamana Ratna Ambika School Opens Historical Museum](https://www.collegenp.com/news/manakamana-ratna-ambika-school-opens-historical-museum). While the specific names of individual founders are not documented in available records, the school remains a central educational hub for the Tumlingtar area [School Profile: Manakamana Ratna Ambika Secondary School](https://www.collegenp.com/school/manakamana-ratna-ambika-secondary-school-sankhuwasabha) [Manakamana Ratna Ambika School Opens Historical Museum](https://www.collegenp.com/news/manakamana-ratna-ambika-school-opens-historical-museum).

## Educational programmes

The school provides a comprehensive range of academic services from Nursery through Grade 12 [School Profile: Manakamana Ratna Ambika Secondary School](https://www.collegenp.com/school/manakamana-ratna-ambika-secondary-school-sankhuwasabha) [Manakamana Ratna Ambika School Opens Historical Museum](https://www.collegenp.com/news/manakamana-ratna-ambika-school-opens-historical-museum). A notable feature of its curriculum is the **technical stream**, which includes a Civil Engineering program launched for Grade 9 in 2016 and subsequently extended to the higher secondary level [School Profile: Manakamana Ratna Ambika Secondary School](https://www.collegenp.com/school/manakamana-ratna-ambika-secondary-school-sankhuwasabha) [Shree Manakamana Ratna Ambika Secondary School Official Website](http://manakamanara.edu.np/). The medium of instruction is both Nepali and English, with English medium classes having been introduced in 2075 BS to meet modern educational requirements [Shree Manakamana Ratna Ambika Secondary School Official Website](http://manakamanara.edu.np/) [Manakamana Ratna Ambika School Opens Historical Museum](https://www.collegenp.com/news/manakamana-ratna-ambika-school-opens-historical-museum). The institution participated in its first School Leaving Certificate (SLC) examinations in 1988 and received authorization for Grade 11 and 12 programs in the 2003/04 academic year [School Profile: Manakamana Ratna Ambika Secondary School](https://www.collegenp.com/school/manakamana-ratna-ambika-secondary-school-sankhuwasabha).

## Community activity

On July 28, 2026, the school inaugurated a **Historical Museum** on its campus to preserve and showcase the cultural heritage of the Sankhuwasabha region [Manakamana Ratna Ambika School Opens Historical Museum](https://www.collegenp.com/news/manakamana-ratna-ambika-school-opens-historical-museum). The museum houses a collection of traditional household items, ancient coins, and agricultural tools, serving as an educational resource for both students and the local community [Manakamana Ratna Ambika School Opens Historical Museum](https://www.collegenp.com/news/manakamana-ratna-ambika-school-opens-historical-museum). This initiative reflects the school's broader commitment to cultural preservation alongside academic excellence [Manakamana Ratna Ambika School Opens Historical Museum](https://www.collegenp.com/news/manakamana-ratna-ambika-school-opens-historical-museum).
