## Overview

Kathmandu Euro IB World School is a private coeducational day school situated in the Tokha Municipality of Kathmandu, Nepal [[Kathmandu Euro IB World School - About Us](https://kathmandueuroschool.edu.np/about-us)] [[International Baccalaureate Organization (IBO) - Kathmandu Euro School Profile](https://ibo.org/school/061594/)]. Located specifically in Baniyatar, Gongabu, the institution operates within a modern educational framework that emphasizes global connectivity and academic excellence [[Kathmandu Euro IB World School - About Us](https://kathmandueuroschool.edu.np/about-us)]. The school celebrated a significant milestone in May 2026, marking its "Golden Jubilee" which denotes fifty years of institutional history [[Kathmandu Euro IB World School - Facebook: Golden Jubilee Announcement](https://www.facebook.com/kathmandueuroschool/posts/1604577524791575/)]. While the current "Euro School" branding is a more recent development, the school maintains a legacy of long-standing service to the Nepalese educational sector [[Kathmandu Euro IB World School - Facebook: Golden Jubilee Announcement](https://www.facebook.com/kathmandueuroschool/posts/1604577524791575/)].

## Recognition

The institution has achieved international distinction through its collaboration with the British Council. Kathmandu Euro IB World School received the **British Council ISA Full Award** for the documented cycle (2019-20) [[British Council Nepal - International School Award Winners List](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/programmes/education/connecting-classrooms/international-school-award)]. This recognition was granted for the school's success in integrating international perspectives into its curriculum and promoting global citizenship among its students [[British Council Nepal - International School Award Winners List](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/programmes/education/connecting-classrooms/international-school-award)]. The award ceremony was conducted virtually on October 21, 2020, in response to the global pandemic conditions at the time [[South Asia Monitor - British Council confers ISA to 23 schools in Nepal](https://www.southasiamonitor.org/nepal/british-council-confers-international-school-award-23-schools-nepal)].

Beyond institutional accolades, the school's students have also achieved international success. In 2026, Seron Thapa, a student at the school, was recognized as the 2nd Runner-Up at the World Junior Tourism Ambassador competition held in Vietnam, highlighting the school's role in developing global ambassadors [[Kathmandu Euro IB World School - Facebook: World Junior Tourism Ambassador 2026](https://www.facebook.com/kathmandueuroschool/posts/1592388822677112/)].

## Educational programmes

Kathmandu Euro IB World School has transitioned to an inquiry-based academic model, achieving authorization as an **International Baccalaureate (IB) World School** for the Primary Years Programme (PYP) on December 13, 2023 [[International Baccalaureate Organization (IBO) - Kathmandu Euro School Profile](https://ibo.org/school/061594/)]. The school continues to expand its academic offerings and is currently designated as a candidate school for the IB Middle Years Programme (MYP) [[Kathmandu Euro IB World School - About Us](https://kathmandueuroschool.edu.np/about-us)].

The educational framework is characterized by a strong focus on **STEAM** (Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics) and environmental sustainability [[Kathmandu Euro IB World School - About Us](https://kathmandueuroschool.edu.np/about-us)]. This commitment to international standards is further evidenced by the school's membership in the **Council of International Schools (CIS)**, which became effective in 2025 [[Kathmandu Euro IB World School - About Us](https://kathmandueuroschool.edu.np/about-us)].

This initiative provides full scholarships to eligible students, reflecting the school's commitment to supporting the families of those who contributed to social movements in Nepal [[eKantipur - Kathmandu Euro School Scholarship Announcement](https://ekantipur.com/en/market/2025/10/10/kathmandu-euro-school-will-give-full-scholarship-to-the-martyred-children-of-gen-g-movement-15-27.html)].
