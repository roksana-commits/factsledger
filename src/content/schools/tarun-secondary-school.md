## Overview

Tarun Secondary School (Tarun Madhyamik Vidhyalaya) is a community-based educational institution located in the Balaju area of Kathmandu, Nepal [Edusanjal Institutional Profile](https://edusanjal.com/school/tarun-secondary-school/). Situated within Ward No. 16 of the Kathmandu Metropolitan City, the school serves as a central educational hub for the local community, providing instruction from Early Childhood Development through Grade 10 [Official School Website](https://tarunmavi.edu.np/). Founded in 1972 (2029 BS), the institution has evolved from a local initiative into a prominent public school designated as a "Model School" under the city's "One Ward, One Model School" initiative [Official School Website](https://tarunmavi.edu.np/) [Edusanjal Institutional Profile](https://edusanjal.com/school/tarun-secondary-school/). The school is officially registered under the National Examination Board (NEB) with code 0731776 [Official School Website](https://tarunmavi.edu.np/).

## Recognition

The school has achieved significant national and international recognition for its academic standards and global integration. In 2001 (2058 BS), it was named the "Best School in Nepal" by the Government of Nepal [Edusanjal Institutional Profile](https://edusanjal.com/school/tarun-secondary-school/). More recently, the institution received the British Council ISA Full Award for the documented cycle [British Council Nepal ISA Winners](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/programmes/education/connecting-classrooms/international-school-award). It was conferred with the Full Award during the 2019-20 cycle (Batch 1) and received special distinction for the "Best International Collaborative Project" in 2020 [British Council Nepal ISA Winners](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/programmes/education/connecting-classrooms/international-school-award) [Spotlight Nepal News Report](https://www.spotlightnepal.com/2020/10/21/23-nepali-schools-receive-british-councils-international-school-award-2020/). The school secured ISA re-accreditation in 2023 [Edusanjal Institutional Profile](https://edusanjal.com/school/tarun-secondary-school/).

## Leadership

The institution is currently led by Principal Mr. Baburam Neupane and Vice Principal Mr. Rishi Timilsina [Official School Website](https://tarunmavi.edu.np/). Under their leadership, the school manages a large student body, which numbered approximately 1,819 students during the 2081/82 BS (2024-25 AD) academic cycle [Edusanjal Institutional Profile](https://edusanjal.com/school/tarun-secondary-school/).

## Educational programmes

Tarun Secondary School provides English-medium instruction and is noted for its integration of practical and vocational training [Edusanjal Institutional Profile](https://edusanjal.com/school/tarun-secondary-school/). A signature initiative is the "Book-Free Friday" program, which emphasizes hands-on skill development in fields such as fashion design, agriculture, mobile repair, and drone technology [Edusanjal Institutional Profile](https://edusanjal.com/school/tarun-secondary-school/). The school's infrastructure supports these technical pursuits with smart-board-equipped classrooms and specialized laboratories for Physics, Chemistry, and Computer Science [Official School Website](https://tarunmavi.edu.np/).

## Community activity

Beyond its academic role, the school functions as a community anchor in Kathmandu. In May 2026, the campus served as a vital relocation center for students displaced by urban cleanup and demolition activities within the city [Onlinekhabar News Coverage](https://english.onlinekhabar.com/demolished-school-students-showed-up.html). By offering high-quality English-medium education, the school attracts students who might otherwise seek private schooling, contributing to the narrowing of the educational gap between public and private institutions in Nepal [Edusanjal Institutional Profile](https://edusanjal.com/school/tarun-secondary-school/).
