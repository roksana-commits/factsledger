## Overview

Don Bosco School, Alaknanda, is a prominent educational institution located in South Delhi, India [2] [3]. Established on April 9, 1980, the school is an all-boys, English-medium institution providing education from the pre-primary level through Class XII [2]. The school is managed by the Salesian Brother’s Society of Delhi (Northern India), a religious congregation within the Catholic Church, and is part of the global network of Salesians of Don Bosco [3].

## Recognition and accreditation

The school is recognized by the Directorate of Education, NCT of Delhi, and is affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE) [1] [2]. It holds CBSE affiliation number 2730082 and school code 25230 [1]. The institution's provisional affiliation is currently valid through March 2030 [1]. Furthermore, the school has achieved ISO certification for its Quality Management System and Environmental Management System, reflecting its commitment to institutional excellence and sustainability [2].

## Leadership and governance

The institution is governed by the Salesians of Don Bosco and operates under the Salesian Preventive System [2] [3]. This educational philosophy, developed by St. John Bosco, is based on the pillars of reason, religion, and kindness [2]. The current principal of the school is Fr. Sujith Augustine SDB [2]. The school moved to its current 4.37-acre campus in Alaknanda in 1984, having initially operated from a temporary structure in Okhla [2].

## Academic programmes

Don Bosco School follows the All India Senior School Certificate Examination (AISSCE) framework for its senior secondary students [2]. The curriculum is designed to provide academic rigor and prepare students for higher education [4]. The school offers specialized academic streams in Science and Commerce at the Class XI and XII levels, allowing students to focus on their preferred areas of study [2] [4].

## Community activity

A central part of the school's social mission is the "Don Bosco Ankur" initiative [2]. This evening school program is dedicated to providing free education, meals, and essential materials to approximately 320 underprivileged children from the surrounding Alaknanda community [2]. This initiative reflects the school's commitment to social outreach and the Salesian mission of serving youth, particularly those in need [3].

## Research or professional work

The school's professional standards are validated by its dual ISO certifications for quality and environmental management [2]. In independent evaluations, the institution has maintained a high standing, including being rated AAAA+ by Careers360 in 2024 [4]. It also frequently appears in the top tiers of the EducationWorld India School Rankings, which assesses schools on various parameters of institutional excellence [5].
