## Overview

Shri Samarth High School, also referred to as Shri Samarth High School and Junior College, is a prominent educational institution situated in Amravati, Maharashtra, India [1]. Established in 1934, the school is one of the older educational establishments in the Amravati district [2]. It operates as a private-aided institution and is managed by a local educational trust [2]. The school provides education from the secondary level starting at Grade 5 through to the higher secondary level, known as Junior College, which concludes at Grade 12 [2].

## Recognition and accreditation

The institution is officially recognized by the District Administration of Amravati and is listed in the official government directory of public utilities [1]. It is assigned the UDISE code 27071503904 [2]. The school is affiliated with the Maharashtra State Board of Secondary and Higher Secondary Education (MSBSHSE) for both the Secondary School Certificate (SSC) and Higher Secondary Certificate (HSC) examinations [1] [2].

The school follows the curriculum prescribed by the Maharashtra State Board [1]. It serves students from Grade 5 through Grade 12 [2].
