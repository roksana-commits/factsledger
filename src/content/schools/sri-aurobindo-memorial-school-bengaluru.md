# Sri Aurobindo Memorial School

## Overview
Sri Aurobindo Memorial School is a private educational institution situated in Banashankari II Stage, Bengaluru, Karnataka, India [3]. Established in 1962, the school provides education from pre-primary levels through Class 10 [1] [4]. The institution was founded by Dr. Santoshananda (Papaji), Smt. Sushila Santosh (Jiji), and Mrs. Espoir Pearson [1]. It was initially known as "The New English School" during its early years [2].

## Recognition and accreditation
The school is formally recognized by the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), holding affiliation number 830002 and school code 45005 [3]. The initial affiliation was granted on January 4, 2017 [3]. The school's current affiliation status is active, with an extension valid until March 31, 2027 [3].

## Leadership and governance
Sri Aurobindo Memorial School is managed by the **New Age Educational Trust**, which was formally established on May 9, 1970 [2]. The trust provides the administrative framework for the institution's operations [2]. The current head of the institution is Principal Anuradha Kumar [3].

## Academic programmes
The school follows the CBSE curriculum, offering classes from the pre-primary level up to Class 10 [4]. The institution has maintained a consistent record of academic performance in the Bengaluru region. In 2025, student Yadhavi D. Kanekal topped the school with a score of 98% in the CBSE Class 10 examinations [5]. Media reports from 2026 highlighted the school's performance, noting that 180 students appeared for the exams with a significant number achieving high scores [6].

Beyond traditional academics, the school participates in technological initiatives such as *The Times of India* Student AI Masterclass, where Grade 10 students have engaged in developing artificial intelligence projects [9].

## Community activity
Students at Sri Aurobindo Memorial School participate in various inter-school competitions and sports events. In 2020, the school's quiz team, comprising Vibhav B. and Suraj Pai, won the Bengaluru leg of *The Hindu Young World Quiz* [4].

In sports, the school has a documented history of participation in cricket and basketball. The school's cricket team has competed in the KSCA inter-school tournaments and recorded victories in matches against other institutions, such as Oxford Senior Secondary School [7] [8].

## What this profile does not claim
This profile does not claim current accreditation status beyond the CBSE affiliation extension ending in March 2027. It does not provide information regarding senior secondary (Class 11 and 12) offerings, as the documented affiliation and reports focus on Class 10.
