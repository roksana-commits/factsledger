## Overview

Balwantray Mehta Vidya Bhawan (Lajpat Nagar), often abbreviated as **BRMVB**, is a co-educational senior secondary institution located in the Lajpat Nagar-IV neighborhood of New Delhi, India [1]. Established in 1961, the school operates as a government-aided institution under the Directorate of Education, Government of National Capital Territory of Delhi (GNCTD) [1] [2]. The school is managed by the Servants of the People Society (Delhi Branch), a social service organization founded by the freedom fighter Lala Lajpat Rai [1] [2].

The institution's primary mission is to provide a holistic educational framework that prepares students for a creative and socially aware life, grounded in diverse cultural heritage [4]. It serves students from primary through senior secondary levels within a government-aided framework, meaning it receives state financial support while maintaining private management [1].

## Recognition and accreditation

The school holds official recognition from major Indian educational regulatory bodies. It is registered with the Directorate of Education, GNCTD, under the school identification number **1925193** [1]. Furthermore, the institution is affiliated with the Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE), holding affiliation number **2774028** [1]. These credentials confirm its status as a legitimate provider of primary and secondary education in the National Capital Territory [1].

## Leadership and governance

Governance of Balwantray Mehta Vidya Bhawan is overseen by the **Servants of the People Society (SOPS)**, which was originally institutionalized by Lala Lajpat Rai in 1921 in Lahore [2]. The school's operational existence and governance structure have been documented in legal records, notably a 1985 Delhi High Court judgment in the case of *B.R Mehta Vidya Bhavan Petitioner v. The Delhi School Tribunal & Others* [3]. The school's leadership continues to be recognized for its contributions to education, including receiving a "Life Time Achievement Award" from the Opkar Foundation in early 2026 [5].

## Academic programmes

The school is organized into two primary segments: the **Primary Wing** and the **Senior Secondary Wing** [1]. Following CBSE standards, the institution offers specialized academic streams at the senior secondary level, including:

*   **Science**
*   **Commerce**
*   **Humanities**

The institution reports a consistent 100% success rate in board examinations, with graduates progressing to various Indian institutes, such as IISER Mohali, and international medical universities [1].

## Community activity

Beyond academic instruction, the school maintains an active presence in regional extracurricular competitions. It has documented achievements at the district and zonal levels in several sporting disciplines, specifically **Taekwondo**, **Volleyball**, and **Athletics** [1]. The school's vision emphasizes making students socially, morally, and ethically aware of their heritage through these diverse activities [4].
