## Overview

**Kathmandu Pragya Kunja School (KPKS)** is a private co-educational institution located in Sangam Chowk, New Baneshwor, Kathmandu, Nepal [Kathmandu Pragya Kunja School Official Facebook Page](https://www.facebook.com/KPKSchool/) [Kathmandu Pragya Kunja School Official Website - About Us](https://pragyakunja.edu.np/about/). Established in 2004, the school provides education from Pre-Nursery to Grade 10 [Kathmandu Pragya Kunja School Official Website - About Us](https://pragyakunja.edu.np/about/). The institution focuses on a holistic development approach, emphasizing active student engagement and the "4Cs": Communication, Collaboration, Creativity, and Critical Thinking [Kathmandu Pragya Kunja School Official Website - About Us](https://pragyakunja.edu.np/about/).

## Recognition

The school has maintained a consistent record of accreditation for its international education initiatives. For the documented cycles, the school received the **British Council ISA Full Award** [Edusanjal: Twenty Six Nepalese Schools got British Council's ISA Award (2017)](https://edusanjal.com/news/twenty-six-nepalese-schools-got-british-councils-international-schools-award-isa/). It was first awarded the British Council Full International School Award (ISA) for the 2016–2019 cycle on August 27, 2017 [Edusanjal: Twenty Six Nepalese Schools got British Council's ISA Award (2017)](https://edusanjal.com/news/twenty-six-nepalese-schools-got-british-councils-international-schools-award-isa/). Subsequently, the school received re-accreditation for the 2019–2020 batch [British Council Nepal: Press Release - 20 October 2020 ISA Awards](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf) [Spotlight Nepal: 23 Nepali Schools Receive British Council's ISA 2020](https://www.spotlightnepal.com/2020/10/21/23-nepali-schools-receive-british-councils-international-school-award-2020/). Most recently, KPKS successfully achieved re-accreditation for the 2021–2024 cycle, as documented in the British Council Nepal's official winners list [British Council Nepal: ISA Award Winners 2020-21 List](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/programmes/education/connecting-classrooms/international-school-award).

## Leadership

The institution is led by Principal **Sunita Poudel**, who also serves as a Director [Kathmandu Pragya Kunja School Official Facebook Page](https://www.facebook.com/KPKSchool/) [LinkedIn Profile: Sunita Poudel - Principal and Director at KPKS](https://np.linkedin.com/in/sunita-poudel-9796a0137). In June 2026, Poudel was honored with the **Dr. Sarvepalli Radhakrishnan National Award – Principal of the Year Award 2026**, recognizing her leadership within the educational community [KPKS Facebook: Principal of the Year Award 2026 Announcement](https://www.facebook.com/KPKSchool/posts/a-moment-of-immense-pride-for-the-pragya-kunja-family-with-great-joy-and-admirat/1616516730481810/).

## Educational programmes

KPKS integrates several specialized programs alongside the national curriculum to foster 21st-century skills. The school collaborates with **Karkhana** for STEAM (Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics) education and the **Robotics Association of Nepal (RAN)** for robotics training [Kathmandu Pragya Kunja School Official Website - About Us](https://pragyakunja.edu.np/about/). Additionally, the curriculum includes the **3Di School Program** from New Zealand and the **Digi School** UK-based computer curriculum [Kathmandu Pragya Kunja School Official Website - About Us](https://pragyakunja.edu.np/about/). The school also partnered with **Bal Bikas NCMAS** in 2013 to focus on early childhood mental development [Kathmandu Pragya Kunja School Official Website - About Us](https://pragyakunja.edu.np/about/).

## Community activity

The school is an active member of the **Quality Kathmandu Schools (QKS)** group, a network of 25 schools dedicated to maintaining high educational standards through institutional collaboration [Kathmandu Pragya Kunja School Official Website - About Us](https://pragyakunja.edu.np/about/). To support student well-being, KPKS partnered with the **Sambhavya Foundation** in 2018 to integrate professional school counseling and mental health support services [Kathmandu Pragya Kunja School Official Website - About Us](https://pragyakunja.edu.np/about/). The school also organizes leadership development events such as **KPKS MUN 4.0**, which was held in January 2026 [KPKS Official Website: News & Media - Sports Fiesta and MUN 4.0](https://pragyakunja.edu.np/newsmedia/).

## Sports

KPKS incorporates athletic competition into its annual calendar to promote sportsmanship. In January 2026, the school organized the **KPKS Sports Fiesta 2082**, a major event involving various sporting activities for the student body [KPKS Official Website: News & Media - Sports Fiesta and MUN 4.0](https://pragyakunja.edu.np/newsmedia/).
