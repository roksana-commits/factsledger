# Overview

Ripumardini Sainik Secondary School, also known as **Ripumardini Sainik Mahavidyalaya (RSMV)** [[3]](https://edusanjal.com/school/ripumardini-sainik-secondary-school/) [[5]](https://www.rsmv.edu.np/), is a co-educational institution located in **Bansbari-3, Kathmandu, Nepal** [[1]](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/programmes/education/connecting-classrooms/international-school-award) [[3]](https://edusanjal.com/school/ripumardini-sainik-secondary-school/). The school was founded on **February 18, 2005** (6th Falgun 2061 B.S.) [[5]](https://www.rsmv.edu.np/) [[12]](https://www.myfreeadmission.com/college/ripumardini-sainik-mahavidyalaya--1189/message-from-the-principal) and was inaugurated by the former **Crown Princess Himani Rajya Laxmi Devi Shah** [[3]](https://edusanjal.com/school/ripumardini-sainik-secondary-school/). It operates under the management of the **Nepali Army Welfare Fund (NAWF)** in collaboration with the **Nepali Army Wives’ Association (NAWA)** [[3]](https://edusanjal.com/school/ripumardini-sainik-secondary-school/) [[4]](https://www.myfreeadmission.com/college/ripumardini-sainik-mahavidyalaya--1189).

The primary mission of the institution is to provide educational empowerment to the children of **martyred and serving army personnel**, though the school also extends admission to **civilian students** [[3]](https://edusanjal.com/school/ripumardini-sainik-secondary-school/) [[4]](https://www.myfreeadmission.com/college/ripumardini-sainik-mahavidyalaya--1189) [[12]](https://www.myfreeadmission.com/college/ripumardini-sainik-mahavidyalaya--1189/message-from-the-principal). As of the 2081/82 B.S. academic year, the school recorded an enrollment of **708 students**, reflecting a notable increase from the 560 students reported in the previous year [[3]](https://edusanjal.com/school/ripumardini-sainik-secondary-school/).

# Recognition

The school is a recognized recipient of the **British Council International School Award (ISA)** [[1]](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/programmes/education/connecting-classrooms/international-school-award) [[2]](https://www.spotlightnepal.com/2020/10/21/23-nepali-schools-receive-british-councils-international-school-award-2020/). It achieved the **Full Award accreditation** for the **2019–20 cycle** (Batch 1) [[1]](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/programmes/education/connecting-classrooms/international-school-award) [[9]](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf). During the virtual award ceremony held on October 20, 2020, Ripumardini Sainik Secondary School was further distinguished by receiving the award for the **"Best 20% Local Curriculum Project"** [[2]](https://www.spotlightnepal.com/2020/10/21/23-nepali-schools-receive-british-councils-international-school-award-2020/) [[9]](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf). Institutional communications also reference an **ISA 2020–2023 Full Award**, which corresponds to the three-year accreditation period granted following their 2020 success [[7]](https://www.facebook.com/ripuschool76/posts/190551335911927/) [[10]](https://www.facebook.com/ripuschool76/posts/195681155398945/). The school received the British Council ISA Full Award for the documented cycle.

# Leadership

The institution is managed by the **Nepal Army Welfare Fund**, which ensures a disciplined and military-backed educational environment [[3]](https://edusanjal.com/school/ripumardini-sainik-secondary-school/) [[4]](https://www.myfreeadmission.com/college/ripumardini-sainik-mahavidyalaya--1189). While specific names of the current administrative leadership are not detailed in primary directory sources, the school's governance is integrated into the broader welfare structures of the **Nepali Army** [[3]](https://edusanjal.com/school/ripumardini-sainik-secondary-school/).

# Educational programmes

Ripumardini Sainik Secondary School provides education for students from **Grade 1 through Grade 12** [[3]](https://edusanjal.com/school/ripumardini-sainik-secondary-school/) [[14]](https://edusanjal.com/school/ripumardini-sainik-secondary-school/#offered-programs). The curriculum is described as **ICT-integrated and child-centered**, focusing on developing patriotic and globally aware citizens [[3]](https://edusanjal.com/school/ripumardini-sainik-secondary-school/). For higher secondary education, the school offers **Ten Plus Two (+2)** programs in **Science** and **Law**, affiliated with the **National Examinations Board (NEB)** of Nepal [[3]](https://edusanjal.com/school/ripumardini-sainik-secondary-school/) [[14]](https://edusanjal.com/school/ripumardini-sainik-secondary-school/#offered-programs).

# Community activity

The school's community focus is rooted in its founding purpose: supporting the families of **Nepali Army personnel** [[3]](https://edusanjal.com/school/ripumardini-sainik-secondary-school/) [[4]](https://www.myfreeadmission.com/college/ripumardini-sainik-mahavidyalaya--1189). By prioritizing the education of children from military backgrounds, the institution serves as a key welfare component for the defense community while maintaining a co-educational environment that includes local civilian children [[3]](https://edusanjal.com/school/ripumardini-sainik-secondary-school/) [[12]](https://www.myfreeadmission.com/college/ripumardini-sainik-mahavidyalaya--1189/message-from-the-principal).
