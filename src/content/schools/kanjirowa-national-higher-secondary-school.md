# Kanjirowa National Higher Secondary School

## Overview
Kanjirowa National Higher Secondary School (KNSS) is an independent, co-educational secondary school located in the Koteshwor district of Kathmandu, Nepal [https://kanjisl.edu.np/feature.php](https://kanjisl.edu.np/feature.php). The institution has undergone a significant institutional evolution, originally established in 1998 under the name Kathmandu Don Bosco High School in New Baneshwor [https://kanjisl.edu.np/feature.php](https://kanjisl.edu.np/feature.php) [https://edusanjal.com/school/kanjirowa-national-higher-secondary-school/](https://edusanjal.com/school/kanjirowa-national-higher-secondary-school/). In 2011, the school implemented a strategic rebranding to adopt a name reflecting its national identity—Kanjirowa, a mountain in western Nepal—and relocated to its current campus in Koteshwor [https://kanjisl.edu.np/feature.php](https://kanjisl.edu.np/feature.php). The campus occupies 8 ropanis of land and provides facilities including modern laboratories, an e-learning library, and transportation services across the Kathmandu Valley and nearby regions [https://kanjisl.edu.np/feature.php](https://kanjisl.edu.np/feature.php) [https://edusanjal.com/school/kanjirowa-national-higher-secondary-school/](https://edusanjal.com/school/kanjirowa-national-higher-secondary-school/).

## Recognition
The school is recognized by the British Council for its integration of international standards into its local curriculum. Kanjirowa received the **British Council ISA Full Award** for the documented 2019–2020 cycle [https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/programmes/education/connecting-classrooms/international-school-award](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/programmes/education/connecting-classrooms/international-school-award) [https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf). This award, announced in a virtual ceremony on October 20, 2020, authorized the school to utilize the ISA kite mark for a three-year period, validating its commitment to global citizenship and collaborative teaching practices [https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/programmes/education/connecting-classrooms/international-school-award](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/programmes/education/connecting-classrooms/international-school-award) [https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf).

## Leadership
Institutional leadership at Kanjirowa is characterized by a proactive approach to modern educational methodologies. The school's leaders have been instrumental in fostering a STEAM-centric environment, hosting national-level forums to discuss the integration of Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics into both Early Childhood Development (ECD) and higher secondary education [https://myrepublica.nagariknetwork.com/news/kanjirowa-national-secondary-school-successfully-conducts-steam-symposium](https://myrepublica.nagariknetwork.com/news/kanjirowa-national-secondary-school-successfully-conducts-steam-symposium).

## Educational programmes
Kanjirowa specializes in higher secondary education, offering **Plus Two (+2)** programs affiliated with the National Examinations Board (NEB) of Nepal [https://kanjisl.edu.np/feature.php](https://kanjisl.edu.np/feature.php). The school provides two primary academic streams:
*   **Science Stream:** Designed for students pursuing careers in medicine, engineering, and biotechnology [https://kanjisl.edu.np/feature.php](https://kanjisl.edu.np/feature.php).
*   **Management Stream:** Focused on business studies, hotel management, and computer science [https://kanjisl.edu.np/feature.php](https://kanjisl.edu.np/feature.php).

## Community activity
The school serves as a hub for educational community engagement. In February 2024, it hosted the **STEAM Symposium**, a national gathering for school leaders and educators to share best practices in STEAM integration [https://myrepublica.nagariknetwork.com/news/kanjirowa-national-secondary-school-successfully-conducts-steam-symposium](https://myrepublica.nagariknetwork.com/news/kanjirowa-national-secondary-school-successfully-conducts-steam-symposium). Additionally, Kanjirowa has collaborated with other institutions to host workshops for the **Duke of Edinburgh's International Award**, facilitating personal development programs for youth in the region [https://www.facebook.com/sanskritiinternationalschool/posts/1053821723450974/](https://www.facebook.com/sanskritiinternationalschool/posts/1053821723450974/).
