## Overview

Glowing Kids Montessori Pre-School is an early childhood education center located in Swachha Saraswati Nagar, Gatthaghar, Madhyapur Thimi 3, Bhaktapur, Nepal [Glowing Kids Montessori Pre School Facebook Page](https://www.facebook.com/p/Glowing-Kids-Montessori-Pre-School-100070439731253/) [Directory of Nepal - Glowing Kids Montessori Pre School](https://www.directoryofnepal.com/company/34914/-glowing-kids-montessori-pre-school.html). The institution serves as a resource for pre-primary education, catering to children from Playgroup through Upper Kindergarten (UKG) [Glowing Kids Montessori Pre School Facebook Page](https://www.facebook.com/p/Glowing-Kids-Montessori-Pre-School-100070439731253/) [Facebook Live: 14th Annual Parent's Day & Graduation Ceremony](https://www.facebook.com/people/Glowing-Kids-Montessori-Pre-School/100070439731253/). The school was likely established in 2012, as evidenced by the celebration of its 14th Annual Parent's Day and Graduation Ceremony in early 2026 [Facebook Live: 14th Annual Parent's Day & Graduation Ceremony](https://www.facebook.com/people/Glowing-Kids-Montessori-Pre-School/100070439731253/). It is a registered member of the Association of Pre-School Educators Nepal (APEN) [APEN Directory - Association of Pre-School Educators Nepal](https://www.scribd.com/document/870045916/APEN-Association-of-Pre-School-Educators-Nepal).

## Recognition

The school received the British Council ISA Full Award for the documented cycle of 2020–2023 [British Council Nepal Press Release - 20 October 2020](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf) [Spotlight Nepal - 23 Nepali Schools Receive British Council's International School Award 2020](https://www.spotlightnepal.com/2020/10/21/23-nepali-schools-receive-british-councils-international-school-award-2020/). This accreditation, endorsed by the Center for Education and Human Resource Development (CEHRD) of the Government of Nepal, recognizes the institution's commitment to embedding internationalism into its curriculum [British Council Nepal Press Release - 20 October 2020](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf). Additionally, the school and its leadership were recognized during a 2023 felicitation program organized by DAV School for their contributions as founding pillars of preschool education in the region [DAV Nepal News - Honouring and Felicitation Programme to the Pre-School Principals](https://davnepal.com/news.php?id=1093).

## Leadership

The institution is led by **Laxmi Shobha Shrestha**, who serves as the Founding Principal and School Director [APEN Directory - Association of Pre-School Educators Nepal](https://www.scribd.com/document/870045916/APEN-Association-of-Pre-School-Educators-Nepal) [DAV Nepal News - Honouring and Felicitation Programme to the Pre-School Principals](https://davnepal.com/news.php?id=1093). Under her leadership, the school has integrated traditional cultural practices with modern educational methods, including the observation of rituals such as Haribodhini Ekadashi and Tulasi Vivaha to foster cultural awareness among students [Facebook Post: Haribodhini Ekadashi Rituals](https://www.facebook.com/groups/Bhagavadgita10/posts/3185244694967843/).

## Educational programmes

The school implements a Montessori-based curriculum designed for early childhood development [Glowing Kids Montessori Pre School Facebook Page](https://www.facebook.com/p/Glowing-Kids-Montessori-Pre-School-100070439731253/) [Facebook Live: 14th Annual Parent's Day & Graduation Ceremony](https://www.facebook.com/people/Glowing-Kids-Montessori-Pre-School/100070439731253/). The educational approach includes hands-on thematic learning events, such as "Blue Day," where students engage in creative arts and crafts to explore conceptual themes [Facebook Post: Blue Day Celebration](https://www.facebook.com/100070439731253/posts/-blue-day-celebration-from-a-cozy-tent-surrounded-by-the-beauty-of-the-blue-sky-/1085600800464568/). Furthermore, the school participated in the British Council's "Connecting Classrooms" project, which facilitates international collaboration to develop global citizenship skills in young learners [British Council Nepal Press Release - 20 October 2020](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf).
