## Overview

Nabakumar Institution is a school in Bakshi Bazar, Old Dhaka, founded in 1916 and named for a zamindar,
Nabakumar ([Banglapedia](https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php/Nabakumar_Institute)). It added a college branch
in 1973, and school and college divisions now share one campus and administration; the college branch carries
the name of the educator Dr. Muhammad Shahidullah
([Banglapedia](https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php/Nabakumar_Institute)). The institution's most independently
documented moment is not routine — it is the 1969 killing of one of its own students during Bangladesh's
mass-upsurge movement against the Ayub Khan government.

## The 1969 mass upsurge and Matiur Rahman Mallik

On 24 January 1969, Standard IX student Matiur Rahman Mallik was shot dead by police during a demonstration in
Dhaka, one of several people killed that day
([The Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/city/mass-upsurge-day-today-1524313)). Dhaka Tribune's account of
the day names him specifically as a student "of Nabakumar Institute"
([Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/303385/historic-mass-upsurge-day-tuesday)). His death is
credited, alongside other student deaths that week, with helping galvanise the wider movement that contributed to
the fall of the Ayub Khan government later that year. A fuller account of these events and Mallik's own
biography is given in his own profile on this site.

## Recognition and memorials

Banglapedia records that the Bangladesh government granted 10 million taka in 1998 toward memorial facilities
at the school, leading to the establishment of a Matiur Rahman Memorial Library and a Matiur Rahman Memorial
Museum on campus ([Banglapedia](https://en.banglapedia.org/index.php/Nabakumar_Institute)). Dhaka Tribune
reports that the Bangladesh Awami League places wreaths annually at a memorial to Mallik at the school in
Bakshibazar ([Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/303385/historic-mass-upsurge-day-tuesday)).

## What this profile does not claim

This profile makes no claim about the school's present-day academic standing, size, or facilities beyond what
is stated on its own website, since no independent, present-day reporting on ordinary school operations was
