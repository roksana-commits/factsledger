# Smt. Sulochanadevi Singhania School

## Overview
Smt. Sulochanadevi Singhania School is a private co-educational institution situated in Thane, Maharashtra, India [1](https://www.singhaniaschool.org/). Founded in **1969** by the industrialist and philanthropist **Late Shri Gopalkrishna Singhania**, the school was established as a memorial to his wife [1](https://www.singhaniaschool.org/). The institution is managed by the **Smt. Sulochanadevi Singhania School Trust** and maintains a strategic association with the **Raymond Group**, a prominent Indian business conglomerate [1](https://www.singhaniaschool.org/). The school caters to students from the foundation stage (Junior KG) through the secondary stage (Standard 12) [1](https://www.singhaniaschool.org/).

## Recognition and accreditation
The institution is widely recognized for its high academic standards and holds multiple affiliations with national and international educational boards. It maintains a permanent affiliation with the **Council for the Indian School Certificate Examinations (CISCE)** under the identifier **MA046** [4](https://cisce.org/). In **2016**, the school achieved authorization as an **IB World School**, allowing it to offer the International Baccalaureate Diploma Programme (IBDP) to its senior students [2](https://ibo.org/programmes/find-an-ib-school/). Furthermore, the school is registered with the state education department with the **UDISE code 27212001710** [1](https://www.singhaniaschool.org/). In **2023**, the institution's students achieved significant academic success, securing the **All India Rank 2** in the ICSE national examinations [4](https://cisce.org/).

## Leadership and governance
The school's governance is directed by the Smt. Sulochanadevi Singhania School Trust, which is headquartered in Ballard Estate, Mumbai [1](https://www.singhaniaschool.org/). The trust ensures the school adheres to the vision of its founder, focusing on providing holistic education and setting benchmarks in the Indian educational sector [1](https://www.singhaniaschool.org/). The leadership emphasizes a balance between traditional values and modern pedagogical approaches [1](https://www.singhaniaschool.org/).

## Academic programmes
The school offers a diverse range of curricula to meet varying educational needs. Under the CISCE board, it provides the **Indian Certificate of Secondary Education (ICSE)** for Class 10 and the **Indian School Certificate (ISC)** for Class 12 [4](https://cisce.org/). The **IB Diploma Programme** serves as an international alternative for students in their final years of schooling [2](https://ibo.org/programmes/find-an-ib-school/). In **2026**, the school's educational footprint expanded with the inauguration of the **Singhania International School**, which offers the Cambridge curriculum [1](https://www.singhaniaschool.org/).

## Community activity
Holistic development is a core focus of the school, evidenced by its numerous student-led initiatives and community engagement programs. The institution hosts the **Singhania Model United Nations (ISMUN)** and **TEDx Youth** events, which are designed to foster global citizenship and leadership skills among students [5](https://www.cialfo.co/client-stories/cialfo-case-study-smt-sulochanadevi-singhania-school). The school's success in empowering its students has been highlighted in professional case studies focusing on educational excellence [5](https://www.cialfo.co/client-stories/cialfo-case-study-smt-sulochanadevi-singhania-school).
