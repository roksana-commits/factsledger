## Overview

Brihaspati Vidyasadan (BVS) is a private educational institution located in the Naxal area of Kathmandu, Nepal ([Official Website](https://bvs.edu.np/)). Established in 1985, the school occupies a significant campus of over 160,000 square meters situated within the historical Balmandir compound ([LinkedIn](https://np.linkedin.com/company/brihaspati-vidyasadan-school-bvs), [CollegesNepal](https://www.collegesnepal.com/brihaspati-college/)). The institution serves students from Grade 1 through Grade 12, providing a comprehensive educational environment that includes specialized facilities such as separate dining areas for different grade levels and an on-premise infirmary staffed by a doctor and nurses ([Official Website](https://bvs.edu.np/)).

## Recognition

The school received the British Council ISA Full Award for the documented cycle ([Spotlight Nepal](https://www.spotlightnepal.com/2020/10/21/23-nepali-schools-receive-british-councils-isa-2020/)). After enrolling in the program in 2018, Brihaspati Vidyasadan was awarded the "Full Award" for the 2020–2023 cycle ([British Council Press Release](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf), [British Council Case Study](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/case-studies/adapting-project-based-learning-pedagogy-whole-school-development)). This recognition is granted to schools that successfully integrate international dimensions into their curriculum and foster global citizenship among their students ([British Council Nepal](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/programmes/education/connecting-classrooms/international-school-award)).

## Leadership

The institution is currently led by Principal Kumar Thapa ([Official Website](https://bvs.edu.np/), [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/bvs.school/posts/1258586796308818/)). Under his leadership, the school has continued to expand its academic offerings and celebrated its 40th anniversary in January 2025 ([Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/bvs.school/videos/1326954368487597/)). The school's history includes an early relocation from Dhobi Khola to its current Naxal site in 1986 to accommodate its growing student body ([EverybodyWiki](https://en.everybodywiki.com/Brihaspati_Vidyasadan)).

## Educational programmes

Brihaspati Vidyasadan provides education across primary, lower secondary, and secondary levels. For its Plus 2 (Higher Secondary) program, the school offers streams in Science, Management, and Humanities ([Official Website](https://bvs.edu.np/), [Edusanjal](https://edusanjal.com/news/international-school-award-2022-british-council-nepal/)). In late 2025, the school announced the integration of the Oxford International Program into its curriculum to further enhance its academic standards ([Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/bvs.school/posts/1258586796308818/)).

## Community activity

The school emphasizes student leadership and community engagement through various initiatives. In May 2023, the institution held an investiture ceremony for its student council ([Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/bvs.school/posts/1407898664710963/)). Furthermore, students have demonstrated active engagement in media and public discourse, including a full-page feature in *The Himalayan Times* in December 2025 that was researched and written by the students themselves ([Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/bvs.school/posts/1280697614097736/)).

## Sports

Brihaspati Vidyasadan maintains an active presence in local inter-school sports competitions. In August 2026, the school's team was declared the Table Tennis Champions in a regional tournament ([Official Website](https://bvs.edu.np/)). Additionally, students have participated in broader cultural and talent competitions, such as the International Kids Queen title won by a BVS student in July 2026 ([Official Website](https://bvs.edu.np/)).
