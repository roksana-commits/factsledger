## Overview

Baylor International Academy is an educational institution located at Godam Chowk in Banepa-10, Kavre District, Nepal ([Baylor International Academy](https://baylor.edu.np/)) ([CollegeNP](https://www.collegenp.com/college/baylor-international-academy-kavre)). The institution was founded in 1996 under the name Banepa Valley Boarding School ([About Us](https://baylor.edu.np/about-us/)). It underwent a formal rebranding and establishment as Baylor International Academy in 2008 ([About Us](https://baylor.edu.np/about-us/)). On February 11, 2020, the academy inaugurated a newly constructed building to expand its physical infrastructure ([CollegeNP](https://www.collegenp.com/college/baylor-international-academy-kavre)).

## Recognition

The institution has been recognized by the British Council for integrating international dimensions into its curriculum. It received the British Council ISA Full Award for the 2014–2015 cycle ([British Council Nepal](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/programmes/education/connecting-classrooms/international-school-award)) ([EducateNepal](https://www.educatenepal.com/news/detail/nine-nepali-schools-receive-isa)). Subsequently, the school received the British Council ISA Full Award for the 2019–2020 cycle, a status valid for the 2020–2023 period ([British Council Nepal](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/programmes/education/connecting-classrooms/international-school-award)) ([Press Release](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf)). In 2019, Baylor International Academy was awarded ISO certification ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IYcZ5e5dtg4)). The school also maintains records of multiple victories in local inter-school competitions within the Kavre district ([Achievement](https://baylor.edu.np/achievement/)).

## Leadership

The academy operates as a comprehensive educational complex with affiliations to national academic bodies ([About Us](https://baylor.edu.np/about-us/)). Its higher education wing collaborates with Kathmandu University to deliver professional programs ([About Us](https://baylor.edu.np/about-us/)).

## Educational programmes

Baylor International Academy provides education ranging from early childhood to undergraduate levels ([Baylor International Academy](https://baylor.edu.np/)). The basic education track includes Montessori and Nursery programs through Grade 10 ([About Us](https://baylor.edu.np/about-us/)). Secondary education offerings include Science and Management streams for Grade 11 and 12 ([About Us](https://baylor.edu.np/about-us/)). At the undergraduate level, the institution offers a Bachelor in Public Health program in collaboration with Kathmandu University ([About Us](https://baylor.edu.np/about-us/)). The school features specialized infrastructure including four laboratories for Physics, Chemistry, Biology, and Computer Science ([Facilities & Features](https://baylor.edu.np/facilities-features/)). It also maintains both traditional and electronic library resources ([Facilities & Features](https://baylor.edu.np/facilities-features/)).

## Community activity

The institution facilitates a Student Quality Circle where students and faculty collaborate on educational problem-solving ([Facilities & Features](https://baylor.edu.np/facilities-features/)). Social service and humanitarian engagement are managed through a Youth Red Cross unit and dedicated Scout circles ([Facilities & Features](https://baylor.edu.np/facilities-features/)).

## Sports

The academy provides facilities for several athletic disciplines, including courts and equipment for basketball, volleyball, football, and table tennis ([Facilities & Features](https://baylor.edu.np/facilities-features/)).
