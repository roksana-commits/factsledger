## Overview

Hathausha Little Star Boarding School, also known as H. Little Star Secondary English Boarding School, is a private educational institution located in Banganga Municipality-11, Odari, Kapilvastu, within the Lumbini Province of Nepal [Official School Website](https://www.hathausalittlestarebs.com/) [Edusanjal School Profile](https://edusanjal.com/school/hathausa-little-star-english-boarding-school-banganga/). The school was established in approximately 2002 AD (2059 BS), as indicated by its official digital identifiers [Official TikTok Profile](https://www.tiktok.com/@h.littlestarebs2059). It operates as a private secondary school under the regulations of the Government of Nepal and is affiliated with the National Examinations Board (NEB) [Official School Website](https://www.hathausalittlestarebs.com/) [Edusanjal School Profile](https://edusanjal.com/school/hathausa-little-star-english-boarding-school-banganga/). As of the 2081 BS (2024 AD) academic year, the school reports an enrollment of 1,249 students [Edusanjal School Profile](https://edusanjal.com/school/hathausa-little-star-english-boarding-school-banganga/).

## Recognition

The school received the British Council ISA Full Award for the documented cycle (2019–20 Batch 1) [British Council Nepal ISA Award Winners List](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/programmes/education/connecting-classrooms/international-school-award) [Spotlight Nepal: 23 Nepali Schools Receive British Council’s International School Award 2020](https://www.spotlightnepal.com/2020/10/21/23-nepali-schools-receive-british-councils-international-school-award-2020/). This recognition, endorsed by the Center for Education and Human Resource Development (CEHRD) of the Government of Nepal, granted the school the right to use the ISA kite mark for a three-year period from 2020 to 2023 [Spotlight Nepal: 23 Nepali Schools Receive British Council’s International School Award 2020](https://www.spotlightnepal.com/2020/10/21/23-nepali-schools-receive-british-councils-international-school-award-2020/). In early 2026, the school's leadership gained further international attention when the Principal was selected as an international nominee for the Global Education & Leadership Summit held in Japan [Principal Sundar Pandey Global Nomination (TikTok)](https://www.tiktok.com/@h.littlestarebs2059/video/7603736987253640455).

## Educational programmes

Hathausha Little Star Boarding School provides a comprehensive educational track ranging from Early Childhood Development (ECD) and Montessori levels through Grade 12 [Official School Website](https://www.hathausalittlestarebs.com/) [Edusanjal School Profile](https://edusanjal.com/school/hathausa-little-star-english-boarding-school-banganga/). At the secondary level, the school offers a specialized Ten Plus Two (+2) Management stream under the NEB curriculum [Official School Website](https://www.hathausalittlestarebs.com/) [Edusanjal School Profile](https://edusanjal.com/school/hathausa-little-star-english-boarding-school-banganga/).
