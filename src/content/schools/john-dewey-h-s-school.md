## Overview

John Dewey H. S. School is a progressive educational institution located in Kapan Marg, Peepalbot, Maharajgunj, Kathmandu, Nepal [John Dewey H. S. School - About Us](https://johndewey.edu.np/about-us). Established circa 2008, the school adopts a child-centric learning approach rooted in progressive education philosophies, aiming to equip students with 21st-century skills such as critical thinking, communication, and collaboration [John Dewey H. S. School - About Us](https://johndewey.edu.np/about-us) [Independent Blog - John Dewey in Nepal (Nov 6, 2016)](https://chipbruce.net/2016/11/06/john-dewey-in-nepal/). As of 2026, the institution reports a community of approximately 340 students and 46 faculty members, with an alumni network of 94 individuals [John Dewey H. S. School - About Us](https://johndewey.edu.np/about-us). The school is accredited by the National Examination Board (NEB) of Nepal [John Dewey H. S. School - Plus 2 Programs](https://johndewey.edu.np/plus2).

## Recognition

The school received the British Council ISA Full Award for the documented cycle [British Council Nepal Press Release (Oct 20, 2020)](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf) [Spotlight Nepal (Oct 21, 2020)](https://www.spotlightnepal.com/2020/10/21/23-nepali-schools-receive-british-councils-international-school-award-2020/). Specifically, on October 20, 2020, the British Council Nepal, in collaboration with the Center for Education and Human Resource Development (CEHRD), granted the award to the school [British Council Nepal Press Release (Oct 20, 2020)](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf) [Spotlight Nepal (Oct 21, 2020)](https://www.spotlightnepal.com/2020/10/21/23-nepali-schools-receive-british-councils-international-school-award-2020/). Furthermore, the institution was distinguished as the winner of the "Most Innovative Project" within the same 2020 ISA cycle [British Council Nepal Press Release (Oct 20, 2020)](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf).

## Leadership

The institution has been operating under its current leadership for over a decade, with independent documentation from 2016 confirming the school's established presence and active management at that time [Independent Blog - John Dewey in Nepal (Nov 6, 2016)](https://chipbruce.net/2016/11/06/john-dewey-in-nepal/). The school's official records cite 18 years of operational experience as of 2026, reflecting a long-standing commitment to progressive education in the Kathmandu region [John Dewey H. S. School - About Us](https://johndewey.edu.np/about-us).

## Educational programmes

John Dewey H. S. School provides a comprehensive educational pathway starting from its Kindergarten program, branded as "Little JD Stars," through to Grade 10 [John Dewey H. S. School - About Us](https://johndewey.edu.np/about-us). For higher secondary education, the school offers Plus 2 programs in Management, Hotel Management, and Humanities [John Dewey H. S. School - Plus 2 Programs](https://johndewey.edu.np/plus2). Additionally, the school serves as a recognized SAT exam center in Kathmandu (Center Code: 74214), facilitating international standardized testing for students in the region [Search Education Nepal - SAT Exam Centers](https://www.searcheducation.com.np/test-preparation/sat).

## Community activity

The school actively engages in cultural and developmental initiatives. It participated in the British Council's "Camara Chica" project, a filmmaking initiative designed to empower youth aged 12 to 16 through visual storytelling and creative expression [British Council Progress Report - Camara Chica](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/2020.04.06_camara_sika_progress_report.pdf). In 2016, the school was also involved in the "Surya Nepal Jazzmandu" event, specifically the "Jazz for students" session, which aimed to introduce students to diverse musical genres and artistic performances [The Himalayan Times (Oct 21, 2016)](https://thehimalayantimes.com/entertainment/surya-nepal-jazzmandu-2016-for-students).
