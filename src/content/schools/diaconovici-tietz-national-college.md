## Overview

Diaconovici-Tietz National College is a bilingual Romanian-German secondary school in Reșița, in Romania's
Banat region, founded on 7 October 1877 as a communal school for the children of local steelworks and mining
families ([Colegiul Național Diaconovici-Tietz](https://cndt.ro/)). It has changed names and structure repeatedly
across Austro-Hungarian, Romanian, and Communist-era administrations, and was elevated to Colegiul Național
(National College) rank in 2016. Locally it is known as "Bastilia" ("the Bastille"), for the fortress-like stone
building it has occupied for well over a century
([Radio România Reșița](https://www.radioresita.ro/fapt-divers/emotii-si-amintiri-dupa-50-de-ani-promotia-1976-a-bastiliei-din-resita-s-a-reunit-la-colegiul-national-diaconovici-tietz)).

## 140 years, and a notable alumnus

The school marked 140 years of operation in October 2017; Caon.ro reported that Reșița mayor Ioan Popa attended
the celebration and remarked that "140 de ani e o cifră care îndeamnă la respect" ("140 years is a number that
commands respect"). The same report put enrollment at around 950 students across preschool through high school
levels, and named mathematician Ciprian Foiaș among the school's notable alumni
([Caon.ro](https://caon.ro/bastilia-a-sarbatorit-140-de-ani-de-invatamant/1911244/)).

Foiaș's own notability is independently well established: the American Mathematical Society's memorial notice
records that "Ciprian Ilie Foias was born on July 20, 1933, in Resita, Romania," and that in 1995 he "was awarded
the very prestigious Norbert Wiener Prize from the American Mathematical Society and the Society for Industrial
and Applied Mathematics for his extensive research in applied mathematics"
([Notices of the American Mathematical Society](https://www.ams.org/journals/notices/202209/noti2545/noti2545.html)).
That notice confirms his birthplace but does not name a specific school; his attendance at this college in
particular is reported only by the school and by local press that appears to draw on the school's own
alumni list.

## International exchanges

The school maintains European student-exchange partnerships. In May 2025, it hosted eight students and two
teachers from Gesamtschule Else Lasker-Schüler in Wuppertal, Germany, for a week-long Erasmus+ visit that
included classroom observation, a tour of the Radio România Reșița studios, and a joint Europe Day celebration,
coordinated by deputy director Sonia Maria Chwoika
([Radio România Reșița](https://www.radioresita.ro/actualitate/vizita-erasmus-la-colegiul-national-diaconovici-tietz-resita-parteneriat-international-cu-germania-5-9-mai-2025)).
The school's own materials describe a 2019–2023 renovation, funded in part by EU project money at roughly €1.1
million, that modernised the building's systems while preserving its historic façade
([Colegiul Național Diaconovici-Tietz](https://cndt.ro/)).

## What this profile does not claim

This profile does not assert, as independently confirmed, that Ciprian Foiaș personally attended this specific
school — only that he was born in the same city and that the school and local press name him as an alumnus.
It does not independently verify the €1.1 million EU renovation figure beyond the school's own account.
