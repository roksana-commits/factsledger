# Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya, Nizamsagar

## Overview
The **Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya, Nizamsagar**, officially designated as **PM SHRI JNV Nizamsagar**, is a co-educational residential school located in the Nizamsagar Mandal of the Kamareddy district, Telangana, India [1]. Established in **1987**, the institution was originally founded to serve the Nizamabad district as part of a national initiative to provide high-quality education to gifted children from rural backgrounds [6]. Following the administrative reorganization of Telangana in 2016, the school's location was incorporated into the newly formed Kamareddy District, though it retains its historical identity in various official records [1].

## Recognition and accreditation
The institution is affiliated with the **Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE)** under affiliation number **3640001** and school code **59519** [4]. In the **2023-2024** academic period, the school was selected for the **PM SHRI School** (Prime Minister Schools for Rising India) initiative, a central government program aimed at developing model schools that showcase the implementation of the National Education Policy 2020 [2] [3]. Furthermore, the school hosts an **Atal Tinkering Lab (ATL)**, established in **2019** with funding from NITI Aayog to foster scientific inquiry and innovation among students [5].

## Leadership and governance
Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya, Nizamsagar operates under the **Navodaya Vidyalaya Samiti (NVS)**, an autonomous organization under the Ministry of Education, Government of India [1]. The school is managed as a central government institution, with its procurement and manpower operations integrated into the Government e-Marketplace (GeM) [3]. It is officially identified by the UDISE code **36150902808** [1] [5].

## Academic programmes
The school provides comprehensive residential education from **Class 6 through Class 12** [7]. The curriculum follows CBSE standards, emphasizing a holistic approach to development and the three-language formula. The institution's infrastructure supports a fully residential environment, catering to the needs of students selected through the Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya Selection Test (JNVST) [7].

## Community activity
The school actively participates in national events, such as National Space Day, and maintains a strong presence in regional athletics [2]. Its alumni network includes individuals who have secured placements in the **Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs)** and international universities, as well as those serving in high-ranking government positions such as Deputy Superintendents of Police and District Forest Officers [6].
