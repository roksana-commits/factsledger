# Suraj Bhan DAV Public School

Suraj Bhan DAV Public School is a co-educational senior secondary institution located in the Vasant Vihar district of New Delhi, India [1] [2]. Founded in 1984, the school is a constituent unit of the **DAV College Managing Committee (DAVCMC)**, which is one of the largest non-governmental educational organizations in India [1] [3]. The school provides a comprehensive educational framework following the principles of the Arya Samaj and the educational vision of Swami Dayanand Saraswati [3] [6].

## Recognition and Accreditation

The school is formally recognized by the **Directorate of Education, Government of NCT Delhi**, and maintains a long-standing affiliation with the **Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE)** [1] [4]. It operates under CBSE affiliation number 2730139 and school code 25092 [1] [4]. Furthermore, the institution adheres to municipal safety regulations, holding a valid Fire Safety Certificate issued by the Delhi Fire Service following rigorous site inspections [8].

## Leadership and Governance

The governance of the school is overseen by the DAVCMC, an organization that manages hundreds of educational institutions across India [3]. The school is named in honor of **Lala Suraj Bhan** (Padma Bhushan Suraj Bhan), a distinguished educator who served as the President of the DAV College Managing Committee from 1981 to 1991 [5] [6]. The day-to-day administrative and academic operations are currently led by the Principal, **Mrs. Gargi Chhabra** [1].

## Campus and Infrastructure

The school occupies a **2.61-acre campus** in Vasant Vihar [1]. The primary academic infrastructure consists of a three-storey building containing **85 rooms**, which include specialized science and computer laboratories, a well-stocked library, and dedicated spaces for performing arts [1] [7]. The campus is designed to support both academic rigor and extracurricular development, featuring facilities for various sports and physical education programs [1] [7].

## Academic Programmes

Suraj Bhan DAV Public School offers a continuous educational path from **Nursery to Class 12** [1] [4]. At the senior secondary level (Classes 11 and 12), the school provides three primary academic streams:
*   **Science:** Focusing on medical and non-medical disciplines.
*   **Commerce:** Including accountancy, business studies, and economics.
*   **Humanities:** Offering a range of social science subjects [1].

Students at the school have historically demonstrated strong academic performance in national board examinations, with frequent mentions of high achievement in CBSE results [9].

## Community Activity

The institution emphasizes social responsibility and community engagement through various initiatives. In August 2026, the school organized a **"Roti Bank Collection Drive"** to support social awareness and food security programs [1]. The school also integrates cultural awareness into its curriculum, such as organizing special assemblies for International Dance Day, which has received coverage in regional press like the *Hindustan Times* [1]. In early 2026, the school was recognized as a **"Change Maker School"** by Times Now, highlighting its efforts in educational innovation [10].
