# Arjun English Boarding High School

## Overview
Arjun English Boarding High School, also identified as Arjun Boarding Secondary School, is a private co-educational institution situated in [Resunga Municipality-01, Tamghas, Gulmi](https://www.nepalyp.com/company/41018/Arjun_Boarding_High_School) [Official School Website](https://www.arjunboarding.edu.np/). The institution was established in [1984 AD](https://www.facebook.com/100092326651370/) (2040 BS) and offers both boarding and day-school services to students in the Lumbini Province [Official School Website](https://www.arjunboarding.edu.np/). It is affiliated with the [National Examination Board (NEB)](https://edusanjal.com/school/arjun-awasiya-higher-secondary-school/) and reports an enrollment of approximately [663 students](https://edusanjal.com/school/arjun-awasiya-higher-secondary-school/).

## Recognition
The school received the [British Council ISA Full Award](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/programmes/education/connecting-classrooms/international-school-award) for the documented [2019-2020 cycle](https://www.spotlightnepal.com/2020/10/21/23-nepali-schools-receive-british-councils-international-school-award-2020/) (Batch 1). This recognition, endorsed by the Center for Education and Human Resource Development (CEHRD), signifies the integration of global perspectives within the school's curriculum [Spotlight Nepal](https://www.spotlightnepal.com/2020/10/21/23-nepali-schools-receive-british-councils-international-school-award-2020/). Through the ISA framework, the institution developed educational [partnerships and collaborations](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/case-studies/connecting-schools-around-world-through-isa) with schools in the United Kingdom, India, Pakistan, Taiwan, and Tunisia [British Council Nepal](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/case-studies/connecting-schools-around-world-through-isa).

## Leadership
The school was founded by [Mr. Dan Bahadur K.C.](https://www.arjunboarding.edu.np/), who serves as the Principal [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/100092326651370/). The administrative team includes Vice Principal [Vivek Panthi](https://www.arjunboarding.edu.np/) and a Board of Directors that includes [Mr. Dilli Raj Bhusal and Mr. Surya Bahadur K.C.](https://www.arjunboarding.edu.np/). The leadership focuses on maintaining a disciplined environment aimed at the academic and moral growth of its student body [Official School Website](https://www.arjunboarding.edu.np/).

## Educational programmes
Arjun English Boarding High School provides education from [Nursery to Grade 12](https://edusanjal.com/school/arjun-awasiya-higher-secondary-school/), including Higher Secondary (+2) streams. The academic infrastructure includes [science laboratories](https://www.arjunboarding.edu.np/) for Physics, Chemistry, and Biology, a [computer lab](https://www.arjunboarding.edu.np/) for digital literacy, a [library](https://www.arjunboarding.edu.np/) with various research tools, and dedicated spaces for [arts and music](https://www.arjunboarding.edu.np/).

## Community activity
The institution engages in [international student exchanges](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/case-studies/connecting-schools-around-world-through-isa) and collaborative projects to promote global citizenship. In early 2020, a group of eight students participated in an exchange visit to [Prestige Public School](https://www.facebook.com/prestigepublicschool.official/posts/8-students-from-arjun-english-boarding-high-school-gulmi-nepal-visited-pps-as-a-/2611456099088676/) as part of the ISA-related initiatives [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/prestigepublicschool.official/posts/8-students-from-arjun-english-boarding-high-school-gulmi-nepal-visited-pps-as-a-/2611456099088676/). These programs are intended to foster [21st-century skills](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/case-studies/connecting-schools-around-world-through-isa) and cultural awareness.

## Sports
The campus includes a [spacious playground](https://www.arjunboarding.edu.np/) for sports and physical education, which supports the school's focus on holistic student development [Official School Website](https://www.arjunboarding.edu.np/).
