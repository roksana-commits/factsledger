## Overview
The **National Creative Co-educational School (NCCS)** is a private educational institution situated on Banshidhar Marg in the Chandol-4 district of Kathmandu, Nepal [[4]](https://edusanjal.com/school/national-creative-co-educational-school/). The school is a component of the broader NCCS Group, which also encompasses the National College of Computer Studies and the National Integrated College [[3]](https://www.nccs.edu.np/school). It is formally affiliated with the **Private Association of Boarding and Schools' Organization Nepal (PABSON)** and adheres to the curriculum standards established by the **National Examinations Board (NEB)** [[4]](https://edusanjal.com/school/national-creative-co-educational-school/). The institution moved to its current purpose-built facility in 2013 to enhance its learning environment [[3]](https://www.nccs.edu.np/school).

## Recognition
NCCS received the **British Council International School Award (ISA) Full Award** for the 2019–20 cycle (Batch 1) [[1]](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/programmes/education/connecting-classrooms/international-school-award) [[2]](https://www.spotlightnepal.com/2020/10/21/23-nepali-schools-receive-british-councils-international-school-award-2020/). The award was formally announced during a virtual ceremony on October 20, 2020, following an evaluation of the school's ability to integrate international dimensions into its curriculum [[2]](https://www.spotlightnepal.com/2020/10/21/23-nepali-schools-receive-british-councils-international-school-award-2020/). This recognition, endorsed by the Center for Education and Human Resource Development (CEHRD), permitted the school to utilize the ISA kite mark on official materials through 2023 [[2]](https://www.spotlightnepal.com/2020/10/21/23-nepali-schools-receive-british-councils-international-school-award-2020/). The profile does not claim that the school is currently accredited beyond the documented cycle.

## Leadership
The school's administrative and academic operations are led by **Principal Shankar Raj Jaisi**, who has been documented in this leadership capacity since at least 2022 [[5]](https://www.collegenp.com/news/nccs-college-concludes-orientation-program-for-bim-bhm-bsccsit-bbm-and-bca).

## Educational programmes
The school provides education from Pre-school (Playgroup) through Grade 12 [[4]](https://edusanjal.com/school/national-creative-co-educational-school/). At the secondary level, it offers specialized "Ten Plus Two" (+2) programs in both **Science and Management** streams [[4]](https://edusanjal.com/school/national-creative-co-educational-school/). The institution reported an enrollment of **591 students** for the 2081/82 BS (2024-2025) academic session [[4]](https://edusanjal.com/school/national-creative-co-educational-school/). A significant feature of its curriculum is a multi-year partnership with **Karkhana**, an organization that facilitates STEM and maker-based learning activities focused on critical thinking and innovation [[6]](https://www.facebook.com/karkhana.asia/posts/1107788338050281/). The school's infrastructure includes a computer lab with a 30-student capacity, a science laboratory, and an audio/visual room for e-learning [[4]](https://edusanjal.com/school/national-creative-co-educational-school/).

## Community activity
The institution manages the **Dakshya Kumari Som Raj Scholarship**, which provides merit-based financial assistance to high-achieving students, including those entering Grade 1 [[3]](https://www.nccs.edu.np/school).
