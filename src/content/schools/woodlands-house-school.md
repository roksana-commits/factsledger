# Overview

Woodlands House School is a private, English-medium educational institution situated in Srinagar, the summer capital of Jammu and Kashmir, India [1] [2]. Established in 1966 by Mrs. Rup S.P. Singh, the school operates at the foothills of the Shankaracharya Temple, maintaining a significant presence in the region's academic landscape [3]. The institution is guided by the motto **"Work is Devotion"** and serves a student population ranging from pre-nursery to the higher secondary level [3].

The school maintains two primary administrative units to accommodate its students: the main campus located on Gupkar Road and a dedicated wing for female students in Shivpora [1] [3].

# Recognition and accreditation

The institution is formally recognized and regulated by the **Jammu and Kashmir Board of School Education (JKBOSE)** [1]. Official government records, including the 2025 Class 10th examination centre notices, confirm the school's status as a registered venue for public examinations under the regional statutory framework [1].

In terms of regional standing, Woodlands House School has consistently appeared in professional educational assessments. The institution was ranked 3rd among day schools in Srinagar by the **Education World India School Rankings** for the 2023-24 cycle, an evaluation based on teacher competence, infrastructure, and academic reputation [2].

# Leadership and governance

The school was founded by Mrs. Rup S.P. Singh, who established the institution's initial framework in 1966 [3]. Governance is conducted through centralized administrative structures that oversee both the Gupkar Road and Shivpora campuses [3]. The institution's longevity and stability are reflected in its continuous operation for six decades within the Srinagar district [4].

# Academic programmes

Woodlands House School offers a comprehensive curriculum that spans from early childhood education (Pre-Nursery) to Class 12th [3]. As an English-medium school affiliated with JKBOSE, it follows the state-mandated academic standards while integrating regional educational requirements [1] [3]. The school's academic environment is supported by facilities that enable both scientific and artistic development, as evidenced by its regular hosting of exhibitions [4].

# Community activity

The institution maintains active engagement with the local community through commemorative events and educational showcases. In September 2023, the school celebrated its 57th Foundation Day, marking over half a century of service to the region [4]. More recently, in August 2026, the school observed its **Diamond Jubilee (60th Anniversary)** [4]. This milestone was commemorated with a Science & Art Exhibition held on August 22, 2026, which showcased the creative and technical work of its students [4].
