# Overview

Adarsh Shiksha Niketan Senior Secondary School, commonly known as ASN Senior Secondary School, is a K-12 educational institution located in Mayur Vihar, New Delhi [1] [2]. Established in **1975** by the educationist **Shri K. L. Luthra**, the school was founded with the mission of providing holistic education integrated with Indian cultural values [1] [4]. The institution operates from a **3.5-acre campus** in East Delhi, which includes digitalized classrooms, specialized laboratories, and various sports facilities [1].

# Recognition and accreditation

The school is recognized by the **Delhi Administration** and maintains a long-standing affiliation with the **Central Board of Secondary Education (CBSE)** under affiliation number **2730203** [4]. It is also identified in the Unified District Information System for Education (UDISE+) with the code **07040322008** [5] [6]. The institution has received multiple international recognitions, including the **International School Award (ISA)** and the **Recognition of International Dimension in Schools (RIDS)** from the British Council [1] [8].

# Leadership and governance

ASN Senior Secondary School is managed by the **Sanatan Dharm Adarsh Shiksha Sansthan**, a government-registered educational society [1] [4]. The institution remains under the leadership of the Luthra family, with **Ms. Sonia Luthra** serving as the Principal as of 2026 [4].

# Academic programmes

The school offers a comprehensive curriculum from **Nursery to Class 12** [1]. The senior secondary wing provides specialized academic streams in **Science, Commerce, and Humanities** [7]. The school is noted for its international collaborations, including partnerships with the **Goethe Institute (Max Muller Bhawan)** for German language education and the **Korean Cultural Centre** for cultural exchange programmes [1]. Students also participate in international educational trips, such as visits to the **Kennedy Space Centre** in the USA [1].

# Community activity

The school actively engages in environmental and community-focused initiatives. It was awarded the **Green Leaf School Award** by the Centre for Science and Environment (CSE) in 2018 and the **Green School Award** by Green Mentors in 2021 [9] [10]. Additionally, the institution hosts professional development activities, such as a two-day **Journalism Programme** held in May 2026 [11].
