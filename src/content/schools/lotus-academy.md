## Overview

Lotus Academy, also known as Lotus Academy English Boarding School, is a private educational institution located in Shantinagar, Simara, within the Jitpur Simara-2 sub-metropolitan city of Bara District, Nepal [1](https://edusanjal.com/school/lotus-academy-jitpur-simara/) [2](https://www.myfreeadmission.com/college/lotus-academy-1854/salient-features/) [3](http://wikimapia.org/30066340/Lotus-Academy-English-Boarding-School-Simara-Bara-5). Established in 2057 B.S. (approximately 2000 AD), the school provides education from Early Childhood Development (ECD) through Grade 12 [1](https://edusanjal.com/school/lotus-academy-jitpur-simara/) [2](https://www.myfreeadmission.com/college/lotus-academy-1854/salient-features/). As of the 2081 IEMIS report by the Center for Education and Human Resource Development, the institution serves a student population of 918 [1](https://edusanjal.com/school/lotus-academy-jitpur-simara/). The school operates as a co-educational facility and offers both day scholar and boarding options for its students [1](https://edusanjal.com/school/lotus-academy-jitpur-simara/) [3](http://wikimapia.org/30066340/Lotus-Academy-English-Boarding-School-Simara-Bara-5).

## Recognition

The school achieved international recognition when it received the British Council ISA Full Award for the documented 2020–2023 cycle [6](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf) [7](https://www.spotlightnepal.com/2020/10/21/23-nepali-schools-receive-british-councils-international-school-award-2020/). The award was formally announced in a British Council Nepal press release on October 20, 2020 [6](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf). This recognition was part of a broader initiative where 23 schools across Nepal were honored for their commitment to embedding internationalism into their curriculum [7](https://www.spotlightnepal.com/2020/10/21/23-nepali-schools-receive-british-councils-international-school-award-2020/).

## Leadership

Lotus Academy is led by Principal Ram Prasad Adhikari [4](https://www.scribd.com/document/329072841/NCSQC-Handbook-16). Beyond his institutional responsibilities, Adhikari has been active in the broader educational community, notably serving as the President of the National Private and Boarding Schools' Organization Nepal (N-PABSON) for the Bara district [4](https://www.scribd.com/document/329072841/NCSQC-Handbook-16).

## Educational programmes

The institution offers a comprehensive academic path starting from Early Childhood Development (ECD) and extending to the secondary level [1](https://edusanjal.com/school/lotus-academy-jitpur-simara/). For the Ten Plus Two (+2) secondary stream, the school provides a Management track affiliated with the National Examinations Board (NEB) [1](https://edusanjal.com/school/lotus-academy-jitpur-simara/).

## Community activity

Lotus Academy engages in various community and collaborative educational activities. In 2016, the school participated in the National Convention on Students' Quality Circles (NCSQC) Hub Convention [4](https://www.scribd.com/document/329072841/NCSQC-Handbook-16). Additionally, the school hosts cultural events, such as a program held in Simara in January 2023, which featured student performances in dance and arts [9](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W28F_ioWjXQ).

## Sports

The school maintains an active presence in regional sports, particularly football and martial arts. In 2017, the football team reached the semi-finals of the CG Education Cup in Birgunj after a notable 7-0 victory against Dawan Academy [8](https://www.goalnepal.com/news/detail/625). More recently, the school recorded victories against Solidarity in December 2023, and both Brighter Academy and New Ekata School in August 2024 [10](https://www.facebook.com/goalnepalofficial/posts/1980075212010419/). Furthermore, Lotus Academy serves as a Taekwondo training center, hosting the Gajuri Taekwondo Dojang [5](https://taekwondonepal.org.np/training/lotus-academy/).
