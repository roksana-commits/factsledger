## Overview

Holy Vision Secondary School (HVSS), also known as **Holy Vision Higher Secondary School**, is a private academic institution located in Tahachal Marg, Kathmandu [Edusanjal Institutional Profile](https://edusanjal.com/school/holy-vision-higher-secondary-school/) [Collegenp College Profile](https://www.collegenp.com/college/holy-vision-secondary-school-kathmandu). Operated as a not-for-profit entity under the **Holy Vision Educational Trust**, the school serves a student body of approximately 1,000 [Official Website](https://www.holyvision.edu.np/). It was formally established in 1992 (2049 B.S.) with the approval of the District Education Office, Kathmandu, initially functioning as a primary school before expanding into a full secondary institution [Edusanjal Institutional Profile](https://edusanjal.com/school/holy-vision-higher-secondary-school/).

## Recognition

The school achieved international recognition by receiving the **British Council ISA Full Award for the documented cycle** of 2019–2020 [British Council Nepal ISA Press Release](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf). The accreditation, granted by the British Council Nepal and endorsed by the Center for Education and Human Resource Development (CEHRD), acknowledged the school's efforts in integrating international dimensions into its curriculum [British Council Nepal ISA Press Release](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf). The award cycle formally concluded in 2023 [British Council Nepal ISA Press Release](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf).

## Leadership

Holy Vision Secondary School is managed by the **Holy Vision Educational Trust**, which oversees its strategic direction and operations as a trustee-run institution [Official Website](https://www.holyvision.edu.np/). The school emphasizes stability and community engagement, as evidenced by the celebration of its 32nd Annual and Parents' Day in February 2025 [Official Website](https://www.holyvision.edu.np/).

## Educational programmes

The institution offers a comprehensive educational path from Playgroup to Grade 12 (+2) [Edusanjal Institutional Profile](https://edusanjal.com/school/holy-vision-higher-secondary-school/). At the higher secondary level, students can enroll in specialized streams including **Science, Management, and Humanities** [Collegenp College Profile](https://www.collegenp.com/college/holy-vision-secondary-school-kathmandu). The curriculum is designed to meet national standards while incorporating global citizenship elements recognized by international accreditation bodies [British Council Nepal ISA Press Release](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf).

## Community activity

The school maintains active engagement with its community through regular events and milestones. On February 8, 2025, the school conducted its **32nd Annual and Parents' Day** ceremony, marking over three decades of operation in the Kathmandu Valley [Official Website](https://www.holyvision.edu.np/).

## Sports

Holy Vision Secondary School provides dedicated facilities for various sports, including courts for **soccer, cricket, and basketball** [HVPSS Activities Portal](https://hvpss.edu.np/activities/). The school has also achieved regional success in martial arts; in July 2024, student **Agrim Shrestha** won first place in the Kumite – 70 kg category at the **2nd South Asian Open** Karate championship [Official Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/holyvision_1992/).
