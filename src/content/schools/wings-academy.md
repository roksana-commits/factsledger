## Overview

Wings Academy is an academic institution located in Chundevi-5, Suryabinayak, Bhaktapur, Nepal [3](https://edusanjal.com/school/wings-academy-suryabinayak/) [4](https://www.bhaktapur.com/directory/wings-school/). Situated in a peaceful environment near the highway, the school provides a learning atmosphere designed for both early childhood and secondary education [4](https://www.bhaktapur.com/directory/wings-school/). Originally founded as Wings Montessori School, the institution has evolved into a comprehensive academy that emphasizes 21st-century skills, including life and career readiness, learning and innovation, and information technology literacy [1](https://wingsacademy.edu.np/) [6](https://www.facebook.com/wingsschool7/).

## Recognition

The school received the British Council ISA Full Award for the documented cycle of 2019-20 [2](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/programmes/education/connecting-classrooms/international-school-award). This accreditation honors institutions for integrating international dimensions into their curriculum [2](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/programmes/education/connecting-classrooms/international-school-award) [5](https://www.spotlightnepal.com/2020/10/21/23-nepali-schools-receive-british-councils-international-school-award-2020/). The award was formally announced during a virtual ceremony held on October 20, 2020, allowing the school to utilize the ISA kite mark on its official materials for the subsequent three-year period [5](https://www.spotlightnepal.com/2020/10/21/23-nepali-schools-receive-british-councils-international-school-award-2020/).

## Educational programmes

The institution began its operations in 2012 with a focus on the Montessori method for early childhood development [1](https://wingsacademy.edu.np/) [3](https://edusanjal.com/school/wings-academy-suryabinayak/). Over the following decade, it expanded its academic offerings, and as of the 2023/24 academic session (2080/81 BS), the school provides education up to Grade X [1](https://wingsacademy.edu.np/) [3](https://edusanjal.com/school/wings-academy-suryabinayak/).

In April 2025, Wings Academy modernized its technical curriculum by collaborating with DigiSchool Global to implement the NCC Education (UK) IT curriculum [8](https://www.facebook.com/thedigischoolglobal/videos/674920348461407/). This program is designed to align the school's information technology education with international standards [8](https://www.facebook.com/thedigischoolglobal/videos/674920348461407/).

## Community activity

The school actively promotes scientific inquiry through its annual "WINGS STEAM FAIR," with the most recent event conducted in February 2026 [1](https://wingsacademy.edu.np/) [6](https://www.facebook.com/wingsschool7/). Additionally, the institution hosts an Agora Speakers Club, which focuses on the development of public speaking and communication skills among students [1](https://wingsacademy.edu.np/). To foster environmental awareness, the school organizes experiential learning trips, such as visits to Hydroponics Nepal [1](https://wingsacademy.edu.np/) [6](https://www.facebook.com/wingsschool7/).
