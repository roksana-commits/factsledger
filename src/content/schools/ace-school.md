## Overview

Ace School is a private K-10 educational institution [[3](https://www.aceschool.edu.np/)] [[4](https://www.collegenp.com/school/ace-school)] located in Gairigaoun-9, Kathmandu, Nepal [[5](https://edusanjal.com/school/ace-school/)] [[6](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vKxeKLG192s)]. The school was established in 2014 (2071 BS) [[7](https://www.instagram.com/p/DTovILrjIVk/)] and is managed by Ace Education Ventures [[3](https://www.aceschool.edu.np/)] [[4](https://www.collegenp.com/school/ace-school)]. It was founded as an extension of the Ace Institute of Management to provide primary and secondary education within the Kathmandu Valley [[5](https://edusanjal.com/school/ace-school/)].

## Recognition

The school received the British Council ISA Full Award for the documented 2016-17 cycle [[1](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/programmes/education/connecting-classrooms/international-school-award)] [[8](https://www.facebook.com/aceschoolnepal/posts/816420291858674/)]. It subsequently received the British Council ISA Full Award for the 2019-20 cycle [[2](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf)].

## Leadership

The institution is led by Founder and Chairman Dr. Ajay Ghimire [[6](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vKxeKLG192s)]. Under his leadership, the school promotes a "Thinking Classroom" environment focusing on active learning and character development [[3](https://www.aceschool.edu.np/)] [[6](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vKxeKLG192s)].

## Educational programmes

Ace School provides education from the primary level through to Grade 10 [[5](https://edusanjal.com/school/ace-school/)]. The school's pedagogical approach, known as "Aceperience," is based on the pillars of Freedom, Belonging, Growth, and Transformation [[3](https://www.aceschool.edu.np/)]. The curriculum incorporates the "Thinking Classrooms" methodology to foster critical thinking [[3](https://www.aceschool.edu.np/)].

## Community activity

The school organizes several annual events including "Kaleidoscope," an inter-school festival [[3](https://www.aceschool.edu.np/)], and "Ace Bazaar," which showcases student entrepreneurship [[3](https://www.aceschool.edu.np/)]. It also hosts "Ace MUN," a Model United Nations conference [[9](https://www.facebook.com/aceschoolnepal/posts/989723646925708/)], and the "Mathletes Olympiad" mathematics competition [[10](https://www.facebook.com/aceschoolnepal/posts/1105644065333665/)]. Additional activities include theater productions and Family Day celebrations [[3](https://www.aceschool.edu.np/)].

## Sports

The school operates a Sports club and an Event Management club as part of its co-curricular offerings [[3](https://www.aceschool.edu.np/)].
