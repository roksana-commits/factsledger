# Overview

Gurukul Academy is a private educational institution located in the Uttar Behadi-4 area of Dhangadhi Sub-Metropolitan City, within the Kailali District of Sudurpashchim Province, Nepal ([Gurukul Academy Official Website](https://www.gurukulacademy.edu.np/)) ([Edusanjal: Gurukul Academy Profile](https://edusanjal.com/school/gurukul-academy-uttar-behadi-dhangadhi/)). The school is recognized as a significant educational provider in the region, serving a student body of over 1,247 individuals with a faculty exceeding 87 members ([Gurukul Academy Official Website](https://www.gurukulacademy.edu.np/)). While the school traces its origins to 1956, its modern institutional identity is also associated with a registration date of 2052 BS (approximately 1995–1996 AD) ([Gurukul Academy Official Website](https://www.gurukulacademy.edu.np/)) ([Gurukul Academy Official Facebook Page](https://www.facebook.com/gurukulacademydhn/)). The institution maintains a documented university acceptance rate of 97% ([Gurukul Academy Official Website](https://www.gurukulacademy.edu.np/)).

# Recognition

The institution has achieved sustained recognition through the British Council’s International School Award (ISA) program, which validates the integration of international dimensions into the school curriculum. Gurukul Academy received the British Council ISA Full Award for the 2012–2015 cycle ([My Republica: British Council awards schools with ISA (2013)](https://www.myrepublica.nagariknetwork.com/news/british-council-awards-schools-with-isa)). This was followed by a successful re-accreditation for the 2016–2019 period ([The Himalayan Times: 11 schools awarded International School Award (2016)](https://thehimalayantimes.com/entertainment/11-schools-awarded-international-school-award)). In October 2020, the school received the British Council ISA Full Award for the 2020–2023 cycle during a virtual ceremony organized by the British Council Nepal ([Ratopati: Gurukul Academy bags international award for third time (2020)](https://english.ratopati.com/story/15992/award)) ([Spotlight Nepal: 23 Nepali Schools Receive British Council's ISA 2020](https://www.spotlightnepal.com/2020/10/21/23-nepali-schools-receive-british-councils-international-school-award-2020/)). In 2025, the school's academic performance was highlighted at the National Science Olympiad, where a team of six students secured three gold and two silver medals, competing against representatives from 240 schools ([Gurukul Academy Official Website](https://www.gurukulacademy.edu.np/)).

# Leadership

The school is governed under the leadership of Chairman Deepak Suri ([Gurukul Academy Official Website](https://www.gurukulacademy.edu.np/)). The administrative and academic operations are directed by Principal Sunita Swar Suri ([Gurukul Academy Official Website](https://www.gurukulacademy.edu.np/)).

# Educational programmes

Gurukul Academy provides education ranging from primary to secondary levels. The institution expanded its offerings to include secondary education (Classes 6–10) in 1968 ([Gurukul Academy Official Website](https://www.gurukulacademy.edu.np/)). In March 2025, the school inaugurated a STEM Innovation Centre, which features specialized facilities such as robotics laboratories and 3D printing equipment to support modern technical education ([Gurukul Academy Official Website](https://www.gurukulacademy.edu.np/)).
