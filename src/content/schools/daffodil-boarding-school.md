## Overview

Daffodil Boarding School is a coeducational institution located in the Kathmandu Valley of Nepal [5](https://daffodil.edu.np/). Established in 2047 BS (approximately 1990 AD), the institution operates as a unified educational group consisting of two sister campuses: **Daffodil Boarding School (DBS)** and **Daffodil Public School (DPS)** [5](https://daffodil.edu.np/). The group provides education from the Nursery level through to Secondary education, following the National Curriculum Framework of Nepal supplemented by international benchmarks [5](https://daffodil.edu.np/).

The institutions are distributed across two primary locations in Kathmandu:

| Campus | Specific Location | Principal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Daffodil Boarding School | Budhanilkantha-10, Kapan (Saraswatinagar) | Ashok Sundar Shrestha |
| Daffodil Public School | Gokarneshwor-1, Nayapati | Maya Lama |

The dual-campus structure allows the management team to serve different administrative and academic roles within the region [5](https://daffodil.edu.np/).

## Recognition

Daffodil Boarding School is recognized for its early adoption of international quality and environmental standards [4](https://edusanjal.com/school/daffodil-school/). It was the first educational institution in Nepal to achieve dual certifications from the International Organization for Standardization: **ISO 9001:2000** for Quality Management and **ISO 14001:2004** for Environmental Management [4](https://edusanjal.com/school/daffodil-school/) [5](https://daffodil.edu.np/).

Furthermore, both campuses have been recipients of the British Council's International School Award (ISA), which acknowledges schools that integrate global citizenship into their curriculum:
*   **Daffodil Boarding School (Kapan)** received the British Council ISA Full Award for the documented 2019–20 cycle [1](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf) [2](https://www.spotlightnepal.com/2020/10/21/23-nepali-schools-receive-british-councils-international-school-award-2020/).
*   **Daffodil Public School (Nayapati)** received the British Council ISA Full Award for the documented 2020–21 cycle [3](https://edusanjal.com/news/international-school-award-2022-british-council-nepal/).

## Leadership

The institution was founded by **Uttam Kumar Sherpa**, who serves as the Founder Director [5](https://daffodil.edu.np/). The management team oversees both campuses, with **Ashok Sundar Shrestha** serving as the Principal of the Kapan campus and **Maya Lama** as the Principal of the Nayapati campus [5](https://daffodil.edu.np/). The leadership emphasizes a tech-focused approach, utilizing digital platforms for student assessment and communication with parents [5](https://daffodil.edu.np/).

## Educational programmes

The school offers a comprehensive curriculum from Nursery to Secondary levels [5](https://daffodil.edu.np/). In addition to the standard Nepalese national curriculum, the school provides instruction in several international languages, including **French, Mandarin, and Spanish** [5](https://daffodil.edu.np/). The academic environment is designed to be progressive, incorporating digital tools and global educational practices validated by the British Council [5](https://daffodil.edu.np/).

## Community activity

Under its "Green School" initiative, the institution promotes environmental consciousness among its students and the local community [5](https://daffodil.edu.np/). The school enforces a strict **"No Junk and Plastic Free Society"** policy [5](https://daffodil.edu.np/). This initiative is supported by the physical infrastructure of the Nayapati campus, which is designed to integrate environmental management into the daily student experience [5](https://daffodil.edu.np/).

## Sports

The Daffodil Public School campus in Nayapati is spread over **14 ropanies** of land, providing significant space for physical education and extracurricular activities [5](https://daffodil.edu.np/). The campus includes a dedicated sports complex and specialized laboratories to support a holistic educational model [5](https://daffodil.edu.np/).
