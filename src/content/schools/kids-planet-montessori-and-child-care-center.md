## Overview

Kids Planet Montessori and Child Care Center is a private educational institution located in [Satungal, Chandragiri-7, Kathmandu, Nepal](https://edusanjal.com/school/kids-planet-montessori-and-child-care-centre-chandragiri/). The school provides specialized early childhood and primary education, serving students from the [Early Childhood Development (ECD) level through Grade 3](https://edusanjal.com/school/kids-planet-montessori-and-child-care-centre-chandragiri/). As of the 2081 BS academic year, the institution reported a total enrollment of [106 students](https://edusanjal.com/school/kids-planet-montessori-and-child-care-centre-chandragiri/), with 67 students enrolled at the ECD level. The school is noted for its integration of the Montessori method and its provision of [daycare and full-day care services](https://edusanjal.com/school/kids-planet-montessori-and-child-care-centre-chandragiri/) to support working parents.

## Recognition

The school achieved global recognition during the 2019–20 International School Award (ISA) cycle, where it was conferred the [ISA Full Award](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/programmes/education/connecting-classrooms/international-school-award) by the [British Council Nepal](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf). This award signifies the successful embedding of international dimensions into the school's curriculum. During the virtual award ceremony held on October 20, 2020, the institution was further honored in the special category for the ["Most Innovative Project,"](https://www.britishcouncil.org.np/sites/default/files/press_release_isa-20_october_2020.pdf) a distinction shared with only one other school in the same batch as reported by [Spotlight Nepal](https://www.spotlightnepal.com/2020/10/21/23-nepali-schools-receive-british-councils-international-school-award-2020/).

## Leadership

The institution is led by [Minju Shobha Chitrakar](https://www.facebook.com/minjuz/), who has served as the Founder and Principal since at least October 2018. Records also identify [Laxmi Prasad Pant](https://www.facebook.com/minjuz/) as a co-founder of the center. The leadership has focused on fostering project-based learning and maintaining an active presence within the [Chandragiri Municipality](https://www.facebook.com/minjuz/).

## Educational programmes

The curriculum at Kids Planet Montessori integrates practical life skills, sensorial education, and language development as detailed by [Edusanjal](https://edusanjal.com/school/kids-planet-montessori-and-child-care-centre-chandragiri/). The school has expanded its pedagogical approach through collaborations with external organizations, such as [Karkhana Asia](https://www.facebook.com/karkhana.asia/posts/1108528647976250/), to conduct Social Emotional Learning (SEL) workshops during the 2023/24 period. Additionally, the school offers [after-school enrichment programs](https://edusanjal.com/school/kids-planet-montessori-and-child-care-centre-chandragiri/) in arts, music, and mental math.

## Community activity

The school actively participates in local cultural and community events. Institutional records document regular student and staff involvement in traditional festivals, including [Ropai Jatra and Sonam Lhosar](https://edusanjal.com/school/kids-planet-montessori-and-child-care-centre-chandragiri/), as part of its effort to integrate Nepali cultural heritage into the educational experience.
